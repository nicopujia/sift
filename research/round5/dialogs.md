## F503 (high): Booking.com: clicking a date in Sift's calendar closes it without choosing the date

Found by: T4-generic. Where: https://www.booking.com/city/ar/mendoza.html (reached by typing 'Mendoza, Argentina' in Sift's search field, picking the first suggestion and pressing Search)

Evidence: The page opens with Booking's date picker open. Sift shows it as a list of 66 day checkboxes ('Thursday, October 1, 2026' …). Clicking the label 'Friday, November 20, 2026' in Sift closed the whole calendar, and the original still read 'Check-in date / Check-out date'. This happened 2 of 2 times, including after reopening the picker with 'Select dates'. With Sift switched off, a real click on the same cell (span role=checkbox data-date=2026-11-20) sets 'Fri, Nov 20' and keeps the calendar open for check-out. So a hotel search with dates cannot be set up from Sift. On this page Sift's header submit button is also labelled 'Calendar', the name of the calendar tab, instead of 'Search'. Shots: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-4/research/shots/r5t4-booking-results.png, /home/nico/projects/sift-test-4/research/shots/r5t4-booking-date.png, /home/nico/projects/sift-test-4/research/shots/r5t4-booking-city-orig.png

Repro: SIFT_PORT=9464 SIFT_TAB=T4-generic bun scripts/act.ts goto:https://www.booking.com/city/ar/mendoza.html wait:12000 (if the calendar is closed: 'click:Select dates' wait:2000) 'click:Friday, November 20, 2026' wait:2000 "eval:[...document.querySelectorAll('[data-testid=date-display-field-start],[data-testid=date-display-field-end]')].map(e=>e.textContent).join(' / ')" gives 'Check-in date / Check-out date'. Compare with the original: press Alt+Shift+S, click [data-testid=date-display-field-start], then click [data-date="2026-11-20"]; the field reads 'Fri, Nov 20'.

Suspected cause: The proxied click on the role=checkbox span inside a td role=gridcell probably does not reach the cell the way a real click does, or a focus or pointer event on the way closes the popup first. The replay in proxy.ts needs checking against this calendar.

Skeptic: Reproduced 2 of 2. The page opened with the date picker shown in Sift as 66 day checkboxes. Clicking the label 'Friday, November 20, 2026' in Sift closed the whole calendar (0 checkboxes after, [data-date=2026-11-20] gone). The original still read 'Check-in date / Check-out date'. Reopening with 'Select dates' and clicking the same date gave the same result. With Sift off, a real click on the start field, then on [data-date="2026-11-20"], set 'Fri, Nov 20 / Check-out date' and kept the calendar open. The header submit button reads 'Calendar' in the screenshot.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/content/proxy.ts, check the click replay that setChecked()/click() sends to a span[role=checkbox] inside td[role=gridcell]. Something in that sequence (a focus or blur on the way, or a pointerdown that the popup reads as an outside click) closes the picker before the click lands. Send the same pointer and mouse sequence that a real click sends to the cell, without first blurring Sift's field into the page. Separately, the header submit button's label should come from the form's own submit button, not from the calendar tab's name.

## F505 (medium): Closing a menu dialog opens a second, stray dialog (Craigslist sort, HN Search dropdowns, Allbirds gallery)

Found by: T4-generic. Where: https://sfbay.craigslist.org/search/sss?query=bike (relevance), https://hn.algolia.com/?q=sqlite (Popularity / All time), https://www.allbirds.com/products/mens-tree-runners (Open image)

Evidence: Craigslist: open 'relevance', then click Close or press Escape. A new dialog appears that lists the toolbar ('list | thumb | gallery | map | relevance | price | condition | sold by | 1 - 6 of 10,000+'), and it needs a second Close. This happened 2 of 2 times. HN Search: open 'Popularity' or 'All time', then click Close or press Enter on it. The dialog stays and is redrawn with a heading and every option twice ('Popularity | Close | Popularity | Popularity | Date | Date'). A second close closes it, but focus lands in the page, not on the trigger. This happened 3 of 3 times. Allbirds: Escape on the photo gallery replaced it with a dialog holding the site's AI chips ('Does it run true to size?', 'Explore this product. Shopping Agent'). These chips sit at y=1050 with opacity-0 in the original. This happened 3 of 4 tries, and closing with the Close button did not do it. Shots: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-4/research/shots/r5t4-cl-close-stray.png, /home/nico/projects/sift-test-4/research/shots/r5t4-hnsearch-state.png, /home/nico/projects/sift-test-4/research/shots/r5t4-allbirds-afteresc.png

Repro: SIFT_PORT=9464 SIFT_TAB=T4-generic bun scripts/act.ts 'goto:https://sfbay.craigslist.org/search/sss?query=bike' wait:9000 click:relevance wait:2000 press:Escape wait:2000 "eval:document.querySelector('sift-root').shadowRoot.querySelector('[role=dialog]')?.innerText.replace(/\s+/g,' ')" gives 'Close list thumb gallery map relevance price condition sold by 1 - 6 of 10,000+'. HN: 'goto:https://hn.algolia.com/?q=sqlite' wait:8000 click:Popularity wait:2000 'click:[role=dialog] button' wait:2500 then the same eval gives 'Popularity Close Popularity Popularity Date Date'.

Suspected cause: After the close, the dialog/popup detection in generic.ts treats the nodes the menu's closing just changed (the toolbar, the re-rendered dropdown, a rotating chip) as a newly opened dialog.

Skeptic: Partly reproduced, and the problem is real on 2 of the 3 sites. Craigslist, 2 of 2: after 'relevance', Escape or Close replaced the sort menu with a dialog 'Close list thumb gallery map relevance price condition sold by 1 - 6 of 10,000+', and a second Close was needed. The original shows no overlay. Its .cl-search-toolbar is a fixed bar that is always there. HN Search, 2 of 2: after Popularity then Close, the dialog was redrawn as 'Popularity Close Popularity Popularity Date Date'. Here the cause is different from the claim: the original dropdown is still open (Dropdown_list--open, visible in the original screenshot), so Sift's Close never closed the site's menu. A second Close removed the dialog, and focus ended on a link in Sift ('Popularity Popularity Date'). Allbirds did not reproduce: Escape on the image gallery gave no new dialog on 3 of 3 tries.

Skeptic's fix lead: Two parts in src/content/extract/generic.ts and index.ts. First, in popupLike(), do not treat a fixed bar that was already present and shown in the page view (its controls are in ctx.visited) as a newly opened popup just because nodes inside it changed. Second, when Sift's Close or Escape dismisses a layer or menu that has no close button, actually close it in the original: proxy a click outside it, or a click on its expanded trigger. Then a dismissed menu that stays open is not found again under a new key.

## F522 (medium): Sift's Close on a page modal that has no close of its own (consent wall, age gate) only hides Sift's copy: the consent-gated article stays 'Loading…' with no way back to Accept/Reject

Found by: T6-fixtures. Where: r4-cookiewall.html?gate=1, core-consentwall-norole.html, r4-latemodal.html?kind=age. Code: H.closeDialog in src/content/index.ts:2284 (marks the key dismissed, then sends Escape, which these pages ignore)

Evidence: The consent sheet opens with focus on Close. Enter (or Escape) right away leaves Sift with 'Rye is back… | Loading…'. Page log [], and the page's #wall (role=dialog aria-modal) is still in the page. Waiting 6 s, or switching Sift off and on (sift:mode original, then null with body inert), does not bring Accept/Reject back. Only the original view or a reload does. core-consentwall-norole.html behaves the same: Enter on Close leaves 'Spelt is the new rye | Loading…'. Age gate: Close hides the sheet while the original still blocks the page (#ov present, no 'ok' or 'x' in the log), so Sift shows content the original gates. Shot /home/nico/projects/sift-test-6/research/shots/r5t6-cookiewall-closed-deadend.png

Repro: SIFT_PORT=9466 SIFT_TAB=T6-fixtures bun scripts/act.ts "goto:http://127.0.0.1:8799/fx/r4-cookiewall.html?gate=1" wait:4000 press:Enter wait:1000 press:Alt+Shift+S wait:800 press:Alt+Shift+S wait:2000 outline (shows only 'Loading…')

Suspected cause: Dismissal is stored no matter whether the page closed its dialog. For a consent or age gate that ignores Escape, the page stays blocked while Sift forgets the only controls that unblock it.

Skeptic: Reproduced twice. On r4-cookiewall.html?gate=1, the sheet opens with focus on Sift's Close. After Enter, Sift shows only 'Rye is back… | Loading…'. The page log is [] and #wall is still present. After 5 s more, and after Alt+Shift+S off and on, Accept/Reject never come back. core-consentwall-norole.html is the same ('Spelt is the new rye | Loading…', #cmp-consent still present). Code: H.closeDialog (index.ts:2284) adds the key to store.dismissed before it knows whether the page closed anything, then sends Escape, which these walls ignore. The only way out is the switch to the original. I did not weigh the age-gate side note, because that page's content is not blocked. Medium.

Skeptic's fix lead: In index.ts H.closeDialog, after sending Escape, check about 300 ms later whether the dialog element is still connected and visible. If it is, undo store.dismissed and dismissedRefs for that key and show the sheet again (or say that the page needs a choice). Also, do not offer Close at all for a modal that has no close control of its own and blocks the content.

## F523 (medium): Page alertdialogs lose their role and description: a screen reader hears 'dialog, Close' without the warning or the consequence

Found by: T6-fixtures. Where: r4-latemodal.html?kind=session (role=alertdialog, aria-describedby) and r5-table.html Delete confirm (role=alertdialog, aria-describedby)

Evidence: Sift's sheet for the session timeout is <div class="sheet" role="dialog" aria-modal="true" aria-label="Are you still there?"> with no aria-describedby. Focus goes to Sift's own Close, so 'you will be signed out in 60 seconds' is not read when the dialog opens. The Delete confirm is the same: role=dialog, label 'Delete order #1003?', desc=null, so 'This removes the order and its invoice' is not read. The pages had focused their own buttons ('Stay signed in', 'Cancel'). Enter on Sift's Close sends Escape (session log 'esc') instead of the page's default 'ok'. Live region empty in both. Shot /home/nico/projects/sift-test-6/research/shots/r5t6-table-confirm.png

Repro: SIFT_PORT=9466 SIFT_TAB=T6-fixtures bun scripts/act.ts "goto:http://127.0.0.1:8799/fx/r4-latemodal.html?kind=session" wait:4500 "eval:document.querySelector('sift-root').shadowRoot.querySelector('.layer [role=dialog]').outerHTML.slice(0,200)"

Suspected cause: Dialog sheets are always rendered as role=dialog named by the title. The page dialog's alertdialog role, its aria-describedby text and its initial focus target are not carried over.

Skeptic: Reproduced. For r4-latemodal.html?kind=session (page role=alertdialog, aria-describedby=d), Sift renders <div class="sheet" role="dialog" aria-modal="true" aria-label="Are you still there?"> with no aria-describedby, and focus goes to Sift's Close. The page had focused 'Stay signed in'. The live region is empty. The r5-table Delete confirm is the same: role=dialog, label 'Delete order #1003?', desc=null, focus on Close. So 'you will be signed out in 60 seconds' and 'This removes the order and its invoice' are not announced when the dialog opens. Medium accessibility issue.

Skeptic's fix lead: Carry the page dialog's role (alertdialog), its aria-describedby text and its initially focused control into the dialog model in the generic.ts popup/dialog extraction. In app.tsx, render the sheet with role=alertdialog and aria-describedby pointing at the description, and move initial focus to the matching button instead of Close.

Found by: T6-fixtures. Where: r5-table.html: Delete, then confirm 'Delete order', then the page shows a non-modal role=status toast 'Order #1003 deleted. Undo' for 6 s

Evidence: Sift shows it as role=dialog aria-modal 'Order #1003 deleted.' with Close and Undo. Focus moves to Close and main.inert=true at +2 s and +4 s. Suspected cause: A fixed role=status element with a button that appears after an action goes through the popup/dialog path instead of the toast/notice path.

## F529 (high): Checking a box or choosing a select option inside a role-less popup removes the popup from Sift; Save becomes unreachable

Found by: R2-interaction. Where: src/content/index.ts:1990-1993 (afterAct calls armRecent(), which empties the recent nodes), called from H.check (index.ts:2214-2222) and H.select (index.ts:2223-2227); src/content/extract/generic.ts:4690 and 4761 (lastPop keeps a popup only when it has a text field, FILL_SEL)

Evidence: New fixture fx/r5int-popcheck.html: a 'People' button opens an absolutely placed div with no role, holding checkboxes Ana and Luis, an Access select, and Save and Cancel. Sift shows it as a MODAL sheet (Close|Ana|Luis|Can view|Save|Cancel). One click on Ana's checkbox in Sift checks it on the page (c1.checked=true), and the page popup stays open. But at 200 ms, 800 ms, 2 s and 4 s Sift shows no sheet, and focus is back on 'People'. Choosing 'Can edit' in the sheet's select with ArrowDown does the same: the page value is 'Can edit', the popup is open, and the Sift sheet is gone. The user cannot reach Luis, Access or Save in Sift. This is the F387 failure through another handler. A native checkbox or select change makes no DOM mutation, so nothing refills the recent list.

Repro: From the scratchpad dir: SIFT_PORT=9467 SIFT_TAB=R2-interaction bun r5int/popcheck.ts (checkbox) and bun r5int/popselect.ts (select). By hand: open http://127.0.0.1:8799/fx/r5int-popcheck.html, click People in Sift, then click Ana's checkbox.

Suspected cause: H.check and H.select end with afterAct(), and afterAct() calls armRecent() without keep, so nodes become []. findPopup only finds role-less popups through recentNodes(), and its lastPop fallback needs a text field (FILL_SEL). Fix: when the checked or selected element is inside the shown dialog or popup, arm with keep (armRecent(2500, true)), or let afterAct take a keep flag. Or add checkbox, radio and select to the lastPop condition.

Skeptic: Reproduced twice for the checkbox and once for the select. On r5int-popcheck.html, People opens a Sift .layer sheet (Close | Ana | Luis | Access select | Save | Cancel). A trusted click on Ana's checkbox in Sift sets c1.checked=true, and the page's .pop stays open. At 200, 600, 1200 and 2000 ms Sift shows no sheet, focus is on 'People', and the outline has no Ana, Luis, Access or Save. Focusing the sheet's select and pressing ArrowDown sets the page value to 'Can edit' with the popup still open, and the Sift sheet is gone the same way. Recovery fails too: pressing People again in Sift, then waiting 4.5 s, still shows no sheet, because the page's handler returns early when .pop exists. The only way to reach Save is to switch to the original. The code matches the claim. H.check and H.select end with afterAct(), which calls armRecent() without keep, so recent nodes become []. The lastPop fallback (generic.ts:4690-4691) keeps a popup only when it holds a FILL_SEL text field.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/content/index.ts H.check and H.select, when the element is inside the shown dialog or popup, arm with armRecent(2500, true) instead of the plain afterAct() arm (or add a keep flag to afterAct). Also, or instead, widen the lastPop condition in src/content/extract/generic.ts (lines 4690-4691) to keep a popup that holds a checkbox, radio or select, not only a text field.

## F530 (high, regression): At 200% zoom or phone width, notices (validation errors, toasts) render in the top bar that has scrolled away, so nothing visible happens

Found by: R2-interaction. Where: src/content/ui/app.tsx:921-932 (fitBar sets data-flat when the bar is taller than 1/8 of the window), src/content/ui/styles.ts:39 (.bar[data-flat] { position: static }), app.tsx:965 (store.notice is rendered inside the header)

Evidence: New fixture fx/r5int-zoomnotice.html, with the form at the end of the page. At a 640x450 viewport (1280x900 at 200% zoom), the bar is data-flat=true. Clicking Sift's 'Send message' with the required email empty creates the notice 'Your email: Please fill out this field.' at top -617 px, bottom -553 px, so it is out of view with Sift scrollTop 681. Focus stays on Send, and the page log is []. At 1280x900 the same notice is at top 64 px, in view. A sighted low-vision user at 200% zoom presses Send and sees nothing. The original shows Chrome's bubble at the field. Toasts that go through notify() ('Added to cart') are hidden the same way.

Repro: From the scratchpad dir: SIFT_PORT=9467 SIFT_TAB=R2-interaction bun r5int/zoom.ts 640x450, then bun r5int/zoom.ts 1280x900 as the control.

Suspected cause: The round-4 change that lets a tall bar scroll away kept the notice inside it ('In the sticky bar, so it shows wherever the user is'). Fix: when the bar is flat, give .bar-notice its own position: sticky/fixed at the top, or keep the bar sticky while a notice shows. Or move focus to the invalid field, or scroll the notice into view.

Skeptic: Reproduced at 640x450 (twice, once with deviceScaleFactor 2) and at phone width 390x844. In both, the bar has data-flat=true. After clicking Sift's 'Send message' with the email empty, the notice 'Your email: Please fill out this field.' sits at top -617 / bottom -553 (-493 / -430 on the phone), out of view. Focus stays on Send, and the page log is []. A screenshot shows only the form with no message. Control at 1280x900: the bar is not flat and the notice is at top 64, in view. On the original page at 640x450 with Sift off (Enter on its Send button), the browser fires 'invalid' on #em and moves focus to the email field, which is in view (top 421). So the original gives visible feedback and Sift gives none. A screen reader still hears the text through the live region, but a sighted user at 200% zoom or on a phone sees nothing happen. I did not test the toast path separately; it renders through the same store.notice element in the header.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/content/ui/styles.ts, make .bar[data-flat] .bar-notice position: sticky or fixed at top: 0 so it stays in view when the bar is static. Or, in src/content/ui/app.tsx, render the notice outside the header when the bar is flat. For validation notices, also focus the invalid field in Sift, as Chrome does on the original page.
