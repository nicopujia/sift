## F511 (high, regression): Google Maps home search opens a 'This area' modal with no suggestions, and normal-speed typing scrambles the query and opens a wrong place

Found by: T3-tools. Where: https://www.google.com/maps (top search field)

Evidence: A real click on Sift's search field opens a modal .sheet named 'This area'. It holds a copy of the search field, Search, Directions, 'This area', '16°' and 'No traffic information to display', and focus moves into its field. The first keys land in the top-bar field, and the rest are inserted at position 0 of the copy. Typing 'Ferry Building' right after the click at 100 ms/key gave 'rry BuildingFe', and at 180 ms/key gave 'erry BuildingF' (page input had the same value). With act.ts typing, the value was ' BuilFerr', and ArrowDown+Enter opened /maps/place/BUILD+LLC in Naples, Florida. With slow typing after a 1.5 s pause, the original grid had 3 rows (Ferry Building San Francisco…), but the Sift sheet still showed no suggestions. The original (Sift off) shows 3 suggestions under the box. Round 4 showed suggestions here (/home/nico/projects/sift-test-3/research/shots/T3-gmaps-suggest.png). Shots: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-3/research/shots/R5-T3-gmaps-home-typing.png, R5-T3-gmaps-home-orig-typing.png

Repro: cd /home/nico/projects/sift-test-3; SIFT_PORT=9463 SIFT_TAB=T3-tools bun scripts/act.ts goto:https://www.google.com/maps wait:10000 "type:input[type=search]=>Ferry Buil" wait:3000 "eval:document.querySelector('input[name=q]').value" "eval:[...document.querySelector('sift-root').shadowRoot.querySelectorAll('.sheet')].map(s=>s.getAttribute('aria-label')+': '+s.innerText.slice(0,120))" press:ArrowDown press:Enter wait:8000 url  (expect value ' BuilFerr' or similar, sheet 'This area' with no suggestions, URL /maps/place/BUILD+LLC). Human-speed variant: bun /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/r5t3/clicktype2.ts "input[type=search]" "Ferry Building" 100

Suspected cause: Same family as F323: focusing Google's search box makes findDialog/popupLike pick the side-panel container ('This area' card plus search box) as a modal dialog, instead of the field's aria-controls grid. Moving focus into the copy's field mid-typing drops the caret at 0.

Skeptic: Reproduced. On 4 of 4 fresh loads of /maps, a real click on Sift's search field opens a modal sheet named 'This area' at once (Close, the copied field, Search, Directions, 'This area', 17°, 'No traffic information to display'). It never shows suggestions. Typing at human speed right after the click scrambled the query in 2 of 2 runs: 'rry BuildingFe' at 100 ms per key and 'erry BuildingF' at 180 ms. The page input held the same wrong value. Meanwhile the page's own grid had 3 rows (Ferry Building San Francisco, Auckland, Gate B). The act.ts 20 ms variant did not scramble for me: the page value was 'Ferry Buil'. ArrowDown+Enter then searched the raw 'Ferry Buil' (/maps/search/Ferry+Buil) and did not pick a suggestion. I did not see BUILD LLC. Original with Sift off, 100 ms per key: 'Ferry Building', 3 suggestions under the box, no extra panel. After toggling Sift off and back on in the same tab, the click opened no sheet and 'F' gave a normal 'Suggestions' sheet. So the bug shows on fresh loads. Shots: research/shots/skep-N0-a.png, skep-N0-b.png, skep-N0-orig.png.

Skeptic's fix lead: generic.ts findPopup/popupLike: the 'This area' card plus box container is accepted as a popup because focusing the field touches it. It is not a [role=dialog] and not a fixed layer, so it comes through findPopup. Reject any root that contains or sits beside the focused page field (document.activeElement, or ctx.searchEl's combobox box). Treat the field's aria-controls grid (ucc-0) as its suggestions. Then focus never leaves Sift's top-bar field while typing.

## F513 (high): Wikipedia header search: keys typed right after clicking the field are lost on the page, so Enter opens the wrong article

Found by: T3-tools. Where: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bagel and /wiki/Bread (header 'Search Wikipedia' field)

Evidence: Sift's field shows the full text, but the page's input[name=search] holds only the first few letters, and no suggestion sheet appears. Real click then typing at 120 ms/key with no pause: page 'so', 'sourdough', 's' (2 of 3 lost). With act.ts typing: Bread 'sou', Bagel 'sourd', Rye_bread fine. Bread at 150 ms/key: 1 of 8 runs lost ('s'). In one run the page held 'sour' and Enter went to https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sourness while Sift's field read 'sourdough'. In another, Enter did nothing (URL stayed /wiki/Bagel). Control with Sift off: typing at 20 ms/key gives 'sourdough bread' and 2 suggestions, and Enter opens /wiki/Sourdough. When typed slowly after a pause, Sift shows suggestions ('Sourdough Type of fermented bread…'). Shot: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-3/research/shots/R5-T3-wiki-search-lost2.png

Repro: cd /home/nico/projects/sift-test-3; SIFT_PORT=9463 SIFT_TAB=T3-tools bun scripts/act.ts goto:https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bagel wait:12000 "type:header input=>sourdough bread" wait:1500 "eval:[...document.querySelectorAll('input[name=search]')].map(i=>i.value)+' sift='+document.querySelector('sift-root').shadowRoot.querySelector('header input').value" press:Enter wait:6000 url  (or CW=0 bun /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/r5t3/trace2.ts "header input" "sourdough" 120)

Suspected cause: Vector's search box is replaced by the Codex typeahead (a new input) once it loads on first focus. Sift keeps replaying keys into the old input, so the page field stops getting keys and Sift's field is never resynced.

Skeptic: Reproduced, flaky: 4 of 7 runs lost keys. act.ts on /wiki/Bagel: page 'sourdou', Sift 'sourdough bread', no sheet, and Enter did nothing (URL stayed /wiki/Bagel). trace2 at 120 ms per key: Bread gave page 'so' while Sift said 'sourdough', and Enter did nothing. Bagel gave page 'sou', and Enter opened https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sou. Three more Bagel runs gave 'sourdough' (with a 'Search results' sheet), 'so' (no sheet), and 'sourdough'. I tagged the original input before typing. Afterward it was disconnected, held 's', and a new input had replaced it. So the page swaps the field on first focus. Original with Sift off, 120 ms per key: 'sourdough', and Enter opened /wiki/Sourdough.

Skeptic's fix lead: index.ts H.input: `const el = elOf(ref); if (!el) return;` drops every key once the page replaces the field, because elOf returns null for a disconnected node. On a miss, re-extract right away and find the replacement (same form#searchform and name=search). Then write Sift's full field value into it with proxy.setValue. Or resync after each render: if Sift's field value differs from the page field's value, push Sift's value.

Shots: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-3/research/shots/R5-T3-gmaps-results-modal2.png, R5-T3-gmaps-trap3.png, R5-T3-gmaps-sugg-esc.png

Suspected cause: When Google's search box loses focus, its container changes state (the 'Cancel search nearby' chip shows), and findDialog/popupLike accept that container as a dialog, the same family as F323.

Skeptic's fix lead: Same root as N0, in generic.ts findPopup/popupLike: blurring Google's box changes its container, and the container (it holds the search input) is accepted as a popup. Reject roots that contain ctx.searchEl or the field Sift just left. Also make Escape close the Suggestions sheet.

## F517 (medium): Amazon search: 'Hello, sign in' in Sift does nothing (it replays on the wrapper div, not the link)

Found by: T5-amazon-gmail-a11y. Where: src/content/adapters/amazon.ts top(): q('#nav-link-accountList'); amazon-search snapshot

Evidence: On the amazon-search snapshot (a real Amazon save), #nav-link-accountList is a <div class="nav-div"> that holds <a href="https://www.amazon.com/ap/signin?...">. Sift renders it as <button>Hello, sign in</button> mapped to the div. A capture listener on the original logged one click on 'div#nav-link-accountList', not prevented. The URL stayed http://127.0.0.1:8799/amazon-search, my request guard saw no document request, and no dialog opened. On the original, a click lands on the inner link and goes to the sign-in page. The product fixture has an <a id=nav-link-accountList> instead, and there it is a working link. Caveat: the snapshot has no page scripts. On live Amazon, its JavaScript might open the account flyout on the hover Sift sends, but that is still not the original's click effect. I did not test live Amazon (forbidden).

Repro: Block requests to hosts containing 'amazon' or 'ssl-images' (CDP Fetch guard). Load http://127.0.0.1:8799/amazon-search, press Escape to close the Change language sheet (it shows only because Amazon's CSS is blocked), then: bun scripts/act.ts "eval:(()=>{window.__clk=[];document.addEventListener('click',e=>window.__clk.push(e.target.localName+'#'+e.target.id),true)})()" "click:Hello, sign in" wait:1500 "eval:JSON.stringify(window.__clk)" url  (prints [.."div#nav-link-accountList"] and the unchanged URL; no request to /ap/signin).

Suspected cause: top() passes the #nav-link-accountList element to toAction() whatever its tag. When it is a div, the action should use the link inside it: q('#nav-link-accountList a[href], a#nav-link-accountList').

Skeptic: Reproduced on the amazon-search snapshot. I loaded it with every request to an Amazon host aborted and pressed Escape to close the language sheet. Sift's top bar shows 'button:Hello, sign in', with no href. In the snapshot's markup, #nav-link-accountList is <div class="nav-div">. It wraps <a href="https://www.amazon.com/ap/signin?..."> and a flyout <button>. A trusted mouse click on Sift's button logged one untrusted click on 'div#nav-link-accountList' (not prevented). The URL stayed http://127.0.0.1:8799/amazon-search. No document request was made or blocked, no dialog opened, and no sheet opened. Comparison with Sift switched off: a trusted click on the original 'Hello, sign in' text hit span#nav-link-accountList-nav-line-1 inside the link. It started a document request to https://www.amazon.com/ap/signin?..., which my guard blocked. So the original goes to sign-in and Sift does nothing. The cause is in the code. amazon.ts top() passes q('#nav-link-accountList') to toAction() whatever its tag. A div gets no href, and proxy.click() sends a div a synthetic mouse 'click', which never activates a link nested inside it. Caveat: the snapshot has no scripts, and live Amazon was not tested (forbidden). Amazon's own JavaScript is unlikely to turn a click on the wrapper div into the link's navigation, though. Medium severity: the account and sign-in entry point does not have the same effect as on the original.

Skeptic's fix lead: src/content/adapters/amazon.ts top(): take the link inside the wrapper first, for example const acct = q('#nav-link-accountList a[href], a#nav-link-accountList') ?? q('#nav-link-accountList'). Keep the label from #nav-link-accountList-nav-line-1.
