# Round 5 findings: yt

Each was reproduced by a skeptic. F5xx are new in round 5; F3xx and F<100 are older findings a round 5 recheck found not fully fixed. The suspected cause and fix are leads, not orders.

## F508 (high): While a watch page loads, the original YouTube page shows through Sift for 1-3 s

Found by: T1-youtube. Where: YouTube watch page fresh load (watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ), about 3-6.5 s after navigation, during the interim generic layout

Evidence: I took 4 screenshots in this window, in 4 different loads: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1/research/shots/R5T1-watchload-jump.png (3.4 s), /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1/research/shots/R5T1-interim.png, /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1/research/shots/R5T1-interimB-0.png and /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1/research/shots/R5T1-burst2-run1-04-6351.png. Each shows the original page's own parts under and over Sift's view: its title at x=16, the channel row with its Subscribe, the gray description box ('1.8B views 16y ago #RickAstley...'), the Like pill, and the related-video column covering the right edge of the player. All of these overlap Sift's H1 and media bar. The left margin, which is pure white in a clean frame, has 7020-8168 dark pixels in these shots and 0 in the clean ones. Shots taken outside the window (1.5-3 s, and after the adapter view returns) are clean. All four bleed frames fall inside the generic-layout window from F309: the poll shows main starting with an h1 (H1 top 96, player top 180) for 1.1-3.5 s in 6 of 6 loads. In the DOM nothing looks wrong at those moments: sift-root and .sift have a white background and opacity 1, and the open popovers are [sift-root, div#movie_player]. So I can only show the bleed in screenshots, not in the DOM. Round 4's screenshot of its generic interim view (/home/nico/projects/sift-test-1/research/shots/R4T1-watchload-1.png) had no bleed.

Repro: cd /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1; SIFT_PORT=9461 SIFT_TAB=T1-youtube bun /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/poll.ts 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ' 9000 R5T1-interimX. It prints Sift's state every 100 ms, and when the H1-above-player state starts it saves 3 screenshots, research/shots/R5T1-interimX-{0,1,2}.png. Or run scratchpad/burst.ts with the URL, a name, 3000 and 6500 to take screenshots every ~400 ms. Then look for the original's left-column title and description box at x<250 in the shots. This happened in about half of my loads.

Suspected cause: About 3-5 s into the load the adapter goes back to the generic view, perhaps when watch() returns null or a partial page while ytd-watch-flexy settles. The switch re-renders the page, maybe re-showing the player popover, and in those frames the original page is painted over Sift's white background. Look at what changes in index.ts/portal.ts render or top-layer order between the adapter and generic views.

Skeptic: Reproduced in 3 of 3 fresh loads of watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ. I recorded each load as a PNG screencast and counted dark pixels in Sift's empty margins (x<250 and x 1030-1140). Each load had a run of bleed frames: 5346-5676 ms, 8937-9288 ms and 8452-8891 ms, so about 0.35-0.85 s per load, not 1-3 s. The bleed shows the original's title, channel, Subscribe, description box and Like pill, plus the related column. All of it is painted over Sift, including over the right edge of Sift's own player. One frame is a ghosted cross-fade between Sift's two layouts. Frames: /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/skv/c1/f-05346.png, c1/f-05625.png, c2/f-09153.png. The cause is not Sift's re-render. YouTube itself calls document.startViewTransition() during the watch load, which I confirmed with a main-world hook: it starts at 3.3-6.2 s, is ready about 1.2-1.8 s later and finishes about 0.4-0.9 s after that. It does this with Sift off too. The page sets view-transition-name on html (root), #player-container (player-resize), #below (metadata) and #secondary (secondary-column). The ::view-transition pseudo-tree paints above the top layer, so during the animation those named original parts are drawn over sift-root. That is why the DOM looked clean: white background, opacity 1, popovers [sift-root, div#movie_player]. The original flashes over Sift on every watch load, against the 'original never flashes' design, so high severity stands.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/main/index.ts (main world), wrap Document.prototype.startViewTransition. While Sift is shown, call skipTransition() on the transition it returns; the update callback still runs. Sift-shown can be read from an attribute that index.ts sets on <html> in sift mode. Another way: while in sift mode, add a page stylesheet that sets view-transition-name: none !important on the page's elements. Either way this covers every site that uses same-document view transitions, not just YouTube.

## F509 (medium): Fresh watch load: keyboard focus starts in YouTube's player, and YouTube takes it back about 6 s in

Found by: T1-youtube. Where: YouTube watch page, keyboard only, from a fresh navigation (address bar or goto)

Evidence: After a fresh load, document.activeElement is div#movie_player. The first Tab goes into YouTube's own player controls inside the portal: 13 PAGE stops ('Close' card teaser, branding button, 'Seek slider', 'fine scrubbing thumbnails' 160x0, Pause, Mute, 'Volume' 0x40, time, Autoplay, Subtitles, Settings, Theater, Full screen). Only then does Tab reach Sift's own Pause. Sift's Skip to content, Search, Menu, Sign in and Settings can't be reached by tabbing forward. Keys meant to scroll go to the player: PageDown does not scroll Sift (portal top stays 95), ArrowDown lowers the volume 0.89->0.85->0.80, Space pauses, and End seeks to the end, which with autoplay moved the tab to another video (watch?v=-OfA04BD4GA) while I was trying to scroll comments. Focus also gets taken back: in 2 of 2 runs I tabbed to Sift's Mute at 2.5 s, and by 5.9 s focus was on PAGE div#movie_player. tabwalk.ts hides all of this because it clicks inside Sift first. Shot: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1/research/shots/R5T1-fresh-focus-in-player.png

Repro: cd /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1; SIFT_PORT=9461 SIFT_TAB=T1-youtube bun /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/tabs2.ts 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ' 25. It prints 'start: PAGE <div#movie_player>', then 13 PAGE stops before 'SIFT <button.btn> Pause'. For the focus being taken: SIFT_PORT=9461 SIFT_TAB=T1-youtube bun /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/early.ts 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ'. It shows 'SIFT <button> Mute' at ~2.5-3.8 s, then 'PAGE <div#movie_player>' from ~5.9 s.

Suspected cause: YouTube calls focus() on #movie_player when the watch page finishes loading. Sift neither moves the initial focus into its own view nor keeps focus that is already inside Sift. The portal keeps YouTube's controls tabbable even though Sift's media bar duplicates them.

Skeptic: Reproduced, but with a narrower scope than claimed. After a fresh load, focus starts at PAGE div#movie_player. Tab passes 13 YouTube player stops before reaching Sift's Pause. early.ts, run twice: focus was on Sift's Mute at 2.4 s, and PAGE #movie_player had it again from 5.3 s and 7.1 s. Much of this is YouTube's own behavior. With Sift off, focus also starts on #movie_player, and in 2 of 2 runs YouTube pulled focus back to #movie_player at about 6 s. On the original, ArrowDown (volume), Space (pause) and End (seek) also act on the focused player. The failure that is Sift's own is scrolling. With focus in the portal, PageDown does not scroll Sift: the portal top stays at 95 and the .sift scrollTop stays at 0. Instead it scrolls the hidden original (scrollY 0 -> 787 -> 1574). On the original, PageDown scrolls the page. So on a fresh watch load, a keyboard user cannot page through Sift's view until they tab out of 13 player controls, and YouTube's refocus at about 6 s puts them back in the player. I rate this medium, as a keyboard-accessibility gap on Sift's main YouTube page.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/content/index.ts, the keydown handler and onPortalKey: for scroll keys that the portal's page did not consume (PageDown and PageUp, and Space or arrows when the target is the player container itself rather than one of its controls), preventDefault and call scrollByKey() on Sift's host. Also treat focus that page script moves into a portal, with no recent pointer or key input there, as not the user's choice: keep focus on the last Sift element, or on main().

## F510 (medium): Mix links lose the Mix in their address: middle-click, Ctrl+click or a copied link opens the plain video

Found by: T1-youtube. Where: YouTube search results, Up next and home, entries that are Mixes (e.g. 'Mix - Rick Astley' on results?search_query=rick+astley)

Evidence: On the original, the Mix entry links to /watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ&list=RDEMt6MUSQI0t_Ts2EDUDWGRhQ&start_radio=1 (search). In Up next it is /watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ&list=RDdQw4w9WgXcQ&start_radio=1&rv=dQw4w9WgXcQ. Sift's links are https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ and https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ&rv=dQw4w9WgXcQ. A middle-click on Sift's 'Mix - Rick Astley' opened a new tab at https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ, the single video with no Mix panel. A plain click does open the Mix, because it is replayed on the original. Now that Sift shows Mix panels (F300), opening a Mix in a new tab or sharing its link silently drops the Mix.

Repro: cd /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1; SIFT_PORT=9461 SIFT_TAB=T1-youtube bun scripts/act.ts 'goto:https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=rick+astley' wait:8000 "eval:[...document.querySelector('sift-root').shadowRoot.querySelectorAll('main li a.title')].filter(a=>/^Mix - /.test(a.innerText)).map(a=>a.href)" "eval:[...document.querySelectorAll('ytd-search yt-lockup-view-model')].filter(l=>/^Mix - Rick Astley$/m.test(l.innerText)).map(l=>l.querySelector('a[href]').getAttribute('href'))". The first eval gives no list= in the URL; the second gives one with list=RD...&start_radio=1. For the new tab: tag the link and run /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/modclick.ts '#zz-mix' middle.

Suspected cause: src/content/adapters/youtube.ts cleanHref() deletes 'list' whenever 'start_radio' is present, so every Mix href becomes the bare video.

Skeptic: Reproduced on results?search_query=rick+astley. Sift's 'Mix - Rick Astley' and 'Mix - Rick Astley - Never Gonna Give You Up (Official Video)' links both point to https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ. The original's links are /watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ&list=RDEMt6MUSQI0t_Ts2EDUDWGRhQ&start_radio=1... and /watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ&list=RDdQw4w9WgXcQ&start_radio=1. A trusted middle-click on Sift's Mix link opened a new tab at watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ, the single video with no Mix. The cause is cleanHref() in src/content/adapters/youtube.ts (lines 22-25), which deletes list whenever start_radio is present. One nuance: in this profile the original also links ordinary video results as radios (list=RD<id>&start_radio=1). So stripping is reasonable for plain videos, and wrong only for entries that are themselves Mixes or playlists. Medium severity: opening a Mix in a new tab, or copying its link, silently drops the Mix.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/content/adapters/youtube.ts, keep list and start_radio for collection entries. In the lockup builder's `collection` branch (around lines 137-140), and for Mix rows in Up next and home, use a cleanHref variant that drops only pp. Plain video rows can keep the current stripping.

But in 6 of 6 polled loads of watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ (plain and Mix), Sift then switches at about 3.1-5.4 s to a generic layout for 1.1-3.5 s, then switches back at 4.2-8.9 s. In that layout main starts with an h1 (H1 top 96, player top 180), with no metadata line, no sections, and no Sign in or Settings. In one burst run, the old generic view (placeholder 'search query', H1 'Never Gonna Give You Up (Official Video) (4K Remaster)' without the prefix) was on screen at 6.35 s: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1/research/shots/R5T1-burst2-run1-04-6351.png. Once, right after the Spanish home visit, the Mix URL still showed the generic view at 12 s (raw Mix links, an 'Action menu' button, no Description/Up next/Comments). The adapter view came by 20 s. Poll script: /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/poll.ts. See the new finding on the original showing through.

Skeptic: Still partly fixed, as reported. Reproduced in 7 of 7 fresh loads (3 screencasts and 4 polls). The adapter layout shows first, at 1.2-2.4 s. Sift then switches to the generic view from 3.9-6.3 s, for 1.3-3.3 s, and then back to the adapter. The generic view has the H1 'Never Gonna Give You Up (Official Video) (4K Remaster)' without the prefix, at top 96, above the player at 180. The search placeholder is 'search query', the header has only Search and Menu, and there is no metadata or sections. Frame: /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/skv/c2/f-07278.png. The switch back is when the N0 bleed shows. Logged DOM state: in one load the generic view began at 6.3 s, while ytd-watch-flexy did not exist yet. It continued after YouTube inserted flexy inside its startViewTransition update (7.1 s), with ytd-watch-metadata present but #title h1 empty until about 9.8 s. This matches watch()'s two null returns: `!flexy && !settling()` and `!fresh && !errShown && !settling()`. In one load the switch came at 4.2 s, inside settling()'s 5 s window, so I have not pinned the exact null path for every load.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/content/adapters/youtube.ts watch(), on a first load keep returning the early page (playerBlock plus loadedTitle plus the loading line) while #movie_player exists and flexy or its title are not ready yet. Do not fall back to null once settling()'s 5 s window ends. For example, restart settling()'s clock when ytd-watch-flexy first appears, or let it not expire while the player for the URL's video id exists.
