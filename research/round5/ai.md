## F570 (high): A refresh that comes due while the first Short version is still being written is dropped for good: the Short version keeps the old number next to the new paragraph

Found by: R3-ai-background. Where: src/content/ai.ts:376-379 (the refresh timer deletes `pending`, then returns when `now.busy` or when !allowed('condense')); nothing re-arms it when the job ends (ai.ts:418-422), and processAi runs only when the extraction changes (index.ts:1506-1601)

Evidence: Chrome 9469 with the mock. tollslow.html is toll.html with a model that takes 20 s. The page changes 'death toll rose to 12' to '31' at 9 s. The refresh timer fires at about 15 s while the first answer is still running, and is dropped. At 26, 35, 60 and 90 s the Short version shows 'The storm killed 12 people in the valley, officials said.' and, in full, 'Officials said the death toll rose to 31 on Monday…'. Only 1 model call is made. Offline, r5ai/mine/busy-refresh.ts (fake timers) gives the same: 1 request at 145 s, with both lines in the plan. Round 3 notes measured 10-40 s per call on the fallback, and long articles are condensed in sequential parts, so calls over 15 s are common.

Repro: bun /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/r5ai/mock.ts (port 8879, has the TOLLSLOW marker); SIFT_PORT=9469 ./scripts/chrome.sh start && reload; SIFT_PORT=9469 bun r5ai/settings.ts mock; SIFT_PORT=9469 SIFT_TAB=R3-ai-background bun r5ai/mine/e2e-watch.ts tollslow.html TOLLSLOW 8,12,20,26,35,60,90 "killed|toll". Offline, from the project directory: bun r5ai/mine/busy-refresh.ts

Suspected cause: refresh() keeps one timer per article. When it fires while the job is busy (or paused), it throws the pending refresh away instead of waiting. On a page that then holds still, no later processAi pass re-arms it. Fix: keep `pending` and re-arm the timer when busy or paused, or check `pending` when the job's done/error arrives.

Skeptic: Reproduced twice in Chrome 9469 with the mock (cache cleared each time). On tollslow.html (20 s model), the page changes 12 to 31 at 9 s. From 24-26 s until 90 s, the Short version shows 'The storm killed 12 people in the valley, officials said.' with the full paragraph 'Officials said the death toll rose to 31…' right after it. There is only 1 condense call. Offline, busy-refresh.ts gives the same: 1 request at 145 s. Control: toll.html with a fast model makes 2 calls and shows 'killed 31' from 18 s. So the only difference is that the job is busy when the refresh timer fires. The code matches the claim. The timer callback in refresh() (ai.ts ~372-380) deletes pending, then returns on now.busy or !allowed. The done/error handler in condense() never looks at pending, and processAi runs only when the extraction JSON changes (index.ts:1506, 1601). A page that keeps mutating its model (relative times) would re-arm it. One that holds still never does. Effect: a lasting contradiction (the stale number in the summary sentence). The current paragraph is shown too, so I would rate it medium-high rather than high.

Skeptic's fix lead: src/content/ai.ts refresh(): when the timer finds now.busy or !allowed('condense'), keep pending[k] and re-arm the timer (for example 2 s later) instead of returning. Alternatively, in condense()'s done/error handler, check pending.get(k) for each key in job.keys and call the refresh path again.

## F571 (medium): A changed paragraph is shown in full next to the model's sentence about its old text: the Short version states two different numbers, permanently once the refresh limit is reached

Found by: R3-ai-background. Where: src/content/short.ts:435-457 (fresh units are put back, but the md segment the model wrote for the old paragraph stays); src/content/ai.ts:363 (MAX_REFRESH = 4, after which nothing is asked again)

Evidence: livecount.html: a long paragraph 'Rescuers have evacuated N people…' goes up every 18 s. By 80 s there were 5 condense calls (4 refreshes). At 120, 150 and 170 s the Short version shows 'Rescuers have evacuated 248 people so far.' above 'Rescuers have evacuated 433 people from the flooded villages so far…', while the page says 433. Offline, r5ai/mine/limit.ts gives the same: 5 requests, then '248' next to '396'. Even with a fast model the mixed state shows during each refresh wait. toll.html at 12 and 14 s shows 'The storm killed 12 people' and 'the death toll rose to 31' together, until the refresh lands at 16 s. Each refresh also re-sends the whole article (5 paid calls in 80 s here).

Repro: Mock and Chrome as above, then SIFT_PORT=9469 SIFT_TAB=R3-ai-background bun r5ai/mine/e2e-watch.ts livecount.html LIVE 10,40,80,120,150,170 evacuated (fixture at r5ai/fx/livecount.html). Offline: bun r5ai/mine/limit.ts

Suspected cause: planShort adds fresh units but keeps the md segments placed at them (s.pos === that unit), which summarize the old text. Fix: drop md segments whose best match is a changed (not new) unit when that unit is put back in full, or show the full text for that part once MAX_REFRESH is reached.

Skeptic: Reproduced in Chrome on livecount.html. At 40 s the Short version showed 'Rescuers have evacuated 137 people so far.' above the full paragraph '…evacuated 174 people…' (2 calls). At 80 s it was clean at 248 (5 calls). At 120, 150 and 170 s it showed '…evacuated 248 people so far.' above the full '…322/396/433 people…', and still 5 calls (MAX_REFRESH = 4 reached). Offline, limit.ts gives the same: 5 requests, then 248 next to 396. On toll.html with a fast model, at 12 and 14 s the Short version showed 'killed 12' together with the full 'rose to 31', fixed at 18 s. In planShort (short.ts ~435-457), fresh units are put back in full, but the md segment matched to that unit (s.pos === i), which summarizes the old text, stays. During a wait the mix is short. After the limit it lasts for good, so the Short version states two different numbers. Medium.

Skeptic's fix lead: src/content/short.ts planShort: when a fresh unit is put back in full, drop the md segments whose pos is that unit (the model's sentence about its old text). Or, once refreshes reach MAX_REFRESH in ai.ts, show the full text for that article part.

## F572 (medium): When the page replaces the article element and changes anything small, the Short version disappears and the whole article is condensed again

Found by: R3-ai-background. Where: src/content/ai.ts:291-306 (a new article key finds no job and no store entry); src/content/ai.ts:397-398 (jobs are shared only when the text is exactly the same)

Evidence: rekey.html (r5ai/fx): at 6 s the page replaces <article> with a new element that has the same text plus <p><small>Updated 10:42</small></p> (one more [[B]] line). At 5 s the Short version shows, after 1 call. At 6.3 s there is no Short version and no 'Shortening…': the full text is back. At 7 s the Short version is back, after 2 calls. With a 10-40 s model the reader sees the full text for that long, and pays twice. On a live BBC sport page (bbc.com/sport/football/live/c6pvglrn2mj4t) I once saw 2 condense calls within 10 s for texts that differed only by one '[[B7]]' line. A second load made 1 call, so I did not prove the cause there.

Repro: Mock and Chrome as above, then SIFT_PORT=9469 SIFT_TAB=R3-ai-background bun r5ai/mine/e2e-watch.ts rekey.html RERENDER 5,6.3,7,8,10,15 "council approved|Updated|SHORT"

Suspected cause: Keys come from element identity (generic.ts:3668, 'art:' + refOf(el)). The re-render handling covers either the same key (sameArticle, then the refresh path) or an identical text (twin), not a new key with a slightly different text. Fix: for a new key, look for a job of this gen whose meta passes sameArticle(meta, src), attach to it, and let the refresh logic handle the difference.

Skeptic: Reproduced twice in Chrome on rekey.html (cache cleared). At 5 s the Short version showed after 1 call. At 6.2, 6.3 and 6.6 s there was no Short version and no pending indicator (pending=false), so the full text was back. At 7-7.5 s the Short version returned, with 2 condense calls in mock.log (172 and 175 words). The code matches. The article key is 'art:'+refOf(el) (generic.ts article()), and a new element gives a new key. In processAi that key has no job and no store entry, so it goes through the 600 ms steady wait and a new condense. condense() only shares a job through jobs.get(src.text), an exact text match. The other key's job is not reused even though sameArticle would pass. With a fast mock the gap is about 1 s. With the slow fallback it is the model's full latency, and the call is paid twice. How often real sites replace the article element with slightly changed text after the first request is unmeasured (the reporter's BBC case was not proven). Medium.

Skeptic's fix lead: src/content/ai.ts processAi/condense: for a key with no job, first look for a job of this gen (or a store.ai 'condense:' entry) whose meta passes sameArticle(meta, src). Attach the new key to it, so the old Short version keeps showing, and let the existing proseChanged/refresh path handle the small difference.

## F573 (medium, regression): One-liners are written in the lang attribute's language: a page marked lang="en" with Spanish headlines gets English (translated) lines

Found by: R3-ai-background. Where: src/background/index.ts:476-477 (every entry gets knownLang(req.lang) when the page's lang is set; the title is no longer looked at); page.lang is only documentElement.lang (dom.ts:805-810)

Evidence: news-lang.html (r5ai/fx): <html lang="en"> with 5 Spanish headlines (news mode). The lines request sent "lang":"en" for every entry, for example {"id":"1","lang":"en","title":"El gobierno provincial anunció que las escuelas…"}. LINES_RULES says 'Write the line in the language given by the entry's "lang" field. Never use any other language'. lineFits then accepts English lines: the mock's English line 'A plain line about video number 4 of the list' was shown, while the teaser under it stays Spanish. Short versions on the same site stay Spanish ('Same language as the text. Never translate'). Before round 4, entryLang(title, page.lang) kept 'es' for a reliably detected Spanish title with Spanish function words. The same policy also translates every Japanese or Spanish title on YouTube or Gmail whose interface is in English.

Repro: Mock and Chrome as above; SIFT_PORT=9469 SIFT_TAB=R3-ai-background bun scripts/visit.ts http://127.0.0.1:8879/fx/news-lang.html x --wait 6000; read the last 'lines' entry in r5ai/mock.log

Suspected cause: The F308 fix chose 'always page.lang' and trusts the attribute. Fix: still one language per list, but check it first. Run detectLanguage once on the joined titles; when that is reliably another language than page.lang, use it for the whole list.

Skeptic: Reproduced in Chrome. news-lang.html (lang="en", 5 Spanish headlines) sent a lines request with "lang":"en" on every entry (mock.log). The outline then replaced three Spanish headlines with English one-liners ('A plain line about video number 0 of the list'), while the teasers under them stayed Spanish. The other two rows kept their titles because the mock's filler lines were rejected. The code matches: background/index.ts:476-477 uses knownLang(req.lang) for every item whenever the attribute is known. Before round 4, entryLang() per title would have returned 'es' for these titles, because they are reliably detected and have Spanish function words. So it is a regression, introduced on purpose by the F308 fix (option 1 of the F308 verdict). The trigger is a mislabeled lang attribute, which is a site fault. But Sift's response is to replace each headline with a line in a language the reader may not read, while its Short versions on the same page stay Spanish. I count that as a real medium defect for such pages. The YouTube part of the claim (Japanese titles get English lines under an English interface) is the deliberate F308 policy, not a defect, and I do not confirm that part.

Skeptic's fix lead: src/background/index.ts doLines (:476-477): keep one language per list, but check the attribute first. Run detect() once on the joined titles. When the result is reliable, differs from page.lang, and passes entryLang's function-word test, use it for the whole list instead of page.lang.

## F395 (still open, partly; original repro in /home/nico/projects/sift/research/round4/*.md)

Recheck by R3-ai-background: Fixed for paragraphs of 8 words or more. grow.html: at 12 s the new section shows in full, and at 16 s it is shortened ('The stadium reopened in 2008 and the garden in 2009'). toll.html: 2 calls, and from 16 s on the page shows 'The storm killed 31 people'. Not fixed for shorter paragraphs. dfp and freshUnits only look at paragraphs of LONG = 8 words or more (short.ts:153, :223, :242), so a changed or new short paragraph neither triggers a refresh nor gets put back. tollshort.html: 'The death toll rose to 12.' becomes '…31.' at 9 s. At 14, 25 and 40 s the Short version still says 'The storm killed 12 people', with 1 call. update.html: an appended <h2>Update</h2><p>The dam on the river has burst.</p> never appears in the Short version (1 call at 40 s). The new heading is also dropped as empty. Offline, r5ai/mine/short-small.ts gives proseChanged=false for both cases. Also see the new findings below on the refresh being dropped while busy and on the stale sentence.

Skeptic: Partly fixed, confirmed in Chrome. Long paragraphs work: on toll.html, 2 calls and 'killed 31' from 18 s (my control run). Short paragraphs do not. On tollshort.html, 'The death toll rose to 12.' became '31.' at 9 s, and at 14, 25 and 40 s the Short version still said 'The storm killed 12 people…', with 1 call and the changed line not shown. On update.html, the appended <h2>Update</h2><p>The dam on the river has burst.</p> appears on the page from 14 s but never in the Short version up to 40 s, again with 1 call. Offline, short-small.ts gives proseChanged=false for both. Cause: dfp, freshUnits and proseChanged only consider units with at least LONG = 8 words (short.ts ~153, ~223, ~242), and the new heading is then dropped as empty. The 8-word gate is meant for ticking times, but it also hides short news updates and number changes. Medium.

Skeptic's fix lead: src/content/short.ts: let freshUnits put back new short prose units whose letters-only text (normWords) is new, even under LONG, at no model cost. For the refresh decision, use a lower threshold (for example 4 words) or exclude only lines that look like times or dates (Updated 10:42, 5 min ago), so 'The death toll rose to 31.' counts as a change.
