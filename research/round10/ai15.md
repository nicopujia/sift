## F1035 (high): G1 article Short version is written in English on a Portuguese page, and the English version is cached

Found by: T2-news. Where: https://g1.globo.com/mundo/noticia/2026/10/26/trump-fala-sobre-eleicao-no-brasil-pareceu-mais-uma-eleicao-fraudada.ghtml

Evidence: The page is html lang pt-BR, and Sift's view has lang pt-BR with Portuguese UI. The Short version paragraphs read 'Trump said Brazil’s election looked fraudulent but gave no evidence. He also said he likes Lula.', 'Lula defeated Flávio Bolsonaro, a Trump ally, in the presidential runoff…' and so on. The cache entry (02:37:30, 1544 chars) holds the English text, and its saved input text is Portuguese ('Sem apresentar provas, Trump fala de…'). A second visit about an hour later showed the same English text. Round 9 saw this article's Short version in Portuguese ('Trump afirmou, sem provas, que a eleição brasileira “pareceu fraudada”…', /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/r9t2/g1trump-short.txt). Spanish Short versions on Infobae, La Nación and Clarín stayed Spanish today. Shot: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-2/research/shots/r10t2-g1-english-short2.png

Repro: SIFT_PORT=9462 SIFT_TAB=T2-news bun scripts/act.ts goto:<g1 url> wait:20000 'eval:[...document.querySelector("sift-root").shadowRoot.querySelectorAll("p")].map(p=>p.innerText).filter(t=>/^Trump said/.test(t))'  → ['Trump said Brazil’s election looked fraudulent…']. It is cached, so every visit shows it until the entry expires.

Suspected cause: doCondense never checks the language of its output. The only language rule is in the prompt ('Same language as the text. Never translate.'), and the user message around the text is English ('Title: … The text has N words. Write about …'). A model that answers in English is saved and shown. A script and language check like vetLine's for lines (scriptFits / entryLang against req.lang) is missing for condense.

Skeptic: Reproduced on two visits. The G1 page is html lang pt-BR and Sift's root is lang pt-BR. The Short version paragraphs are in English: 'Trump said Brazil’s election looked fraudulent but gave no evidence…', 'Lula defeated Flávio Bolsonaro, a Trump ally…' and 'Asked if he would act on the result…'. One Portuguese paragraph sits among them. The same English text came back on the second visit, so it is served from cache. In src/background/index.ts, doCondense (lines 738-860) does no language check on the model's output. checked() only strips preambles, title lines and unsupported quotes. The only guard is the prompt rule 'Same language as the text. Never translate.', and the user message around the text is English. Lines get a language check through scriptFits/entryLang (around line 680); condense has none.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/background/index.ts doCondense, after the model call and before save(), check the language of the output against req.lang. Reuse entryLang/scriptFits/langScript from src/background/text.ts, as the line check does around line 680. On a mismatch, retry once with a note that names the language, and never cache a wrong-language result. Also run that check in checked() on cache hits, so the English entry already cached is dropped. Optionally name the language in the user message ('Write in Portuguese').

## F1036 (high): La Nación home headline lines misattribute: Milei's allies become Bolsonaro's, and a quote about the vote extension is pinned on Lula's win

Found by: T2-news. Where: https://www.lanacion.com.ar/ (home, headline lines after the AI rewrite)

Evidence: (1) The original card is 'Los amigos de Milei. Tras la derrota de Bolsonaro, las elecciones que ponen a prueba al club de sus aliados'. The article H1 is 'Las elecciones que ponen a prueba al club de aliados internacionales de Milei'. Sift's line says 'Las elecciones que ponen a prueba a los aliados de Bolsonaro tras su derrota'. That names the wrong leader. (2) The original card is '"Hubo un gravísimo error". Diego Guelar analizó el triunfo de Lula: “cambio de reglas”, la relación con Milei y el posible indulto a Bolsonaro'. Sift's line says 'Diego Guelar analizó el triunfo de Lula, un “gravísimo error”, el “cambio de reglas”, …', which reads as if Guelar called Lula's win a grave error. The article says Guelar called the court's two-hour extension of voting a 'gravísimo error'. This is the F927 pattern (a teaser quote joined to the story's event), still alive with PROMPT_VERSION 11. The other 98 La Nación pairs I read are faithful. Pairs: /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/r10t2/ln-home-pairs.txt

Repro: SIFT_PORT=9462 SIFT_TAB=T2-news bun scripts/act.ts goto:https://www.lanacion.com.ar/ wait:60000 'eval:[...document.querySelector("sift-root").shadowRoot.querySelectorAll("a")].filter(a=>/Guelar|ponen a prueba/.test(a.innerText)).map(a=>a.innerText)'. Then open the two hrefs (…/tras-la-derrota-de-bolsonaro-las-elecciones-que-ponen-a-prueba-al-club-de-aliados-internacionales-de-nid26102026/ and …/diego-guelar-analizo-el-triunfo-de-lula-…-nid25102026/) and read the H1 and the 'gravísimo error' sentence.

Suspected cause: The news-line prompt's new rule covers only order and cause words ('after', 'because'). It does not cover resolving a pronoun ('sus aliados') to the nearest name, dropping the kicker that names the subject ('Los amigos de Milei'), or attaching a separate kicker quote by apposition. vetLine's quoteAsFact does not catch a quote moved next to a different noun.

Skeptic: Reproduced from the cached lines on La Nación home. (1) Sift shows 'Las elecciones que ponen a prueba a los aliados de Bolsonaro tras su derrota'. The original card reads 'A FONDO Los amigos de Milei. Tras la derrota de Bolsonaro, las elecciones que ponen a prueba al club de sus aliados'. The article H1 (fetched) is 'Las elecciones que ponen a prueba al club de aliados internacionales de Milei'. Sift's line names the wrong leader, a clear factual error. (2) Sift shows 'Diego Guelar analizó el triunfo de Lula, un “gravísimo error”, el “cambio de reglas”…'. The article's kicker is the separate quote '"Hubo un gravísimo error".' The body says Guelar called the court's two-hour voting extension a 'gravísimo error'. Placed right after 'el triunfo de Lula', the quote reads as apposition, as if he called Lula's win the error. Item 1 alone is a high-severity meaning error, and item 2 is misleading.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/background/index.ts, add to the news-line rules (the news prompt's LINES_RULES section): a kicker before the headline ('Los amigos de Milei.', a quote) sets whose story it is. A possessive ('sus') refers to the kicker's subject, never to the nearest name. A kicker quote stays with its speaker and is never set beside another noun. In vetLine, reject a line that drops a person's name present in the kicker and adds a possessive tie to a different name. Extend quoteAsFact (src/background/text.ts) to flag a title's kicker quote placed directly after a different noun phrase.

## F1044 (high): Search results in a <dl> get a Short version by default: 7 of 25 results hidden, snippets replaced by AI prose

Found by: T4-generic. Where: https://wiki.debian.org/SourcesList?action=fullsearch&context=180&value=pinning&fullsearch=Text (MoinMoin search, reached from Sift's search field and 'Text' button)

Evidence: The results page opens as 'SHORT VERSION / Show full text'. Only 18 of the 25 result titles in the original's dl.searchresults are links in Sift, and none of the 25 context snippets (dd) are shown. Under the first result (SummerOfCode2009/Aptitude_mixed_install_apt_pinning_integration) Sift puts an AI sentence about the whole search: 'The search found pages about APT pinning and other uses of “pinning.”...'. A description of that first result appears further down, after other links. Bare links repeat (AptConfiguration x2, Glossary x2, DebianTesting x4). 'Show full text' brings back 25/25 titles and 25/25 snippets. Shot: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-4/research/shots/r10t4-debwiki-search.png

Repro: SIFT_PORT=9464 SIFT_TAB=T4-generic bun scripts/act.ts 'goto:https://wiki.debian.org/SourcesList?action=fullsearch&context=180&value=pinning&fullsearch=Text' wait:45000 'eval:(()=>{const M=document.querySelector("sift-root").shadowRoot.querySelector("main");const st=M.innerText.replace(/\s+/g," ");const dts=[...document.querySelectorAll(".searchresults dt")].map(d=>d.querySelector("a").innerText.trim());const dds=[...document.querySelectorAll(".searchresults dd")].map(d=>d.innerText.replace(/\s+/g," ").trim());const sl=[...M.querySelectorAll("a")].map(a=>a.innerText.trim());return [dts.length,dts.filter(t=>sl.includes(t)).length,dds.filter(t=>st.includes(t.slice(0,40))).length]})()' prints [25,18,0]. After 'click:Show full text' it prints [25,25,25].

Suspected cause: A <dl> of dt link + dd snippet is not recognized as a result list, so the page goes to findArticle() and gets condensed like an article. A dl whose dt entries each hold one link should become a list of items, and a page with a search-results title ('Full Text Search: ...', 'Results 1 - 25 of about 96') should never be condensed.

Skeptic: Reproduced twice. The MoinMoin search page ('Full Text Search: "pinning"', 25 results in dl.searchresults) opens as SHORT VERSION. The repro eval prints [25,18,0] both times. The 7 results that are not links are it/AptConfiguration, ja/Glossary, it/DebianTesting, pt_BR/DebianTesting, ko/DebianTesting, de/Backports and sv/DebianExperimental. They appear only as words inside AI prose. Under the first result, the AI text describes the whole search ('The search found pages about APT pinning...'). Descriptions are also misattributed: under 'NvidaGraphicsDrivers . . . 8 matches' is a paragraph about AptConfiguration, Glossary and DebianTesting. 'zh_CN/DebianRepository/Setup' gets the Swedish DebianExperimental description, and 'DebianTesting . . . 3 matches' gets none. After 'Show full text' it prints [25,25,25]. A result list loses links and gets wrong snippets, so this is a functional loss, not polish.

Skeptic's fix lead: src/content/extract/generic.ts defs(): a dl whose dt entries each hold one link with a short dd snippet (MoinMoin .searchresults) should become an items list (link title + snippet), not facts or bold p plus walked blocks. The Short-version gate (findArticle() and short.ts) should not condense a page whose main content is such a result list.
