## F1072 (medium): Spanish Wikipedia 'Pan': Sift's H1 is the site tagline 'Wikipedia, la enciclopedia libre', and the article title is an H2

Found by: T3-tools. Where: https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pan

Evidence: Sift's main headings: h1 'Wikipedia, la enciclopedia libre', h2 'Pan', h2 'Historia'. The original has a single h1#firstHeading 'Pan' (height 40). The tagline exists only in a hidden div#siteSub and in document.title ('Pan - Wikipedia, la enciclopedia libre'). A screen reader user who jumps to the H1 hears the site's tagline, not the article's name, and the large visual title is wrong. es Focaccia and en Bread are correct (h1 'Focaccia', h1 'Bread'). Round 9's shot of this page shows the same H1, so it is not new. Shot: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-3/research/shots/R10-T3-eswiki-pan.png

Repro: cd /home/nico/projects/sift-test-3; export SIFT_PORT=9463 SIFT_TAB=T3-tools; bun scripts/act.ts goto:https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pan wait:9000 "eval:[...document.querySelector('sift-root').shadowRoot.querySelectorAll('main h1, main h2')].slice(0,2).map(h=>h.localName+':'+h.innerText).join(' | ')+' || orig h1: '+[...document.querySelectorAll('h1')].map(h=>h.innerText).join(',')"   (prints 'h1:Wikipedia, la enciclopedia libre | h2:Pan || orig h1: Pan')

Suspected cause: The page title is split on ' - ' and the wrong half is taken, or a Wikimedia box near the top is read as the article header (round 9's shot shows a 'Proyectos Wikimedia · 13 ene 2004' byline under that H1).

Skeptic: Reproduced. Sift's headings are h1 'Wikipedia, la enciclopedia libre', h2 'Pan', h2 'Historia'. The original has one visible h1 'Pan' (height 39.6). #siteSub ('De Wikipedia, la enciclopedia libre') is display:none, and document.title is 'Pan - Wikipedia, la enciclopedia libre'. Likely cause (from reading the code): titleH1() in generic.ts filters h1s with textIn(h).length > 3, so the 3-letter 'Pan' is never a candidate. pageTitle() then falls back to docTitle(). docTitle() splits the title on ' - ' and keeps the longest part that does not match the site name ('es.wikipedia.org' does not match the tagline), which gives the tagline. en Bread works because 'Wikipedia' alone matches the host and is dropped, and 'Bread' is longer than 3 letters. The wrong H1 harms screen reader navigation. Medium is fair. It is not a regression.

Skeptic's fix lead: src/content/extract/generic.ts titleH1(): accept short h1s (for example length >= 1 or >= 2 instead of > 3). In docTitle(), also drop a part that contains a word of the site's host name (wikipedia), or prefer the part that matches a visible h1, over the longest part.

## F873 (still open, partly; original repro in /home/nico/projects/sift/research/round9/*.md)

Skeptic: Partly fixed holds, but the lag is rarer and usually smaller than claimed. I used the claimant's fp2t.ts on weather.com today (TemperatureValue vs Sift '\d+°'). At 4x throttle, 3 sets gave 23 runs at load 5-12. Sift matched the original in 21. It was later in 2: 13676/14470 (0.8 s, load 8.7) and 28679/32068 (3.4 s, load 12.5). On a slow or busy CPU, Sift still sometimes shows the forecast seconds after the page does. I did not see the claimed 3 of 10 at 5-7 s at 4x, but I saw a 6.8 s lag at 6x. Medium only for slow machines.

Skeptic's fix lead: src/content/index.ts schedule(): while stillLoading() and Sift has not yet shown the page's main content (thinView, or no change since the last big addition), skip the requestIdleCallback path and cap d at about 300 ms. Also run once on readystatechange to 'complete'. A poll trace of a lagging 6x run would show which wait (lastDur*3, the idle callback or the 600 ms timeout) holds it.
