## F705 (high): A rewritten or removed paragraph leaves its old fact in the Short version: the old and new death toll show side by side, for good after the refresh limit

Found by: R3-ai-background. Where: src/content/short.ts:371-381 (staleAbout), src/content/ai.ts:415 (MAX_REFRESH)

Evidence: Chrome 9469 with the mock, r7ai/fx/rewordlive.html. The lede alternates between 'The death toll … rose to N' and 'Officials now say N people died after rescuers searched…'. With ?at=6000, the Short version at 12 s shows both 'Officials now say 31 people died…' and 'The death toll from the storm rose to 12.'. With ?at=6000&every=17000: at 90 s it shows '107 people died' next to 'rose to 88'. At 115 s it shows '145 people died' next to 'rose to 88'. The call count stayed at 3 from 70 s to 130 s, so the refresh limit had been reached. Offline (r7ai/t/reword.ts): the model's 'The storm killed 12 people.' stays next to the new paragraph 'Thirty-one people are now confirmed dead…'. r7ai/t/removed.ts: when the paragraph 'Police said two men were arrested…' is removed from the article, 'Police arrested two men for looting.' stays in the Short version.

Repro: bun /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/r7ai/t/reword.ts and t/removed.ts (run from /home/nico/projects/sift-test-9). In Chrome: start r7ai/mock.ts (port 8871), SIFT_PORT=9469 bun r7ai/settings.ts, then SIFT_PORT=9469 SIFT_TAB=R3-ai-background bun r7ai/watch.ts 'rewordlive.html?at=6000&every=17000' 12,30,50,70,90,100,115,130 'toll|died|killed'

Suspected cause: staleAbout counts an old paragraph as changed only when a fresh paragraph shares at least half its tokens (dice >= 0.5). It returns null when no paragraph is fresh, and it takes `gone` numbers only from those changed paragraphs. A lede rewritten in other words (common in live news) or a removed paragraph is never treated as stale. After MAX_REFRESH, nothing replaces the old sentence. Fix lead: treat every old paragraph that is no longer in the article as changed, with or without a close replacement. Drop the sentences that best match it, and drop any sentence stating a number it held that the article no longer has.

Skeptic: Confirmed, high. Offline, t/reword.ts keeps 'The storm killed 12 people.' next to the full new paragraph in both wordings ('Thirty-one people are now confirmed dead…' and 'Officials now say 31 people died…'). t/removed.ts keeps 'Police arrested two men for looting.' after that paragraph is gone, with proseChanged true. In Chrome 9469 with the mock, rewordlive.html?at=6000 showed 'Officials now say 31 people died…' next to 'The death toll from the storm rose to 12.' at 12 s. The refresh fixed it by 20 s. With ?at=6000&every=17000, the condense calls stopped at 3 from 70 s to 140 s (one refresh came from the cache, so all 4 MAX_REFRESH were used). At 90 s it showed '107 people died' next to 'rose to 88', and at 115 s '145 people died' next to 'rose to 88'. When the lede went back to the 'rose to N' wording (100 s, 130 s), staleAbout did drop the old sentence. So only reworded or removed paragraphs are missed, as the claim says. The cause is in the code: staleAbout (short.ts:369-380) returns null when there is no fresh paragraph, and counts an old paragraph as changed only when a fresh one has dice >= 0.5 with it. The gap lasts until the refresh lands, and for good once the limit is reached.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/content/short.ts staleAbout: drop the `!fresh.size` early return. Count every old paragraph whose fingerprint is no longer in src.dfp (within sentUnits) as changed, with or without a close fresh match. Take `gone` numbers from all of those paragraphs. Keep the best-match test so that sentences mainly about a gone or reworded paragraph are dropped, and keep the restore logic for merged facts. Optionally, in ai.ts once MAX_REFRESH is reached and the prose still differs, show the full text instead.

## F706 (medium, regression): The new link-word check strips correct links whose words were inflected or reworded: the link moves to 'Links in the full text', or vanishes when the article has more than 30 links

Found by: R3-ai-background. Where: src/content/short.ts:337-349 (sameWords), :659-667, :674

Evidence: Offline (r7ai/t/inflect.ts): '[la nueva norma](L0)' for the source words 'las nuevas normas de estacionamiento' loses its link. So do '[новые правила](L0)' for 'новых правил парковки', '[die neue Parkregel](L0)' for 'die neuen Parkregeln', and '[Book an appointment](L0)' for 'click here'. Chrome with the mock, r7ai/fx/linkword.html: the Short version shows 'Book an appointment online…' as plain text. The booking link appears only at the end, as 'Links in the full text: click here'. r7ai/fx/linkmany.html is the same article with 33 distinct links: the booking link is nowhere in the Short version. Before round 6 the link stayed on 'Book an appointment' and opened /book.

Repro: bun /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/r7ai/t/inflect.ts and t/here2.ts. In Chrome with the mock: SIFT_PORT=9469 SIFT_TAB=R3-ai-background bun r7ai/outline.ts linkword.html 4 (and linkmany.html)

Suspected cause: sameWords needs an exact shared token of 4 or more letters, or 2 shared tokens. Inflected forms (nueva/nuevas, правила/правил, Parkregel/Parkregeln) fail. So does a descriptive phrase that replaces a generic label ('click here', 'aquí'). The extra list is skipped when there are more than 30 distinct links. Fix lead: also accept words that share a stem or prefix with Link.a, or that appear in the source sentence around the link. When the words are rejected, keep the link on the source's own words instead of dropping it.

Skeptic: Confirmed, medium regression from the F616 fix. Offline, t/inflect.ts strips the link from '[la nueva norma](L0)' (source 'las nuevas normas de estacionamiento'), '[новые правила](L0)' ('новых правил парковки'), '[die neue Parkregel](L0)' ('die neuen Parkregeln') and '[Book an appointment](L0)' ('click here'). The link moves to the extra list. Only '[the application form](L0)' for 'this form' keeps it. t/here2.ts, with 33 distinct links: the booking link is nowhere in the plan. In Chrome with the mock, linkword.html showed 'Book an appointment online…' as plain text, plus 'Links in the full text: [link] click here'. linkmany.html showed the same plain text with no extra list, so the link is reachable only after 'Show full text'. sameWords (short.ts:337-349) needs an exact shared token of 4 or more letters, so inflected forms and a descriptive rewrite of a generic label always fail. I did not run a pre-round-6 build. The 'before round 6' behavior comes from F616's own description (no code checked the words then). The prompt does tell the model to keep link words, so how often real models trip this is unmeasured. Inflection changes in Russian and German rewrites, and 'click here' turning into a descriptive phrase, are plausible.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/content/short.ts sameWords: also accept a match on a shared prefix stem (for example, the first 4-5 letters of tokens of 4 or more letters, so nueva/nuevas and Parkregel/Parkregeln match). Also accept model words that appear in a small window of the source paragraph around that link (the source sentence 'To book an appointment, click here'). Still reject words that match another link's text better, which keeps the F616 protection. In planShort (around :674), always list a link that sameWords rejected in plan.extra, even when distinct.size > 30, so it never vanishes from the Short version.

## F707 (medium, regression): A lines batch that stops at a filter or the output limit now keeps the title of every unanswered entry, on the page and for a day: one flagged headline, or a batch in a long-token script, loses the lines of the whole batch

Found by: R3-ai-background. Where: src/content/ai.ts:303-308; src/background/index.ts:578-583, :548/:557 (budget 120 + 50 per entry)

Evidence: Mock, r7ai/fx/onefilter.html: 6 headlines, one marked [FILTER]. The mock stops the batch with finish_reason content_filter, as a filter or a 403 moderation of the batch input would. After 3 calls (the chain), all 6 keep their titles, and a revisit makes 0 calls. IndexedDB holds keep-title entries with a 1-day TTL. r7ai/fx/limit.html: the mock stops with finish_reason length after the first line. 1 entry gets a line, the other 4 keep their titles, there is 1 call, and a revisit makes 0 calls. The budget does not depend on the script. By the code's own estTokens, a 12-word Tamil line as JSON is 115 tokens and a Hindi one 82 (r7ai/t/budget.ts), so a 25-entry Tamil batch (1,370 tokens) fits about 11 lines. Before round 6, cut entries were asked for again. I could not measure real token counts without paid calls.

Repro: Mock running and settings set, then: SIFT_PORT=9469 SIFT_TAB=R3-ai-background bun r7ai/watch.ts onefilter.html 5,12 'Police|court|plain line' twice (the second run shows 0 calls), and the same for limit.html. bun r7ai/t/budget.ts for the token estimates.

Suspected cause: The F610 fix treats every final error alike. A filter or an output limit is about the batch, not each entry. Fix lead: on 'length', ask once more for the unanswered entries in a smaller batch. On a filter or moderation stop, split the batch to isolate the flagged entry, and cache KEEP_TITLE only for entries that fail alone. Size maxTokens from estTokens of the titles, as condense does.

Skeptic: Confirmed as code behavior, medium at most. The regression covers only the output-limit case. In Chrome with the mock, after clearing the cache, onefilter.html made 3 lines calls (the whole chain filtered with no text). All 6 headlines kept their titles at 5 s and 12 s, and a second visit made 0 calls. Control: the same page without [FILTER] gave all 6 'Plain line' rewrites in 1 call. Before I cleared the cache, the control page also sent only the flagged headline, because the 5 innocent headlines were cached KEEP_TITLE site-wide. limit.html: 1 call, 1 line, 4 titles kept, a revisit made 0 calls. idb.ts shows keep-title rows with ttlDays 1. background/index.ts:581 caches KEEP_TITLE for every unanswered entry on any final Incomplete. Caveats: (1) For the filter case, the outcome is not new. Before the F610 fix, the same batch, flagged entry included, was re-sent and filtered again (endlessly), so those entries got no lines then either. Only the length case loses entries that a smaller retry would have answered. (2) A filter with no text moves the chain to the next model. The whole batch is lost only when every model refuses, or when one stops mid-stream. (3) The bench data (research/llm_bench_results) shows gemini-3.5-flash-lite using 28-34 completion tokens per Latin-script entry against a budget of 50 per entry plus 120. The Tamil and Hindi overflow rests on estTokens, not on real token counts, so a real 'length' stop is unproven. The outcome is lost one-line descriptions, with titles kept. Nothing is wrong or broken.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/background/index.ts doLines (around :578-583): on a final 'output limit reached', do not cache KEEP_TITLE. Send the unanswered misses once more as a smaller batch, or let ai.ts re-request them once. On a filter or moderation stop, split the unanswered entries in halves and retry, caching KEEP_TITLE only for an entry that fails alone (or for the entry the stream stopped at). Optionally size maxTokens from estTokens of the titles (as condense does) instead of a flat 50 or 70 per entry.
