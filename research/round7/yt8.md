## F720 (high): At 200% zoom (640 CSS px), the watch page loses Save: the original moves it into 'More actions', which Sift does not show

Found by: T1-youtube. Where: YouTube watch page at 640x450 with deviceScaleFactor 2 (200% zoom of a 1280 window), action row under the title

Evidence: Action rows, Sift against the original's visible #actions buttons. At 1280x900 Sift has 'Subscribe | Like 19M | Dislike this video | Save', and the original has Like, Dislike, Share, 'Save to playlist' and 'More actions'. At 640x450@2x Sift has 'Subscribe | Like 19M | Dislike this video' only, and the original has Like, Dislike, Share and 'More actions'. In original mode at that size, 'More actions' opens a menu reading 'Save Report'. At 400x800 Sift shows Save again, because YouTube shows it there. So at 200% zoom, Save (Watch later or a playlist) cannot be reached in Sift and the only way is to switch to the original. Tab order at 200% goes Like 19M > Dislike this video > Description, with no Save. Shots: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1/research/shots/r7t1-zoom200-actions.png and /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1/research/shots/r7t1-zoom200-orig-more.png

Repro: cd /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1; SIFT_PORT=9461 SIFT_TAB=T1-youtube bun /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/t1r7/actions.ts 640 450 2 (it sets the viewport, loads watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ, waits 16 s, and prints Sift's buttons next to the original's visible #actions buttons; it resets to 1280x900 afterwards). Compare with 'actions.ts 1280 900 1'. To see the original's menu: set 640x450@2x, load the same video, press Alt+Shift+S, then run /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/t1r7/moreactions.ts. It prints 'Save Report'.

Suspected cause: The YouTube adapter reads Save only from the visible top-level action buttons. When YouTube's layout folds Save into the 'More actions' overflow menu (ytd-menu-renderer's overflow items), the adapter neither reads that menu's items nor offers a 'More actions' button.

Skeptic: Reproduced on the live watch page (dQw4w9WgXcQ). At 1280x900, Sift shows Subscribe, Like 19M, Dislike this video and Save, and the original shows Like, Dislike, Share, 'Save to playlist' and More actions. At 640x450@2x (200% zoom), Sift shows only Subscribe, Like 19M and Dislike this video. The original shows Like, Dislike, Share and More actions. After switching to the original with Alt+Shift+S, More actions opens a menu reading 'Save Report', and a screenshot shows it. The problem is wider than 200%: at 1024x720@1.25 (125% zoom), Save is also folded into More actions in the original and is missing from Sift. At 853x600@1.5, Save is present in both. The code matches what I saw. In src/content/adapters/youtube.ts (around line 572), the watch actions look for Save only among the visible '#top-level-buttons-computed button, #flexible-item-buttons button'. Sift never offers the overflow 'More actions' button. So at these widths, Save (and Report) can only be reached in the original.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/content/adapters/youtube.ts, in the watch-page actions block after the Save lookup: when no visible Save button is found, add the original's visible overflow button as an action. The selector is 'ytd-watch-metadata #actions ytd-menu-renderer > yt-button-shape button[aria-label]' or '#button-shape button', named by its aria-label. Its ytd-menu-popup-renderer in ytd-popup-container then appears as Sift's dialog, just like the other YouTube popups. Simpler but weaker alternative: always add More actions next to Save.

## F721 (medium): The sign-in prompts from Like and Save close by themselves 1-3 s after opening, while the original's prompt stays open

Found by: T1-youtube. Where: YouTube watch page (signed out), the Like / Save buttons under the title, clicked while the page is still loading its lower parts (about 11-25 s after a fresh load)

Evidence: Timed with /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/t1r7/dlgpoll2.ts and savevanish.ts. In 6 of about 16 runs, Sift's dialog 'Want to watch this again later?' (Save) appeared, then disappeared with focus back on Save, while the original's tp-yt-iron-dropdown with the same text stayed visible. Examples: Sift shown at 2194 ms and gone at 3017 ms; original shown at 408, Sift shown at 1415, Sift gone at 2079 with the original still open. Like did the same: 'Like this video?' shown at 520 ms and gone at 3421 ms with the original open. In one case a second Save click briefly showed Sift's dialog while the original's popup had closed, and then both were gone. Settings did not vanish in 2 runs. When the page is idle, the prompts stay (4 of 4 runs later in the session). Shot after the vanish: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1/research/shots/r7t1-save-vanished.png. Also, Sift's dialog appears 0.3-1.9 s after the original's.

Repro: cd /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1; EXTRA=6000 RUNS=5 SIFT_PORT=9461 SIFT_TAB=T1-youtube bun /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/t1r7/savevanish.ts. Each run is a fresh load: wait for Save, wait 6 s, click Save, poll. It stops at the first run where Sift's dialog vanishes while the original popup is still open and prints 'sift vanished (orig still open) <ms>'. LABEL=Like does the same with Like. It hit in 1 of 3 and 1 of 1 runs. Plain act.ts version: bun scripts/act.ts goto:https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ wait:25000 click:Save, then poll the visible [role=dialog] in Sift's shadow root against document.querySelector('ytd-popup-container tp-yt-iron-dropdown').checkVisibility() once a second for 5 s.

Suspected cause: YouTube's sign-in popups have no dialog role, so they reach Sift only through findPopup in src/content/extract/generic.ts, which wants the popup among the nodes the user's last action touched (recentNodes(true) in src/content/recent.ts). While the page keeps mutating (comments and Up next loading), a later re-extraction no longer counts the popup as recent, either after the armRecent window or after the >400-node pruning, so it is dropped. Not established whether round 6 introduced this.

Skeptic: Reproduced, often. I ran my own poller (fresh load, wait for Save, wait N ms, click, then poll every 100 ms for 8 s). With Save clicked 1-3 s after it appeared, while the page was still loading comments (thread count rising from 0-18 toward 20), Sift's dialog 'Want to watch this again later? Sign in' appeared 0.1-0.5 s after the original's dropdown. It then disappeared 0.3-1.4 s later while the original's tp-yt-iron-dropdown was still visible. Save: 6 of 6 runs at those delays (for example, shown at 383 ms, gone at 814 ms, original still open at 8 s). Like ('Like this video? Sign in to make your opinion count'): 4 of 4 runs (for example, shown at 451 ms, gone at 780 ms, original open to 8 s). With the click 6-18 s after Save appeared, once comments sat at 20, the dialog stayed (4 of 4 runs, plus the reporter's script with EXTRA=6000 gave 0 of 5). In several hit runs the original's popup also closed on its own 0.5-1 s after Sift's vanished. In 4 runs, though, it stayed open for the full 8 s with nothing in Sift. One detail differs from the claim: Sift's dialog lagged the original by only 0.1-0.6 s, not 0.3-1.9 s. The mechanism looks plausible from the code. In src/content/recent.ts, noteMutations' pruning ends with '.slice(-300)', which can drop nodes noted during the action window (byAction) when the page adds hundreds of nodes while loading. In findPopup in src/content/extract/generic.ts, a changed recent[0] re-arms armFirst/armShown. I did not instrument the isolated world to prove which of these is the cause.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/content/extract/generic.ts findPopup, keep shownPop as the popup while it is still connected and visible and location.href === armUrl, and nothing new has been armed since. This is the same rule that already keeps lastPop for popups with fields. In src/content/recent.ts noteMutations, make the >400 pruning never drop byAction nodes: apply slice(-300) only to the 'later' nodes, so the popup's own nodes survive a burst of page mutations.

## F722 (medium, regression): End no longer loads more Up next once YouTube has loaded comments on its own: it loads hidden comments instead, and Up next stays at 20

Found by: T1-youtube. Where: YouTube watch page, Up next section open and Comments section closed, End key at the bottom of Sift's view

Evidence: 3 of 4 fresh loads. With 14 s waits, the original already held 20 comment threads before any key press, although Comments was never opened in Sift. Then Up next gave sift=20 orig=20. End gave Up next 20/20 with comments 40. End gave 20/20 with comments 40. End gave 20/20 with comments 40, in 2 of 2 runs, plus 1 earlier run. In the run where YouTube had not yet loaded comments (origCommentThreads=0), End gave Up next 20 to 40 to 60 in step with the original. On the original's wide layout, the related column's continuation comes into view first, so scrolling down loads more related videos. works.md line 62 ('Up next loads more on End (20->40), in step with the original') no longer holds in these runs.

Repro: cd /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1; SIFT_PORT=9461 SIFT_TAB=T1-youtube bun scripts/act.ts goto:https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ wait:14000 'click:Up next' wait:2000 "eval:(()=>{const r=document.querySelector('sift-root').shadowRoot;const d=[...r.querySelectorAll('details')].find(d=>/Up next/.test(d.querySelector('summary').innerText));return 'siftUpNext='+d.querySelectorAll('li').length+' origRelated='+document.querySelectorAll('#secondary #related yt-lockup-view-model, #secondary #related ytd-compact-video-renderer').length+' origComments='+document.querySelectorAll('ytd-comments ytd-comment-thread-renderer').length})()" press:End wait:4000 (same eval) press:End wait:4000 (same eval). When origComments is 20 before the first End, Up next stays at 20 while origComments goes to 40.

Suspected cause: youtube.loadMore() in src/content/adapters/youtube.ts picks comment continuations whenever q('ytd-comments#comments ytd-comment-thread-renderer') exists. Its comment says that means 'the reader opened them', but YouTube loads the first 20 comments by itself. With Comments closed in Sift and Up next open, the choice should follow the section the reader has open or is scrolled to, not whether comments exist.

Skeptic: Reproduced 3 times. 14 s after a fresh load, with Comments never opened in Sift, the original already holds 20 comment threads. After clicking Up next, Sift shows 20 entries. Each End press then gives Up next 20/20 while the original's comments go 20 to 40 (runs 1 and 2: 20/20/20 after three Ends; run 3: related 20, comments 40). Sift's Up next section has no load-more control. With Sift off, the same End presses take the original's related column from 20 to 40 on the first End, while comments go 40, 60, 80. So the original can load more Up next and Sift cannot, which contradicts works.md line 62. The cause is in the code. youtube.loadMore() in src/content/adapters/youtube.ts (line ~1097) uses comment continuations whenever any 'ytd-comments#comments ytd-comment-thread-renderer' exists. YouTube loads those first 20 threads by itself, so this check does not mean the reader opened Comments.

Skeptic's fix lead: In youtube.loadMore() in src/content/adapters/youtube.ts, choose the comment continuation only when the reader has the Comments section open in Sift, or is at it. Do not decide on whether comment threads exist. If Up next is open and Comments is closed, use the continuation inside '#related' / ytd-watch-next-secondary-results-renderer. That needs a flag from the renderer for which section is open, or a check on Sift's open details. Otherwise take the last continuation outside ytd-comments.
