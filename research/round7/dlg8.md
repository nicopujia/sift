## F700 (medium): Gmail compose: Escape, Sift's Close or a click outside hides the draft only in Sift, and it then comes back as a modal sheet over the next email opened

Found by: T5-amazon-gmail-a11y. Where: http://127.0.0.1:8799/fx/gmail.html (Compose)

Evidence: Original: the compose window is a role=dialog box fixed at the bottom right with no close button. Escape and clicks elsewhere leave it open, and the inbox stays usable beside it. Sift shows it as a modal sheet 'New Message' (aria-modal=true, main.inert=true) with its own Close. I typed To 'a@b' and Subject 'Hi', then pressed Escape, clicked Close, or clicked the backdrop at (1100,700). Each time Sift's sheet goes away and focus returns to Compose, but the page's compose is still open with To 'a@b' and Subject 'Hi'. Sift then shows no draft, and nothing is announced. When I then opened 'Your package was delivered', the URL became #inbox/3, and the 'New Message' sheet came back over the email with main.inert=true. I could not read the email, and a click on 'Back to Inbox' did not reach the page (hash stayed #inbox/3). So you cannot read another email while a draft is open, as you can on the original, and closing the sheet does not do what it does on the page. Clicking Sift's Compose again after hiding replaces the draft on this fixture: the fields are empty. Shots: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-5/research/shots/t5r7-gmail-compose-open.png, /home/nico/projects/sift-test-5/research/shots/t5r7-gmail-compose-backdrop.png, /home/nico/projects/sift-test-5/research/shots/t5r7-gmail-compose-returns.png. Caveat: I could not check what Escape does in real Gmail's compose (no sign-in allowed). The modal-versus-floating difference holds whatever Escape does there.

Repro: cd /home/nico/projects/sift-test-5; SIFT_PORT=9465 SIFT_TAB=x bun scripts/act.ts goto:http://127.0.0.1:8799/fx/gmail.html wait:3000 click:Compose wait:1000 press:a press:@ press:b press:Escape wait:1000 "eval:[!!document.querySelector('sift-root').shadowRoot.querySelector('[role=dialog]'), document.querySelector('#compose-root .agP')?.value]" "click:Your package was delivered" wait:1500 "eval:[document.querySelector('sift-root').shadowRoot.querySelector('.sheet')?.getAttribute('aria-label'), document.querySelector('sift-root').shadowRoot.querySelector('main').inert, location.hash]" prints [false,"a@b"] (Sift's sheet is gone, the page's draft is open), then ["New Message",true,"#inbox/3"] (the sheet is back over the email). Click:Close, or a mouse click outside the sheet, in place of press:Escape gives the same result.

Suspected cause: Sift renders every page dialog as a modal sheet with a Close that only hides it in Sift (a dismissed key). The dismissal is reset when the page navigates, so the page's non-modal compose window (role=dialog, fixed, small, no close button of its own) returns as a modal. A compose window, or any small fixed dialog that does not cover the page, might be better shown as a non-modal panel, with Sift's Close not offered when the page has none. Leads: dialogIn() and blocksPage() in src/content/extract/generic.ts, the Dialog sheet in src/content/ui/app.tsx, and the compose form in src/content/adapters/gmail.ts.

Skeptic: I reproduced this on the fixture three times, once each with Escape, Sift's Close and a backdrop click at (1100,700). Each time Sift's 'New Message' sheet goes away, focus returns to Compose, and the page's compose stays open with To 'a@b' (and Subject 'Hi' in the backdrop run). Nothing is announced. When I then opened 'Your package was delivered', the hash became #inbox/3 and the sheet came back with aria-modal and main.inert=true. Focus went into the To field, which still held a@b, so the email could not be read. Screenshot: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-5/research/shots/skeptic-n0-returns.png. With Sift off, the original behaves differently: Escape and a click outside leave the compose floating. Clicking the email row opens #inbox/3 beside it, and the draft keeps a@b. Turning Sift back on shows the modal sheet over that email. The cause is in the code. composeDialog() in src/content/adapters/gmail.ts returns no `ref`, so closeDialog stores an undefined ref in dismissedRefs. The navigation reset in index.ts (about line 2032: `elOf(dismissedRefs.get(k))` is null) then drops the dismissal. Sift's own rule says a dismissed dialog the page still shows stays dismissed, and this breaks it. The same missing ref sends closeDialog's Escape to document.body instead of the compose box. Two limits on scope. First, the fixture has no close control, but real Gmail has 'Save & close', which the adapter's close selector matches in English. On English Gmail, Sift's Close and Escape would click it and close the page's compose, so the 'hidden only in Sift, then returns' part would likely not happen there. The Spanish label ('Guardar y cerrar') is not in that selector; I did not verify this. Second, the modal-versus-floating difference holds on real Gmail too: in Sift you must close the draft before you can read another email, which the original does not require. Medium severity is reasonable for the target fixture.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/content/adapters/gmail.ts composeDialog(), add `ref: refOf(box)`. The dismissal then survives navigation while the page still shows the box, and closeDialog's Escape reaches the compose box. Better: show the page's floating compose as a non-modal form, either as a block in page.blocks like the inline reply editor() or as a non-modal panel, rather than as page.dialog, so the inbox stays usable beside the draft. Also add the Spanish close labels (e.g. aria-label^='Guardar y cerrar') to the close selector. If only `ref` is added, a dismissed draft is hidden in Sift with no way back, so the non-modal form is the real fix.

## F701 (high, regression): A 'session expired, sign in again to save your changes' modal is hidden when it appears while the reader is idle, and Sign in is then unreachable in Sift

Found by: T6-fixtures. Where: New fixture http://127.0.0.1:8799/fx/r7-sessionsave.html (/home/nico/projects/sift/research/fixtures/r7-sessionsave.html); also r4int-latedialog.html#nag. Code: src/content/extract/generic.ts marketingModal() and the MARKETING regex, added in round 6 (~line 5806 of the merged file)

Evidence: Fresh load, no input, wait 6 s. The page shows its modal 'Your session has expired / Sign in again to save your changes…' with Sign in and ×. Sift shows no sheet and no notice, and no 'Sign in' button exists anywhere in Sift (buttons: Skip to content, Menu, Account, Save draft, Preview, Add a photo, Sift). The user then types and presses Save draft: log 'save blocked', and Sift shows 'Not saved: you are signed out. Sign in again to save.' with no way to sign in short of switching Sift off. ?msg=2 ('Log in again to keep saving your work') is also hidden. The control ?msg=3 ('For your security we ended your session…', which names neither signing in nor saving) is shown as a sheet with Close and Sign in. If the user was typing when the session expired, the sheet is shown. The pre-round-6 build, run in a scratch Chrome on port 9486, shows all three variants as sheets, and shows the r4int-latedialog #nag dialog ('Subscribed / Sign in to save recipes.'). The merged build hides that one too (no sheet 9-14 s after it opens), so the works line 'latedialog #nag opens Subscribed with Close' no longer holds. Shots: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-6/research/shots/r7t6-session-idle-sift.png and /home/nico/projects/sift-test-6/research/shots/r7t6-session-idle-original.png

Same with argument "?msg=2" (hidden) and "?msg=3" (control: shown). Original side by side: bun research/r7t6/session-orig.ts. Pre-round-6 comparison needs the scratch build: bun research/r7t6/pre-session.ts against a Chrome on 9486 built from round6-fixes.diff reversed.

Suspected cause: MARKETING matches '\b(sign in|log in|…)\b.{0,80}\b(save|saving|…)\b', and 'save' there means storing work, not saving money. marketingModal() then hides any late modal with a Close or × button and no field, unless the user's last action opened it.

Skeptic: Reproduced on 3 runs. On r7-sessionsave.html with no input, the result after 8 s was {pageModal:true, siftSheet:null}. Sift had no sheet and no notice. Its buttons were 'Skip to content | Menu | Account | Save draft | Preview | Add a photo | Sift', with no Sign in. After typing and pressing Save draft, the log said 'save blocked' and Sift showed 'Not saved: you are signed out. Sign in again to save.' ?msg=2 was also hidden. The control ?msg=3 was shown as a sheet: 'Your session has expired · Close · For your security… · Sign in', with focus on Close. I tested the MARKETING regex in generic.ts directly. It matches 'Sign in again to save' (msg 1), 'Log in again to keep saving' (msg 2) and 'Sign in to save' (#nag). It does not match msg 3. marketingModal() then passes on the × button with aria-label Close and no field. round6-fixes.diff adds both MARKETING and the marketingModal() check (lines 1203-1232). The only difference between the hidden and shown variants is the text the regex matches, so this is a round-6 regression. I did not rebuild the pre-round-6 Chrome. That conclusion rests on the diff and the msg=3 control. latedialog #nag is hidden on the merged build ('dialog opened' in the log, no sheet after 11 s). That part is arguably intended, since a 'Sign in to save recipes' nag the page raised by itself is noise under the spec. The real defect is the session-expired case. There, the hidden modal holds the only way to sign in without leaving the draft, so saving work is blocked in Sift. High is fair. Shot: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-6/research/shots/skeptic-session-idle.png

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/content/extract/generic.ts, tighten MARKETING (~line 5807). 'save/saving' should count only as money ('save money', 'save \d+%', 'save up to', 'save $/€', 'savings'), not as storing work ('save your changes/work/draft/progress'). Also make marketingModal() return false when the dialog text says the session expired, timed out or signed the user out (/session (has )?(expired|timed out)|signed out|logged out|log in again|sign in again/i).

## F702 (high): 'Continue with Google' is removed when its container's class contains 'social' (e.g. social-login), so a social sign-up or sign-in is not possible in Sift

Found by: T6-fixtures. Where: New fixture http://127.0.0.1:8799/fx/r7-signup.html (/home/nico/projects/sift/research/fixtures/r7-signup.html). Code: src/content/dom.ts:780, the noise class regex, which includes 'social'

Evidence: On r7-signup the original has <div class="social"><button>Continue with Google</button></div>. Sift's buttons and links are 'Skip to content | Menu | Log in | Show | terms | Create account | Sift', with no Continue with Google. With ?cls=alt (the same box renamed) Sift shows it. With ?cls=social-login it is gone again. On r4-login, where the same button is not inside a .social box, Sift keeps it. The pre-round-6 build also drops it on r7-signup, so this is not new.

Repro: cd /home/nico/projects/sift-test-6 && SIFT_PORT=9466 SIFT_TAB=T6-fixtures bun scripts/act.ts goto:http://127.0.0.1:8799/fx/r7-signup.html wait:2500 "eval:[...document.querySelector('sift-root').shadowRoot.querySelectorAll('button,a')].map(b=>b.innerText.trim()).join(' | ')"  then the same with ?cls=alt (it shows) and ?cls=social-login (it is gone).

Suspected cause: The class-name noise list treats any 'social' token as share or follow chrome. Sign-in buttons ('Continue with', 'Sign in with', 'Log in with' + provider) should be exempt.

Skeptic: Reproduced. On r7-signup.html, Sift's controls are 'Skip to content | Menu | Log in | Show | terms | Create account | Sift'. With ?cls=alt, 'Continue with Google' appears. With ?cls=social-login it is gone. I also tried the real-world class names kc-social-providers (Keycloak) and auth0-lock-social-buttons-container (Auth0). Both drop the button too. Cause: NOISE_TOKEN in src/content/dom.ts:780 includes 'social', and the walker in generic.ts (~line 3334) returns early on any NOISE_TOKEN box outside a dialog. Common identity-provider login pages wrap 'Sign in with Google/GitHub/SSO' in such boxes. An account that only has social or SSO sign-in then cannot sign in or sign up in Sift. This is not a regression, but High is fair.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/content/extract/generic.ts around line 3334, before dropping a NOISE_TOKEN box: if the token is only 'social', keep the box when it holds a button or link named like /^(continue|sign in|log in|sign up|register) with\b/i or naming a sign-in provider. Keep it also when it sits in a form or section with a password or email field. Alternatively, drop 'social' from NOISE_TOKEN in src/content/dom.ts and rely on SOCIAL_HOSTS and SHARE_LABEL for share and follow bars.
