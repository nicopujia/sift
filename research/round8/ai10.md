## F805 (medium, regression): Round 7's looser link-word checks let a link the model moved keep words that name a different action: 'Update your email preferences' unsubscribes, 'cancel your subscription' subscribes

Found by: R3-ai-background. Where: src/content/short.ts:785-790 (fits), :389-394 (nearWords), :358-363 and :374 (stemOf in sameWords)

Evidence: Chrome 9469 with the mock, fixture scratchpad/r8ai/fx/nearswap.html. The source sentence is 'To unsubscribe from all emails, <a href=/unsubscribe>use this link</a>, and to update your email preferences, sign in to your account settings…'. The model text is '[Update your email preferences](L0) in your account settings.' Sift's Short version shows '[link] Update your email preferences', and clicking it fires the page's /unsubscribe link (document.body.dataset.clicked = '/unsubscribe'). Offline (r8ai/t/nearswap.ts), '[Change the delivery address](L0)' opens /orders/cancel when the sentence is 'If you want to cancel your order, click here; to change the delivery address, call…'. Offline (r8ai/t/stem.ts), '[cancel your subscription](L0)' is accepted for the 'Subscribe' link (/subscribe): stemOf matches subscription/subscribe on 7 shared letters. The pre-round-7 sameWords rejected all three, because the words share no exact word of 4 or more letters with the link text, so the link went to 'Links in the full text'. This reopens part of F616: the action does something other than its label says. It depends on the model misplacing a link id, as F616 did.

Repro: cd /home/nico/projects/sift-test-9 && bun /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/r8ai/t/nearswap.ts && bun /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/r8ai/t/stem.ts. In Chrome: run r8ai/mock.ts (port 8873, NEARSWAP marker), SIFT_PORT=9469 bun r8ai/settings.ts, then SIFT_PORT=9469 SIFT_TAB=R3-ai-background bun r8ai/outline.ts nearswap.html 5, then bun scripts/act.ts 'click:Update your email preferences' wait:500 'eval:document.body.dataset.clicked'.

Suspected cause: nearWords accepts any words that come from the link's whole sentence (12 words before and after, split only at . ! ?), so the words of another clause in that sentence ('to update your email preferences') pass. The two-links guard covers only sentences with two links. stemOf accepts any two words sharing a long prefix, and opposite actions often share one (subscribe/subscription). A fix could take near words only from the link's own clause (stop at ; , and 'and to'), and require a stem match only for words that are not verbs of another action. Otherwise keep the old exact-word rule for the action word.

Skeptic: Confirmed, medium. This is a regression from round 7. It depends on the model putting a link id on the wrong clause, as F616 did. In Chrome 9469 with the mock, nearswap.html showed '[link] Update your email preferences' in the Short version. A trusted click on it set document.body.dataset.clicked to '/unsubscribe'. On the original page, that link reads 'use this link' and sits in the 'To unsubscribe from all emails' clause. Offline, nearswap.ts accepted both swaps: 'Change the delivery address' opened /orders/cancel, and 'Update your email preferences' opened /unsubscribe. stem.ts accepted '[cancel your subscription](L0)' for the 'Subscribe' link (/subscribe). I ran the same three tests against the pre-round-7 code in /home/nico/projects/sift-r7-base. There, the first two swaps and the stem case lose the link: the words stay as plain text and the link goes to the extra list. Causes: Link.near holds up to 12 words on each side of the link and stops only at sentence ends (short.ts:160-166), so nearWords (short.ts:389-394) accepts words from another clause of the same sentence. stemOf (short.ts:358-363) matches subscription/subscribe on 7 shared letters. One note: the third offline case ('Download a copy of your data' opening /account/delete) passed in pre-round-7 code too, because sameWords accepted the shared 4-letter word 'your'. That part is older and not a regression.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/content/short.ts: (1) when building Link.near (around :160-166), cut 'before' and 'after' at clause breaks (; : , and before 'and to', 'or', 'but'), so near words come only from the link's own clause; (2) in sameWords (around :368-376) and nearWords, do not accept a stem-only match for the action word. Require an exact match there, or reject a stem pair when either word is a known opposite or other action verb (cancel/subscribe, delete/download, unsubscribe/update). Also make sameWords skip stopwords like 'your' and 'this' for its 4-letter rule.

## F806 (medium, regression): Isolating filtered list entries re-runs the whole three-model chain for every sub-batch and forgets the unsettled entries: 3 filtered headlines in 20 cost 28 calls on the first visit, 28 on the second and 11 on the third

Found by: R3-ai-background. Where: src/background/index.ts:605-627 (run), :572-591 (each ask goes through withChain with all three models), :476 (MAX_LINE_CALLS)

Evidence: Chrome 9469 with the mock (a content_filter answer with no text for any batch holding a [FILTER] title). Fixture scratchpad/r8ai/fx/threefilter.html: 20 news headlines, 3 of them flagged. Visit 1 made 28 calls, 24 of them filtered: each filtered sub-batch was tried on mock/lines, then lines-fb, then last, with sizes 20×3, 10×3, 5×3, … Only 9 of the 17 innocent headlines got lines. The other 8, all in the second half, kept their titles on that view, because the 12-ask cap was reached and the rest was 'unsettled'. Visit 2 made 28 more calls (24 filtered), visit 3 made 11 (9 filtered). That is 67 calls for one list of 20, and round 6 made 3. One filtered headline in 6 (onefilter.html) took 15 calls, 12 of them filtered. Once the full batch has been refused by all three models, every sub-batch that still holds the flagged entry is refused by all three again. The cache then keeps a keep-title row (1 day) only for the isolated entries, so the daily expiry starts a new isolation.

Repro: Run r8ai/mock.ts on 8873 and SIFT_PORT=9469 bun r8ai/settings.ts (clears the cache). Then three times: SIFT_PORT=9469 SIFT_TAB=R3-ai-background bun r8ai/watch.ts threefilter.html 20 zzzz; curl -s http://127.0.0.1:8873/log | count the lines entries and filter:true. To see which entries got lines on visit 1: bun scripts/act.ts 'eval:[...document.querySelector("sift-root").shadowRoot.querySelectorAll("a")].map(a=>a.innerText.slice(0,40))'.

Suspected cause: run() calls ask() for every half, and ask() always walks [lines, linesFallback, lastResort]. After a chain-wide final refusal, the halves could be isolated on one model, and the fallbacks asked only for the isolated entry. Entries left when MAX_LINE_CALLS is reached are not remembered for this batch, so the next visit starts again from the top. They could be remembered in the session (or in the cache, briefly), or the cap could count HTTP calls rather than asks.

Skeptic: Confirmed, medium at most. This needs every model in the chain to refuse the batch. In Chrome 9469 with the mock and a cleared cache, threefilter.html made 28 lines calls (24 filtered) on visit 1, 28 (24 filtered) on visit 2 and 11 (9 filtered) on visit 3, the same counts as reported. The visit 1 sequence was 20F x3 models, 10F x3, 5 ok, 5F x3, 3F x3, 2F x3, 1F x3, 1 ok, 1 ok, 2 ok, 10F x3, 5F x3, then the cap. On visit 1, entries 0-4 and 6-9 got lines. Entries 11-15 and 17-19 (8 innocent headlines) kept their titles. onefilter.html took 15 calls (12 filtered), and its revisit made 0 calls. So the restart only happens when the cap is hit. The code matches the claim. In run() (background/index.ts:605-627), each ask() goes through withChain with [lines, linesFallback, lastResort] (:572-575), so MAX_LINE_CALLS = 12 counts asks, not HTTP calls. That allows up to 36 calls per batch, against its comment 'each try may be paid for'. Entries left 'unsettled' at the cap are not remembered, so each visit starts the isolation again. Round 6 (sift-r7-base :581) made one chain walk and cached KEEP_TITLE for all entries. Caveat: with the default chain (Gemini, then GPT-6-luna twice), this needs both providers to filter the same headlines. When only one model is reachable, each ask costs one call, which still means 12 calls per visit.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/background/index.ts doLines run()/ask(): once a whole batch got a final refusal from every model, isolate the halves on the model that refused first. Ask the fallbacks only for an entry that fails alone. Count HTTP calls toward MAX_LINE_CALLS, not asks. Remember the entries still unsettled at the cap, briefly (in pc or a short cache row), so a revisit does not run the whole isolation again.

## F807 (medium): Dropping a stale sentence leaves an abbreviation behind as a fragment: 'Roads north stay closed. U.S. Schools stay shut until Friday', 'Mr. Roads north stay closed'

Found by: R3-ai-background. Where: src/content/short.ts:458-477 (sentences splits after 'U.S.', 'Mr.', 'Dr.'), :480-490 (withoutSentences), :664-670 (stale drop)

Evidence: Chrome 9469 with the mock, fixture scratchpad/r8ai/fx/abbrlive.html. The lede 'U.S. officials said the death toll … rose to 12…' becomes '…rose to 31…' at 6 s. The model text is 'Roads north stay closed. U.S. officials say the storm killed 12 people. Schools stay shut until Friday.' At 9 s and 13 s the Short version shows the new paragraph in full and then 'Roads north stay closed. U.S. Schools stay shut until Friday.' The dangling 'U.S.' now reads as part of the next sentence. Offline (r8ai/t/abbr2.ts, r7ai/t/abbrev.ts), 'Mr. Lopez said the storm killed 12 people. Roads north stay closed.' becomes 'Mr. Roads north stay closed.' and 'Dr. Ana Ruiz said…' becomes 'Dr. Roads north stay closed.' This text shows until the refresh lands (up to REFRESH_MS, 15 s, after the request). Once the refresh limit is reached it stays. After the F705 fix, every gone paragraph's sentences are dropped, so this happens on any edited news paragraph whose summary sentence starts with a title or 'U.S.'.

Repro: Run r8ai/mock.ts on 8873 (ABBR marker) and SIFT_PORT=9469 bun r8ai/settings.ts, then SIFT_PORT=9469 SIFT_TAB=R3-ai-background bun r8ai/watch.ts abbrlive.html 4,9,13 'U\.S\.|Roads|Schools'. Offline: cd /home/nico/projects/sift-test-9 && bun /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/r8ai/t/abbr2.ts

Suspected cause: sentences() ends a sentence at any '.' followed by a space, so 'U.S.' and 'Mr.' become one-token 'sentences'. The stale test skips them (want.size < 2) and keeps them while the sentence they open is dropped. Fix: do not split after a short capitalized abbreviation or initials (Mr, Mrs, Dr, St, Gen, U.S., single letters). Or, when a sentence is dropped, also drop a kept fragment of fewer than 2 tokens right before it.

Skeptic: Confirmed, medium-low. This bug is older than round 7, as claimed. In Chrome 9469 with the mock, abbrlive.html showed 'Roads north stay closed. U.S. officials say the storm killed 12 people. Schools stay shut until Friday.' at 4 s. At 9 s and 13 s, after the lede changed to 31, it showed the new paragraph in full and then 'Roads north stay closed. U.S. Schools stay shut until Friday.' That reads as if U.S. schools are closed. The original page has no such phrase. At 20 s the refresh landed. Offline, abbr2.ts gives 'Mr. Roads north stay closed.' I ran the same test on /home/nico/projects/sift-r7-base and got the same output there. Cause: sentences() (short.ts:458-477) ends a sentence at any '.' followed by a space, so 'U.S.' and 'Mr.' become one-token 'sentences'. The stale test skips them (want.size < 2 in staleAbout) and withoutSentences keeps them, while the sentence they start is dropped. It only happens on the stale-drop path: a paragraph that changed during the visit. It lasts until the refresh, or for good after MAX_REFRESH (4) refreshes (ai.ts:31).

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/content/short.ts sentences(): do not end a sentence after a short abbreviation or initials, such as a capital letter followed by '.', or Mr/Mrs/Ms/Dr/St/Gen/Sen/Rep/No/e.g./i.e. when the next word follows. As a backstop in withoutSentences, when a sentence is dropped, also drop a kept piece of fewer than 2 tokens right before it.
