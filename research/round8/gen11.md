Suspected cause: Unverified. One of the round 7 card or story-list changes in src/content/extract/generic.ts now treats the answers block as a list of story cards. Which answers are on the page decides it: trending works and scoredesc does not.

Skeptic: Confirmed. It is a high-severity regression. I ran 927358?answertab=scoredesc twice on the current build. Both runs printed [69,0,30,'https://stackoverflow.com/posts/927386/revisions']. The cards are titled 'Undo a commit & redo', 'Option 1: git reset --hard', 'How to undo a local commit' and '4 Comments'. trending gives 72/72 and modifieddesc gives 83/83. On 178325 (scoredesc) the original has 42 pre and 5 'Run code snippet'. Sift has 0 and 0, and 30 cards titled '23 Comments', '11 Comments'…, with one titled '1 Comment' that links to learn.jquery.com. On 2003505 I got 68 original pre and 7 in Sift (the claim says 1), across 34 cards. I built the committed tree (HEAD, before the round 7 fixes) into a second Chrome. It gives 69/69 on 927358 and 42/42 on 178325, so this is new. Cause: the only field in each SO answer is the share sheet's hidden, read-only input holding 'https://stackoverflow.com/a/<id>'. The old detectItems counted it as a field (withFields = 30 of 30), and that rule kept answers from becoming cards. The F738 change (copyField plus visible(f) near generic.ts line 2360) no longer counts it, so the 30 answers turn into cards. Test: I added one visible text input to each answer and switched Sift off and on. Sift went back to 69/69 pre. A control run with the same switch and no added inputs stayed at 0.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/content/extract/generic.ts detectItems(), add a direct guard instead of relying on the field count. Return null (walk the members) when most members hold a post body: a pre or blockquote, [itemprop=text] or .s-prose. asCard() already refuses blocks with pre or blockquote. Also narrow the F738 exemption in detectItems so only an embed code (a value starting with '<') is skipped, not a hidden share-link input.

## F857 (medium): HN Search: about 1 in 4 results shows the raw URL as the headline and the real title as a small link

Found by: T4-generic. Where: https://hn.algolia.com/?q=rust (default Popularity sort)

Evidence: 7 of 30 Sift items have a headline that is the URL in parentheses, e.g. '(https://fasterthanli.me/articles/a-half-hour-to-learn-rust)' in bold, then 'A half-hour to learn Rust · 1444 points | selvan | 6 years ago' below. Others: '(http://blog.rust-lang.org/2015/05/15/Rust-1.0.html)' over 'Announcing Rust 1.0', and windows-rs, 'why-i-left-rust', and more. The original's DOM is the same for every story (title link to item?id=, then a URL link). Items with short titles (under about 30 chars) lose the headline to the URL, so the list is harder to scan. Their tab order also differs (URL, title, points, author, time, comments vs title, URL, author, comments). The round 7 tab walk showed the same order, so this is not new. Shot: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-4/research/shots/r8t4-hns-rust.png (items 3 and 4)

Repro: SIFT_PORT=9464 SIFT_TAB=T4-generic bun scripts/act.ts "goto:https://hn.algolia.com/?q=rust" wait:6000 "eval:(()=>{const li=[...document.querySelector('sift-root').shadowRoot.querySelectorAll('li.item')];return [li.length, li.filter(l=>/^\(https?:/.test(l.querySelector('a')?.innerText||'')).length]})()" prints [30,7].

Suspected cause: Unverified. The item-title choice in src/content/extract/generic.ts seems to prefer the longer link text when the real title is short, and then demotes the title link into the meta line.

Skeptic: Confirmed, 30 items and 7 of them with a URL headline, as claimed. Item 3 is '(https://fasterthanli.me/articles/a-half-hour-to-learn-rust)' in bold, with 'A half-hour to learn Rust · 1444 points…' as a small link under it. Item 4 is '(http://blog.rust-lang.org/2015/05/15/Rust-1.0.html)' over 'Announcing Rust 1.0'. The original's .Story_title markup is the same for every story: a[href=item?id] with the title, then a.Story_link with '(url)'. Cause: titleLink() counts a link as a title when its text is at least 70% of its line. For the URL link, the line includes the title link, so a URL at least about 2.3 times as long as the title passes. Discord's 77-character URL against its 40-character title fails, and the half-hour one passes. toItem() then sorts the named links by length, so the URL wins. This is not new, but it puts the wrong headline on about 1 in 4 search results, more than polish. Medium.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/content/extract/generic.ts titleLink(), return false for a link whose whole text is in parentheses '(…)' or is a bare URL. That is the same 'source after a headline' rule side() already applies to the link's neighbors. Another option: in toItem(), drop such links from `named` before the longest-text sort.

Evidence: Sift's main starts with 'No purchase necessary. Ends 10/31/26. See terms.', H1 '$5,000 Cruise Giveaway', 'Royal Caribbean's prize takes you to sea.' and 'Enter here', and only then the dates and travelers controls. Further down, the 'Offers' block keeps the 'Genius Rewards Visa®' credit-card ad. The spec says Sift removes promos and banners. On the original this is the hero banner behind the search box. Shots: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-4/research/shots/r8t4-booking-home.png vs /home/nico/projects/sift-test-4/research/shots/r8t4-booking-home-orig.png

Suspected cause: Unverified. The hero promo (a sweepstakes with its own 'Enter here' call to act) is taken as the page's main heading because the real search box has no heading. Promo detection does not cover a hero banner with a terms line and an 'Enter here' link.

Skeptic: Confirmed on 2 of 2 loads. Sift's main starts with 'No purchase necessary. Ends 10/31/26. See terms.', H1 '$5,000 Cruise Giveaway', 'Royal Caribbean's prize takes you to sea.' and 'Enter here'. Only then come the dates and occupancy controls (dates at y=285). It sits in the hero header behind the search box, and 'Enter here' goes to cruises.booking.com/promotion/sweepstakes.do. The search controls are still on Sift's first screen, so nothing is blocked. But a sweepstakes ad as the page's first content and its H1 goes against the spec's 'removes promos'. Medium, at the low end.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/content/extract/generic.ts promo detection, add sweepstakes wording ('no purchase necessary', 'giveaway', 'sweepstakes', 'enter here' as the only call to action) to PROMO_STRONG. Add 'promotion' and 'sweepstakes' path segments to PROMO_PATH_HEAD. Drop a hero header block that matches, including its H1, and fall back to the site name or the search form for the page heading.
