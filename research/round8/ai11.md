## F821 (medium, regression): On Spanish pages (hl=es), one-line rewrites mix English and Spanish in the same list

Found by: T1-youtube. Where: YouTube watch page 'A continuación' (Up next) and search results, with ?persist_hl=1&hl=es

Evidence: Watch page dQw4w9WgXcQ in Spanish (UI fully Spanish, lang=es-ES). After 30 s, Up next lines 2-4 are English: 'Metallica performs “Fade to Black” live at Sphere on October 8, 2026', 'Culture Club’s “Karma Chameleon” official music video' and 'Song in Latin and Koine Greek by The Skaldic Bard'. The other 16 lines are Spanish ('Remix del tema de Morrigan...', 'Never Gonna Give You Up de Rick Astley, versión de 30 minutos', 'Video oficial de Maneater de Daryl Hall y John Oates', ...). works.md line 64 ('Up next and home one-liners are Spanish') no longer holds. On Spanish search results for 'rick astley', 3 lines are Spanish ('Videoclip de Rick Astley interpretando «Keep Singing»...', 'Video oficial de Rick Astley para «Never Gonna Stop»', 'Videoclip oficial de «Hopelessly»...') and about 30 are English ('Rick Astley’s “Together Forever” official video, 4K remaster', ...), all in one list. Spanish home was fully Spanish. Round 7's merge did not touch the line-language code (doLines, listLang, entryLang), so the cause is older, but the works line fails now. Shots and outlines: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1/research/shots/r8t1-es-watch.png and /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1/research/shots/r8t1-es-search.txt

Repro: cd /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1; SIFT_PORT=9461 SIFT_TAB=T1-youtube bun scripts/act.ts 'goto:https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ&persist_hl=1&hl=es' wait:15000 'click:A continuación' wait:30000 "eval:(()=>{const r=document.querySelector('sift-root').shadowRoot;const d=[...r.querySelectorAll('details')].find(d=>/A continuación/.test(d.querySelector('summary').innerText));return [...d.querySelectorAll('li a')].map(a=>a.innerText.slice(0,80)).join('\n')})()". For search: goto 'https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=rick+astley&persist_hl=1&hl=es', wait 30 s, list main li a. Reset with hl=en afterwards. Results are cached per site, so another profile may show a different mix.

Suspected cause: In doLines in src/background/index.ts, pc.lang is stored only when a batch has 4 or more entries. A smaller first batch (here, three English titles) decides its language alone, and later batches decide again. The cache key includes the language, so lines made in 'en' for one batch and in 'es' for another both come back. Lead: settle one language per page from all its titles, even for a small first batch, and on a page whose lang is es prefer es unless the whole list is clearly another language.

Skeptic: Reproduced twice on watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ&persist_hl=1&hl=es (lang=es-ES, UI fully Spanish). I compared each Up next line with the original's titles. Run 1: lines 2-4 were Spanish rewrites ('Boney M. interpreta «Rasputin»...', 'Metallica interpreta «Fade to Black» en Sphere...'). The other 15 rewrites were English ('Daryl Hall & John Oates perform “Maneater”', 'Michael Jackson performs “Billie Jean” live at Madison Square Garden...'). Even the Japanese titles came out in English ('Hatsune Miku sings the original song “LEGENDS”', 'Ado song “Patient A”'). Run 2 had the same pattern: lines 2-4 Spanish, then 16 English rewrites. The reporter saw the reverse split (mostly Spanish), but the effect is the same: one list in two languages. That breaks the design comment in doLines and listLang, which promise one language for the whole list. The code supports the suspected cause. In doLines (src/background/index.ts lines 492-495), pc.lang is stored only for a batch of 4 or more items. A first batch of 3 decides by itself, and the next batch decides again. The 3-then-rest split I saw fits that. I did not re-check the search page, to save AI calls. I reset hl=en afterwards.

Skeptic's fix lead: In doLines in src/background/index.ts, store pc.lang from the first batch of any size, at least when knownLang(req.lang) is set. Every later batch on the page then reuses that one decision. Also make listLang/entryLang in src/background/text.ts keep the page language for a small sample unless the titles are clearly in another language.

## F824 (high, regression): Headline lines invent details: a quote attributed to the wrong person, and a count changed

Found by: T2-news. Where: https://www.infobae.com/america/ (news-mode line rewrites, made fresh at 21:18 and 21:42 by this build)

Evidence: (1) The original says 'El día que Diego Maradona compró el icónico auto de Rocky: la reacción del vendedor y una frase para la posteridad'. Sift says '…el vendedor reaccionó y Maradona dijo una frase memorable'. In the article, the memorable phrase ('Mi hermano te puede comprar 25 autos de estos…') was said by his brother Lalo, not by Diego. (2) The original says 'Un conductor alcoholizado embistió a un grupo de 30 ciclistas…'. Sift says 'embistió a 30 ciclistas'. The article says about 30 people were riding, and 2 died, 1 was badly hurt and 1 slightly hurt. This breaks works.md line 80 ('Headline lines are faithful… 102 Infobae home pairs'). I checked 90 pairs: /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/r8t2/ib-home-pairs.txt

Repro: SIFT_PORT=9462 SIFT_TAB=T2-news bun scripts/act.ts goto:https://www.infobae.com/america/ wait:30000 'eval:[...document.querySelector("sift-root").shadowRoot.querySelectorAll("a")].filter(a=>/Maradona compró|30 ciclistas/.test(a.innerText)).map(a=>a.innerText+" | "+a.href)'. Compare each line with its title in the original and with the article text, for example goto: the Maradona URL and search the body for 'frase'. The lines are cached, so a reload shows the same text.

Suspected cause: The v9 headline rule says a teaser 'is answered in the story… If the context gives the answer, say it'. With only the teaser, the model guesses who said the phrase. Dropping 'un grupo de' breaks the existing rule to keep the words that set the relation.

Skeptic: Partly confirmed, as medium rather than high. Maradona: the Infobae /america line reads 'Diego Maradona compró el icónico auto de Rocky; el vendedor reaccionó y Maradona dijo una frase memorable'. The article says Lalo said 'Mi hermano te puede comprar 25 autos de estos…' and that 'Mi hermano no se olvidó más de esa frase'. So the line gives Diego a phrase his brother said, which is a made-up fact. Not confirmed: (a) '30 ciclistas'. The article itself says the car 'las embistió' (the ~30 riders), so dropping 'un grupo de' changes little. (b) 'Made fresh by this build' and the regression claim. The cached Maradona line was written at 18:26:39, before the 21:07 dist build. The cache-hit path in doLines re-serves it with only keepQuotes, so this build shows it, but I cannot say this build would write it again.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/background/index.ts NEWS_SYSTEM, add to the teaser rule (line ~99): never name who said, did or chose something the title leaves unnamed. In doLines, give lines from the cache the same checks as new ones (claimsNoAnswer, META), and consider bumping PROMPT_VERSION so pre-fix lines are not re-served.

## F825 (medium): Infobae Short version puts an invented one-word quote in Trump's mouth

Found by: T2-news. Where: https://www.infobae.com/estados-unidos/2026/10/26/donald-trump-dijo-que-las-elecciones-en-brasil-estuvieron-amanadas/ (Short version)

Evidence: The Short version opens 'Donald Trump calificó de “fraudulentas” las elecciones de Brasil, pero no presentó pruebas.' The word 'fraudulentas' does not appear in the full text. Trump's quoted words there are 'parece que es otro fraude electoral' and 'amañadas'. The other quotes in the Short version ('corrupto', 'impresión terrible', 'amañadas', 'muy extraña', 'indiscutibles') all appear in the full text. Two cache entries (21:42:04, 21:43:26) both start with this sentence. Word ratio 0.42; no new numbers.

Repro: SIFT_PORT=9462 SIFT_TAB=T2-news bun scripts/act.ts goto:<url> wait:15000 'eval:document.querySelector("sift-root").shadowRoot.querySelector("article p, main p").innerText', then click:Ver texto completo wait:2000 'eval:/fraudulentas/.test(document.querySelector("sift-root").shadowRoot.querySelector(".sift").innerText)' gives false.

Suspected cause: keepQuotes puts back changed quotes but does not remove a quoted word that the source never has.

Skeptic: Reproduced. The Short version's first paragraph is 'Donald Trump calificó de “fraudulentas” las elecciones de Brasil, pero no presentó pruebas.' In the full text, /fraudulentas/ is false. The source says '“fraude”' and 'parece que es otro fraude electoral' and 'amañadas'. The cache holds two entries written after the build (21:42:04 and 21:43:26), both starting with “fraudulentas”. The cause is in code: keepQuotes in src/background/text.ts skips a quote shorter than 3 words ('small … continue'). An invented one-word quote therefore keeps its quote marks, while a longer unmatched quote loses them. Here the meaning is close (fraude vs fraudulentas), but the gap lets any 1-2 word invented quote through as the speaker's own words.

Skeptic's fix lead: In keepQuotes (src/background/text.ts), check a small quote too: if it is not verbatim in the source (the verbatim(q.inner, words, tight) test), drop its quote marks as the unmatched branch already does. Do not skip it with 'continue'.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/background/index.ts doCondense, base the key on stable text: drop toolbar, count and reading-time lines (better, keep them out of the article blocks in generic.ts), or key on the ShortMeta fingerprint of the longer paragraphs. Strip a leading meta or preamble paragraph (one that talks about 'el texto fuente', 'the source text', word counts or [[B…]]) before keepQuotes and before saving.

## F833 (medium): Long Fox and Infobae articles get no Short version, and 'no short version' is saved for 30 days

Found by: T2-news. Where: https://www.foxnews.com/world/nearly-1000-arrested-across-france-police-find-potential-weapons-protestors ; https://www.infobae.com/estados-unidos/2026/10/26/un-conductor-alcoholizado-embistio-a-un-grupo-de-30-ciclistas-en-un-paseo-de-halloween-dejo-dos-muertos-y-arrastro-mas-de-un-kilometro-a-una-victima/

Evidence: The Fox article (round 7 repro used its 'Show full text') had no Short version toggle after 80 s, nor on a later load. The Infobae article (543 words) had none either. The extension cache holds two '\u0000no-short' markers written at 21:24:46 and 21:41:27, just after those loads, both with no expiry (kept 30 days). Other articles of similar length got one (Infobae Trump 641 words, 0.42).

Repro: SIFT_PORT=9462 SIFT_TAB=T2-news bun scripts/act.ts goto:<fox url> wait:60000 'eval:[...document.querySelector("sift-root").shadowRoot.querySelectorAll("button")].map(b=>b.innerText)' has no 'Show full text'. Then list IndexedDB sift/kv entries whose value is '\u0000no-short' and their exp fields.

Suspected cause: doCondense saves NO_SHORT for good when the result is tooLong, whichever model wrote it. With the fast models out of credit, a long reply from the slow fallback blocks the Short version for 30 days. The one-day rule only covers an incomplete fallback reply.

Skeptic: Reproduced. The Fox France article (no 'Show full text' after 30 s) and the Infobae cyclists article (no 'Ver texto completo' after 45 s) both show no Short version. No new condense entry was written, so the answer came from the cache. sift/kv holds no-short markers written at 21:24:46 and 21:41:27 with exp null (kept 30 days), and my La Nación load added a third at 22:42:45. The code matches the claim. In doCondense, the catch path keeps a fallback's final error for only a day ('main ? save : cacheSet(…, DAY)'). But line 722, 'save(pc, req, key, tooLong(all) ? NO_SHORT : all)', keeps a too-long result for 30 days whichever model wrote it. With the main model out of credit, one long fallback reply blocks the article for a month. Not verified: which model wrote these three markers.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/background/index.ts doCondense, record which chain link produced `all` (withChain already knows). Save NO_SHORT for tooLong without a TTL only when the main condense model wrote it, and otherwise for at most a day, as the catch path does.
