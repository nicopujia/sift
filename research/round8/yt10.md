## F820 (medium, regression): With Up next open, Tab keeps loading more Up next, so Comments takes 188 Tabs to reach

Found by: T1-youtube. Where: YouTube watch page (watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ), Up next section open, Comments closed, keyboard only

Evidence: I loaded the page fresh, focused the Up next summary, pressed Enter, then pressed Tab with 150 ms between presses (scratchpad t1r8/tabgrow.ts). Up next grew as focus moved through it: 40 items after 20 Tabs, 60 after 40, 100 after 60, 139 after 100, 157 after 140, 175 after 160 and 187 after 180. The Comments summary was reached only after 188 Tabs, once YouTube ran out of related videos. Before that, in a kb.ts walk, 250 Tabs from the Up next summary never reached Comments: Up next ended at 178 items, the same as the original's 178. In round 7 (works.md line 61, Tab order 'Up next, Comments, Sort, reply buttons'), Up next stayed at 20, so Comments came about 21 Tabs after it. In the original, ytd-comments comes before #secondary #related in the DOM (compareDocumentPosition: commentsBeforeRelated=true), so a keyboard user reaches the comments first. The only way around it is to Shift+Tab back to the Up next summary and close the section.

Repro: cd /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1; SIFT_PORT=9461 ./scripts/chrome.sh start; SIFT_PORT=9461 SIFT_TAB=T1-youtube bun /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/t1r8/tabgrow.ts (it loads watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ, opens Up next with Enter, and presses Tab up to 200 times, printing the Up next size every 20 Tabs and the Tab count when the Comments summary gets focus).

Suspected cause: This comes from the F722 fix. youtube.loadMore() in src/content/adapters/youtube.ts now uses the Up next continuation whenever Comments is closed (store sec:comments !== true). Focus moving to the last Up next link scrolls Sift's view to its end, and that seems to trigger loadMore, so each pass adds about 20 more before focus can leave the section. Leads: do not load more because of a focus move (only on End or a scroll by the reader), or let Up next load more through an explicit 'More' control, or put Comments before Up next, as the original's order does.

Skeptic: Reproduced 3 times with the reporter's tabgrow.ts on my own tab: fresh watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ, Up next opened with Enter, Tab every 150 ms. Up next grew as focus moved through it in every run. Two runs needed 191 and 189 Tabs to reach the Comments summary, with Up next growing 20 to 40, 60, 100, 120, 139, 178, then 190 or 188. One run stopped growing at 40 and reached Comments after 41 Tabs. The code matches: the host scroll listener in src/content/index.ts (around lines 2324-2346) calls loadMore() whenever Sift's view is within 1.5 screens of its end. The reader does not have to have scrolled. With Comments closed, youtube.loadMore() (src/content/adapters/youtube.ts, around line 1102) picks the Up next continuation. So each focus move onto the last links loads 20 more. Up next and Comments both start collapsed, so this hits a keyboard user who opened Up next. They cannot Tab past it until YouTube runs out of related videos. Medium fits. I did not check whether this is a regression.

Skeptic's fix lead: In the host scroll listener in src/content/index.ts, do not call loadMore() for a scroll caused by focus. Skip it when the last input was Tab or Shift+Tab, or when the scroll came right after a focusin, and keep it for wheel, touch, End, PageDown and scrollbar scrolling. Or give the Up next items block an explicit 'more' control (Block.more) built in the watch() code of src/content/adapters/youtube.ts, and stop youtube.loadMore() from loading Up next on scroll alone.

Found by: T1-youtube. Where: YouTube home, Topics > Music (also any home view that has ytd-rich-shelf-renderer video shelves)

Suspected cause: home() in src/content/adapters/youtube.ts (lines 333-349) reads ytd-rich-section-renderer children only for Shorts (shortsIn). Video lockups inside a ytd-rich-shelf-renderer are never read. Lead: read the shelf's yt-lockup-view-model items as a titled list or a folded section under the shelf's own title, as is done for Shorts.

Skeptic: Reproduced on youtube.com home with Topics > Music. The original shows two visible ytd-rich-shelf-renderer shelves: 'Top music videos in your region' (Post Malone - Sunflower, LISA - Tarantino, Golden lyric video) and 'New to you' (Lady Gaga/Bruno Mars - Die With A Smile, Scooby Doo Pa Pa, Shakira - Dai Dai). The reporter's 'siftHasWham=false' did not hold for me, because a Wham video was in the grid on this load. The core claim still holds: every video in the shelves is dropped. In home() in src/content/adapters/youtube.ts (lines 337-349), the ytd-rich-section-renderer branch only calls shortsIn, so a shelf of video lockups is never read. Losing about 6 of 27 videos from the list view is medium.

Skeptic's fix lead: In home() in src/content/adapters/youtube.ts, in the ytd-rich-section-renderer branch, also read the shelf's ytd-rich-item-renderer yt-lockup-view-model items with fromLockup. Skip isAd and dedupe by href with seen. Add them as a collapsed section titled with the shelf's #title, or append them to the feed list. Shorts keep using shortsIn.

## F721 (still open, not_fixed; original repro in /home/nico/projects/sift/research/round7/*.md)

Recheck by T1-youtube: The Sift-only drop is fixed: in 0 of 23 runs did Sift's dialog vanish while the original's popup stayed open. The user still sees the prompt close by itself, though. I clicked Save or Like 1-3 s after the button appeared on a fresh load (scratchpad t1r8/vanish.ts, EXTRAS=1000,1500,...). The original's tp-yt-iron-dropdown closed on its own 0.3-3.1 s after opening, and Sift's dialog followed it 0-2.3 s later. This happened in 18 of 23 runs: Save 3/6, 5/6, 4/4 and 3/3, Like 3/4, and once the Like prompt closed at 339 ms before Sift ever showed it. With Sift switched off (Alt+Shift+S, then t1r8/origvanish.ts with the same delays), the original's prompt stayed open for the full 8 s in 11 of 11 runs (Save 7, Like 4). Cause, found by logging events (t1r8/vanish3.ts): an untrusted click on 'yt-icon-button#guide-button' in ytd-masthead (at 83, 613, 1582 and 2924 ms) is followed at once by 'iron-overlay-canceled(click on yt-icon-button)' on the dropdown, then 'yt-popup-closed(canceled=true)'. That click comes from renderGuide() in src/content/adapters/youtube.ts (lines 64-98). It clicks YouTube's guide button 4 s plus idle time after load, and clicks it again to close the guide. To YouTube's open popup that is a click outside, so the popup closes. Fix lead: in renderGuide, skip or postpone the click while anything in ytd-popup-container is open (tp-yt-iron-dropdown or tp-yt-paper-dialog checkVisibility()), or render the guide only when the reader opens Menu.
