## F870 (high): Google Maps: focusing the search field sometimes opens a fake modal dialog (Google's header bar or the filter chips) that takes focus and swallows typing

Found by: T3-tools. Where: https://www.google.com/maps/search/coffee/@37.7955,-122.4035,15z?hl=en (Sift's top-bar search field)

Evidence: Under machine load (load average 15-25) or 4x CPU throttling, clicking or Shift+Tabbing into Sift's search field sometimes made Sift show a modal sheet (role=dialog, aria-label 'Results'). It held 'Close | Google apps | Sign in' (the page's #gb Google bar, in a fixed div) or 'Close | Price | Rating | Hours | All filters', and focus moved to its Close. Rates: 3 of 6 mouse trials at x4 throttle; 7 of about 35 unthrottled trials under load; 0 of 14 at load average 1; 0 of 4 at x4 when the field was not focused (control). Typing trace (scratchpad r8t3/typetrace.ts): after 'T', focus went to button(Close), so 'oit Tower' was lost and the page field kept 'T'. Other runs ended with 'Tower' or 'CoTower'. The sheet stayed open for over a minute until closed, greying the map. The original shows no dialog. Round 7 ran the same keyboard flow under load and did not report this. When it does not happen, the keyboard and mouse flows work. Shot: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-3/research/shots/R8-T3-gmaps-layer.png

Repro: cd /home/nico/projects/sift-test-3; export SIFT_PORT=9463 SIFT_TAB=T3-tools; S=/tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad; bun $S/r8t3/gdlg.ts mouse 6 6000 4   (loads the coffee search 6 times with 4x CPU throttling, real-clicks Sift's search field, and watches 6 s for a Sift .layer; prints e.g. 'mouse trial 3: 3017 ms after focus: Close | Google apps | Sign in'). Control: bun $S/r8t3/gdlg.ts none 4 6000 4 prints 0/4. Typing loss: bun scripts/act.ts "goto:https://www.google.com/maps/search/coffee/@37.7955,-122.4035,15z?hl=en" wait:5500 press:Shift+Tab press:Shift+Tab press:Control+a; bun $S/r8t3/typetrace.ts 0 'Coit Tower'  (repeat while the machine is busy; failures show focus=button(Close)).

Suspected cause: Sift's popup or dialog finder (or the new field-panel logic around fieldList/fieldPanel/panelList in src/content/index.ts) takes a page container near the focused search box for an opened dialog. This happens while Google is still settling after the focus: the #gb Google bar in a fixed header, or the filter chip bar. Sift then shows it as a modal and moves focus into it.

Skeptic: Reproduced. I used a fresh tab (SIFT_TAB=skeptic) with Google Maps coffee search, 1280x900, and 4x CPU throttle. First run of gdlg.ts with mouse focus: 5 of 6 trials showed a Sift .layer reading 'Close | Google apps | Sign in' 0.6 to 3.7 s after focus. A second run gave 1 of 5 mouse trials and 3 of 5 keyboard (Shift+Tab) trials. Controls showed nothing: no focus at 4x was 0 of 4, and unthrottled at load average 1 was 0 of 4. I also ran my own typing trace (gtype.ts: click, then type 'Coit Tower' key by key). In 1 of 5 trials the layer appeared right after the click. Focus ended on button(Close), the page field ended as 'coTowerffee' so 'Coit ' was lost, and the sheet then showed 'Close | Price | Rating | Hours | All filters' over a greyed page. Original control (Sift off, 4x): the same typing went into the field, Google showed only its suggestion list, and there was no dialog. It is intermittent and only shows under CPU pressure. When it hits, it is a real input-loss bug and is caused by Sift. Note: one keyboard run I first made had Sift switched off by an earlier control; I threw it away and reran it with Sift on.

Skeptic's fix lead: src/content/extract/generic.ts findPopup()/popupLike(): do not take a positioned container as a popup the action opened when it was on screen before the action. Record visible fixed/absolute boxes when arming, beyond armShown, which only holds what Sift showed. Also reject it when its controls are already in Sift's page view (the chip bar) or when it is the site's account bar (#gb, which has a 'Google apps'/'Sign in' link). In src/content/index.ts, do not move focus into a newly opened sheet while the user is typing in a field they just entered.

## F871 (high): MDN compatibility 'Settings' opens the page's own modal over Sift, and mouse clicks no longer work anywhere (its buttons, Sift's links, the Sift switch)

Found by: T3-tools. Where: https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/JavaScript/Reference/Global_Objects/String/toUpperCase (Browser compatibility > Settings); likely every MDN page with a compat table

Evidence: Clicking Sift's 'Settings' button opens MDN's native 'Browser compatibility settings' modal (a dialog in mdn-compat-table-lazy's shadow root). It is drawn on top of Sift, and Sift shows no sheet of its own. A real click on its Cancel (817,692) leaves it open (open dialogs: 1; elementFromPoint is mdn-compat-table-lazy). A click on the Bun chip's × changes nothing. While it is open, a click on a Sift link (String.prototype.toLowerCase()) does nothing, and a click on the Sift switch does nothing. Tab moves focus from the modal's close button to <body>. Only Escape closes it. Sift's yellow banner shows a raw text dump: 'Browser compatibility settings Exit modal Select the browsers to show in compatibility tables. Your selection is saved in this browser. Desktop Chrome Edge Firefox Safari Internet Explorer Opera Mobil…'. On the original (Sift off), the same real clicks open the modal, and Cancel closes it (open dialogs 0). Shots: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-3/research/shots/R8-T3-mdn-settings-sift.png, /home/nico/projects/sift-test-3/research/shots/R8-T3-mdn-compat-orig.png

Repro: cd /home/nico/projects/sift-test-3; export SIFT_PORT=9463 SIFT_TAB=T3-tools; S=/tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad; bun scripts/act.ts goto:https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/JavaScript/Reference/Global_Objects/String/toUpperCase wait:9000; bun $S/r8t3/mdnsettings.ts Cancel   (prints 'after Settings click: open dialogs: 1' and 'real click on 'Cancel' ... open dialogs: 1'); bun scripts/act.ts press:Escape  (closes it). Original control: bun scripts/act.ts press:Alt+Shift+S; bun $S/r8t3/mdnsettings-orig.ts  (after Cancel: open dialogs: 0); bun scripts/act.ts press:Alt+Shift+S.

Suspected cause: The page opens a showModal() dialog inside nested shadow roots. Sift's dialog detection does not see it, so it is not mirrored in Sift's sheet. The dialog enters the top layer above Sift's popover, but the shield keeps the page inert, so its controls get no clicks, and its backdrop covers Sift.

Skeptic: Reproduced twice on String/toUpperCase. A real click on Sift's Settings opens MDN's native modal over Sift (open dialogs: 1). Real clicks on its Cancel (817,692) and Save (882,692) leave it open. On the original (Sift off, Settings scrolled into view, real clicks) Cancel closes it (open dialogs: 0). Cause, checked in the DOM: the dialog sits in mdn-modal's shadow root, but Cancel, Save, the browser chips and the footer are mdn-modal's slotted light-DOM children. The Cancel mdn-button is not inside the dialog per dialog.contains(), and its closest [inert] is a footer marked data-sift-inert. Sift's shield exempts only the dialog and its ancestors, so the slotted controls stay inert, and no pointer or click event reached Cancel. Two corrections to the report. First, the modal's own close X (898,199, inside the shadow dialog) does close it, so Escape is not the only way out. Second, Sift's links and switch not working while a page modal is open is the intended design (index.ts: a page modal blocks everything else, as on the original). The real defect is that the modal's own main controls do nothing.

Skeptic's fix lead: src/content/shield.ts desired(): do not mark a child whose assigned slot (following assignedSlot up the chain) lies inside an exempted element (composed-tree containment). Or, in index.ts refreshModals()/exempt(), exempt the shadow host that holds the modal dialog (mdn-modal), so its slotted content stays usable.
