## F308 [low] One-liners on a Spanish page mix English and Spanish for English titles
- **where**: YouTube home with ?persist_hl=1&hl=es (document lang es-ES)
- **evidence**: Most English titles get Spanish one-liners ('Crazy Frog - Axel F (Official Video)' becomes 'Video oficial de “Axel F” de Crazy Frog'; 'Cute Lamb Needs Attention' becomes 'Un cordero necesita atención'). These are the same strings the English home showed earlier. Once, on the English home just after the Spanish visit, I saw the reverse: 'Rick Astley Plays Soccer' shown as 'Rick Astley juega al fútbol'. A fresh English reload did not repeat it. Outline: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1/research/shots/R4T1-home-es.txt
- **repro**: SIFT_PORT=9461 SIFT_TAB=T1-youtube. Run bun scripts/visit.ts https://www.youtube.com/ en --wait 30000, then bun scripts/visit.ts "https://www.youtube.com/?persist_hl=1&hl=es" es --wait 30000. List the li a.title texts that are English while lang is es-ES. Reset with ?persist_hl=1&hl=en.
- **suspectedCause**: background/index.ts doLines keys the cache and the requested language on entryLang(title, page.lang): a reliably detected English title gets lang 'en' whatever the page language. Whether a title counts as 'reliable' depends on its length, which gives the mix.
- **verdict**: Reproduced on the current build (after the reinstall). On ?persist_hl=1&hl=es (lang es-ES), most lines are Spanish ('Rick Astley bebe agua', 'Escenas CGI de Bob Esponja', 'Video titulado «Butterball Downstairs»'). One is the same English one-liner the English home shows: 'Rick Astley performs “Take Me to Your Heart” in a remastered video'. A few other English rows look like raw titles kept as names. This comes from entryLang() in background/text.ts: for a same-script title it returns the title's language when it is reliably detected and has enough function words. So the mix is a side effect of a per-title policy, not a random fault. Severity is low.
- **fix**: In src/background/index.ts doLines (and entryLang's use there), pick one policy per page. Either always write list one-liners in page.lang when the page language is known, or keep the title's language for every entry. Do not decide per title by detection reliability.

## F395 [high] The Short version is never refreshed when the article changes by under ~30%: new sections, edited facts and updated numbers are missing from the default view
- **where**: src/content/short.ts:145 (normWords drops digits), :234-240 (sameArticle: 70% of long paragraphs and ±30% words), :387-389 (only dropped blocks and headings are put back, never prose); src/content/ai.ts:236 (no new request when sameArticle); src/content/ui/app.tsx:500

At 12 s and 16 s Sift still shows the same 8-line Short version, with no 'stadium'/'garden' text and no 'covers only the first part' note. 'Show full text' shows the new section. Offline: scratch/t2.ts gives sameArticle true for 160→200 words, and the plan has no 'stadium'/'garden'/'What comes next'. scratch/t3.ts confirms processAi sends no second request after growth.

The cause is as stated. normWords drops digits, sameArticle accepts 70% overlap and ±30% words, ai.ts:236 skips any new request, and planShort puts back only blocks and headings.
