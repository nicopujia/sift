Valley floods update [TOLL]

Officials said the death toll rose to 12 on Monday after rescuers reached the flooded villages north of the river and searched the collapsed houses.

Roads to the north of the valley remain closed, and the regional government asked drivers to avoid the mountain pass until engineers inspect the bridges.

Schools across the district will stay shut until Friday, and families can collect food parcels at the sports hall next to the old station every morning.

The weather service said more rain is expected on Wednesday night, with up to forty millimetres in the hills and strong winds along the coast.

Volunteers from three towns have joined the rescue teams, and the red cross opened two shelters for people who lost their homes in the floods.

The prime minister is expected to visit the area on Thursday and has promised emergency funds for repairs to homes, farms and small businesses.

Residents who need help can call the regional hotline, which is open day and night, or visit the town hall where staff are taking requests.
