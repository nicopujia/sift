Cup final sets a record [FINALLIVE]

The cup final on Saturday night became the most watched football match of the year in the country, after a late goal from the home side's captain decided a tense game in the last minute of extra time.

The final drew 9 million viewers.

The broadcaster said the audience peaked during the penalty that was saved in the second half, and that streams on its app also set a record, with more people watching on phones than ever before.

Advertisers paid about twice the usual price for the breaks around the match, which kicked off at 8 pm and ended almost three hours later, after the trophy was handed to the winning team.

The league plans to move next year's final to a Sunday afternoon, so that families with young children can watch it, and it will talk with the clubs about the change in the coming weeks.

Ticket prices for next year's final will stay the same, and fans who held a season ticket this year can buy two seats each before the general sale opens in the spring.
