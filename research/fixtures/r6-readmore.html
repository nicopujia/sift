Rye sourdough: reviews

4.6 out of 5 (3 reviews)

★★★★★ Best rye in town

Ana · October 20, 2026

Dense, sour and moist, exactly like the rye my grandmother bought in Riga. It keeps for a full week in a paper bag, and toasts beautifully. I slice it thin and eat it with butter and smoked fish. The crust is thick but not hard. One loaf lasts our family of three about five days. Note that the loaf is smaller than it looks in the photo, about 700 grams, so order two if you have guests.

★★★★☆ Good, but salty

Ben · October 18, 2026

Lovely texture and a deep flavor from the long ferment, but a little too salty for me. My partner liked it a lot. Delivery came on time and the bread was still warm. I would buy it again but ask the bakery to use less salt, which they said they can do on request if you add a note at checkout.

★★★★★ Perfect for open sandwiches

Chen · October 15, 2026

Holds together when sliced thin, which is what you want for smørrebrød. Not gluten-free: rye contains gluten, so people with celiac disease should not eat it, as the bakery clearly says on the label.
