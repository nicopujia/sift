The rider's song

The chain is oiled before the light, the road is wet and running far,
and every turn remembers where the hands that steered it are.

Ride on, ride on, the river sings, the bridge is waiting by the shore,
ride on, ride on, the morning brings the road we have not ridden before.

My father rode this road at six, his tyres were thin, his coat was too,
he left the bike for me to fix and left the road for me to do.

Ride on, ride on, the river sings, the bridge is waiting by the shore,
ride on, ride on, the morning brings the road we have not ridden before.

And now the bike is mine to mend, the road is long, the hills are high,
I learn the road has no real end, I learn to let the years go by.

Ride on, ride on, the river sings, the bridge is waiting by the shore,
ride on, ride on, the morning brings the road we have not ridden before.
