Bus line works [LINKBOLD]

The council approved the new bus line on Monday after a long debate about costs, routes and the timetable in the town hall that went on until late at night.

Residents can open the route map to see every stop, or cancel their parking permit before the works on Elm Street start next spring.

Drivers must not park on Elm Street during the three weeks of works, because the council will tow every car left there and charge the owner for the towing.

The mayor said the line would cost two million euros and would be paid by a regional grant over five years, with no new local taxes for residents or shops.

The first buses will run every twenty minutes from six in the morning to eleven at night, and every forty minutes on Sundays and public holidays across the valley.

The council will review the line after six months and may add stops near the new school and the clinic if enough residents ask for them at the public meetings.
