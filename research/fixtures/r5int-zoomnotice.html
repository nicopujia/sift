Contact the bakery

We bake gluten-free bread every morning in a dedicated kitchen. Nothing with wheat, barley or rye ever comes through our doors, so the loaves are safe for people with celiac disease.

Our sourdough uses a starter made from brown rice flour. It rises for twenty hours before it is baked, which gives it a deep flavor and a crisp crust.

We also make sandwich loaves, seeded rolls, focaccia with rosemary, and a cinnamon swirl on weekends. Everything is labeled with its ingredients.

Orders for parties need two days of notice. We can deliver within the city for a small fee, or you can pick up at the counter from seven in the morning.

Questions about allergens other than gluten are welcome. We keep a list of every ingredient and its supplier, and we update it whenever a supplier changes.

Send us a message with the form below and we will answer within one working day. For urgent questions about an order for today, please call the shop.
