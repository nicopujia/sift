Why does my rye starter smell like nail polish?

My rye starter is ten days old. Since yesterday it smells sharply of acetone. I feed it once a day with equal weights of whole rye flour and water, and keep it at about 24 degrees. Is it ruined, or is this a normal stage?

It is hungry. Acetone means the yeast ran out of food. Feed it twice a day for a few days. – Ana

Try a cooler spot too; 24 degrees is warm for a once-a-day feeding. – Ben

Totally normal at day ten. Mine did the same and settled down by day fourteen. – Chen
