President Lula won Brazil election 'by WhatsApp' with cunning online campaign

President Lula's election victory in Brazil was based on a cunning online campaign which won over millions of voters, and it could hold lessons for left-wing parties in the United Kingdom and in Europe.

Dr Matthew Richmond, a lecturer in political geography at Newcastle University and electoral analyst, said the 80-year-old man relied on trusted allies to turn around his first-round defeat.

Each supporter was asked to contact three others to back the Lula campaign, and many of them did so within a few days of the first round.
