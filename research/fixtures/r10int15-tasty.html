Soft Oat Cookies

Jump to Recipe

These oat cookies are soft in the middle and crisp at the edges. We tested them a dozen times so you do not have to, and the trick is to take them out before they look done.

Why these work

Melted butter makes them chewy, and a short rest in the fridge keeps them from spreading too far on the tray while they bake.

The Softest Oat Cookies

Soft oat cookies with a crisp edge, ready in twenty minutes.

Ingredients

Units
  • 8 tablespoons of salted butter
  • 1/2 cup white sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 1 egg
  • 1 1/2 cups rolled oats (5 ounces)

Instructions

  1. Melt the butter and beat it with the sugar.
  2. Add the vanilla and the egg, then the oats.
  3. Bake for 9 to 11 minutes.
