Delivery guide

Every order ships from our warehouse within two working days. Couriers deliver on weekdays between eight in the morning and six in the evening, and you get a message with a time window on the morning of delivery. If nobody is home, the courier leaves a card and tries again the next working day.

Large items such as furniture travel with a two-person crew, who carry the item to the room you choose and take the packaging away. Small parcels can go to a pickup point instead, where they wait for fourteen days before they come back to us.

Below are the questions customers ask most often about changing a delivery after the order is placed, with the steps to follow in your account.

How do I change the delivery address after I have already placed my order?

Open Your orders, choose the order, and press Change address before it ships.

Can I move my delivery to another day after the courier has already picked it up?

Yes. Use the link in the courier message to pick another weekday.
