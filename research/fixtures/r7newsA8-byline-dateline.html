Lula vows no return to dictatorship after election win

Leaders and governments worldwide congratulate Lula on a dramatic comeback seen as an important signal to the rest of Latin America.

Tom Phillips in São Paulo and Tiago Rogero in Brasília
Mon 26 Oct 2026 15.51 GMTLast modified on Mon 26 Oct 2026 20.19 GMT

Nearly 1,000 people were arrested on Saturday as nationwide protests resumed, the interior minister told reporters in the capital.

Earlier in the day, police officers searched protesters and arrested people suspected of carrying potential weapons, including fireworks, and ball bearings.

Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva promised that dictatorship and fascism would never return after defeating his far-right opponent in a historic fourth election victory.

“This is God’s work – and it’s thanks to all of you,” Lula told tens of thousands of supporters who had gathered in central São Paulo in the early hours of Monday.
