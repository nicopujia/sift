City services

Renew your parking permit [BOLD]

To renew your permit, fill in the online form and upload a recent photo and a copy of your proof of address, then wait for the email that confirms it. Most renewals are checked by a clerk within two working days of the request.

Do not pay the processing fee a second time if the payment page shows an error: the bank charges it again and refunds take up to sixty days. The city cannot speed up a refund once the bank has charged the fee twice.

The office answers questions by phone on weekdays between nine and five, and it is closed on public holidays and on the last Friday of each month. Calls are busiest on Monday mornings, so the office asks people to call later in the week.

Permits renewed online arrive by mail within ten working days; if yours has not arrived after three weeks, call the office with your reference number. A temporary permit can be printed from the email in the meantime.

You can also renew in person at any branch, but you must book an appointment first, and walk-in visits are no longer accepted at any branch. Bring the same documents as for the online form, and a card to pay the fee.
