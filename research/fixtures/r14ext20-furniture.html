City council approves the new riverside park after a long debate

Jane Doe John Roe
EXAMPLE NEWS
Updated Oct. 30, 2026, 2:04 p.m. ET

The city council voted on Monday to approve a new riverside park on the old rail yards, ending a debate that lasted almost two years and split the neighborhood into two loud camps.

Supporters said the park would bring shade, a playground and a path along the water. Opponents worried about the cost, which the city puts at 42 million dollars over five years.

The first phase starts in spring. It covers the path and the playground. The second phase adds a boat launch and a small market hall, if the budget allows it.

The park opens to the public in two years, the council said, once the path and the playground are done and the soil on the old rail yards is clean.
