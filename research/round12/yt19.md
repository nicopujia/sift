## F1230 (high): Home and search: the Shorts section sits after the endless list, moves away when reached, and a mouse click on a Short can open a different video

Found by: T1-youtube. Where: https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=rick+astley (also https://www.youtube.com/ home), the collapsed 'Shorts' section

Evidence: On search, Sift puts the Shorts as a collapsed section after ol.items, as the last block (shortsTop 3723 of 3909 on load). The original shows the Shorts shelf inline, at result 8 of 44. When a mouse user scrolls the Shorts section into view (top about 715), Sift loads more results and adds them above it, so the section moves off screen within 0.5-3 s. Three rounds on search: 714 -> 966 -> … -> 4103 (li 40 -> 80); 715 -> 4777 (87 -> 134); 777 -> 3830 (134 -> 170). Home does the same: 714 -> 2730 (li 69 -> 93), 806 -> 2822 (165 -> 189). Mouse clicks aimed at a Short in the open section often hit something else. Clicks on /shorts/5Dj2DppA4aI opened watch?v=O7H-W1VqZrs&list=RD…, then watch?v=0h5QM6Kzh1w&list=RD…. With a 2 s pause before the click, the target had moved from y 445 to 1554, the point under the cursor was the 'Rick Astley's song “High Enough”' result, and watch?v=dZIDNuKKDQk opened. A click on the 3rd Short (/shorts/BFCvD0fBzyY) opened watch?v=cc91EfoBh8A. A click on the 2nd did nothing. With the keyboard, Enter on the same links opened the right Shorts (/shorts/5Dj2DppA4aI, /shorts/qswSBJqm_s0). Shots: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1/research/shots/r12t1-search-shorts-reached.png and /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1/research/shots/r12t1-search-shorts-10s-later.png (same scroll position 10 s later: the section is gone, replaced by new results).

Repro: cd /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1; SIFT_PORT=9461 SIFT_TAB=T1-youtube R=3 bun /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/t1r12/shortsreach.ts 'https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=rick+astley' prints the Shorts summary's top every 500 ms after it reaches the screen; it runs to 3000-4800. Then SIFT_PORT=9461 SIFT_TAB=T1-youtube N=0 PRE=2000 bun /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/t1r12/shortclick.ts opens the Shorts section, scrolls the first Short into view, waits 2 s and clicks it. It prints what is under the cursor and 'after: …/watch?v=…&list=RD…' instead of /shorts/<id>. With KEY=Enter, the right Short opens. Same on home: R=2 shortsreach.ts 'https://www.youtube.com/'.

Suspected cause: src/content/adapters/youtube.ts (home and search extract, around lines 375 and 473) does blocks.push(shortsBlock(shorts, …)) after the result list. So the Shorts section always comes after an endless list. Reaching it starts loading more, which pushes it down. Putting the section where the original's shelf is (inline at its index), or above the list, would keep it still.

Skeptic: Reproduced on the live site. Search 'rick astley', using real wheel steps (shortsreach.ts with my own tab). Three rounds where the collapsed Shorts section reached the screen: top 714 went to 4103 within 4 s (li 40 to 80), 715 went to 3964 (li 80 to 118), and 764 went to 3790 (li 118 to 153). Home did the same: 715 went to 2760 after 0.5 s (li 117 to 141). The original, read from the same DOM, has its Shorts shelves inline at rows 8, 12, 24 and 27 of 44 (grid-shelf-view-model). Sift folds every shelf into one section after ol.items. Mouse clicks miss. With PRE=2000, the click aimed at /shorts/5Dj2DppA4aI (y 446) landed on the 'Don't Say Goodbye' result after the target moved to y 1219, and watch?v=X6uzlX51kbo&list=RD… opened. With the default 300 ms pause I ran it twice: once the target moved to y 573 and the click hit a metadata line (nothing opened), and once it moved to y 769 and watch?v=X6uzlX51kbo opened. The code agrees. youtube.ts home() at line 375 and results() at line 473 push shortsBlock after the items block. nudgeEnd in index.ts loads more whenever the view is within 1.5 screens of the end, and a section that comes last is always that close. So a mouse user cannot reach the section until the feed runs out, and a click often opens a different video. No earlier rejected.md covers it. This is a real usability bug, medium to high.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/content/adapters/youtube.ts home() (line ~375) and results() (line ~473), stop pushing shortsBlock(...) after the items block. Insert it before the feed/results items block (after Topics or chips), or split the items list at the index of the original's first Shorts shelf. Then infinite loading never moves the section.
