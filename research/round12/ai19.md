## F1231 (medium): English home: some foreign-language video titles stay untranslated, with no English one-line description

Found by: T1-youtube. Where: https://www.youtube.com/ (English), home list one-liners

Evidence: On one English home load (lang en/en), 3 of 21 entries showed their original title with no English line: 'آیا فیلترشکن جامپ جامپ خطرناکه؟ | تست امنیت vpn جامپ جامپ', '翻墙必看！不容错过的10大宝藏级网站丨翻墙指南丨实用网站' and '#EnVivo ¬ #LosPeriodistas ¬ Cómo Taddei traicionó…'. The other 18 have English lines. On a later load, Sift's cache (IndexedDB sift/kv, key built as in doLines) held '\0keep-title' for 4 of the 6 non-Latin titles Sift asked about. Those 4 were written 07:59-08:20 UTC today, after chrome.sh reinstalled the worker with this build. One of them, 'هرگز به این ۵ سوال پاسخ ندهید! (بر اساس فلسفه شوپنهاور)', got a Spanish line ('Cinco preguntas que no debes responder…') in round 11's evidence. In the same session, other Persian titles did get English lines ('Manshe Amir says Israeli elections may be repeated'). One model reply captured from the worker for a Persian Short was 'نیلوفر خانم’s intelligence: YouTubers versus dubbing masters'. The original shows the same foreign titles, so nothing is lost. But these entries have no one-line description in the page's language. I did not find which check drops these lines: my offline run of Sift's vetting and language checks passes plausible English lines for them.

Repro: cd /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1; SIFT_PORT=9461 SIFT_TAB=T1-youtube W=60 bun /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/t1r11/lines.ts 'https://www.youtube.com/' prints each entry. Look for '~' rows with an empty [] title whose text is not English. Then SIFT_PORT=9461 SIFT_TAB=T1-youtube bun /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/t1r12/linecache.ts 'https://www.youtube.com/' '[^\x00-\x7F]{3}' prints each such item's cached value ('\0keep-title'). The feed is personalized, so the titles vary.

Suspected cause: The model's line fails one of the line checks (vetLine or lineFits in src/background/index.ts), and KEEP_TITLE is saved, so the title stays for 30 days. One possible gap I found offline: unsupported() flags 'Never answer these 5 questions…' as an unsupported number when the title writes 5 as the Persian digit '۵'.

Skeptic: Reproduced. On an English home load (lang en/en), 3 of 21 entries showed only a foreign title with no English line: 'اخبار بامدادی | سهشنبه ۵ آبان', 'کلاهبرداری که فلیم حسین رو ۱.۲ میلیارد دونیت کرد…' and 'Радіодиктант національної єдності 2026 | Дивіться НАЖИВО на Суспільному'. All four of their cache entries, plus the Chinese one, were '\0keep-title' with no exp field. That means save() wrote them after a failed check (30 days), not the one-day refusal path. I found the check T1 missed. I deleted those four entries, hooked the worker's fetch and reloaded. The model (openai/gpt-6-luna, the fallback) answered 'Persian morning news for Tuesday, 5 Aban', a good English line, and Sift stored keep-title again. Offline, unsupported() returns 'number' for that line, for 'Never answer these 5 questions…' against '۵', and for '1.2 billion' against '۱.۲'. numbersOf/sourceNumbers in src/background/text.ts match digits with \d, which in JS is ASCII 0-9 only. So a title written with Persian or other non-ASCII digits has no numbers, and any line that repeats its number is dropped. The Ukrainian entry fails for a different reason: the model answered in Ukrainian ('Національний радіодиктант єдності 2026 наживо…'). lineFits rightly rejected it, but keep-title is then cached for 30 days with no retry. The Chinese entry got an English line this time, so its earlier keep-title was model variance. The original shows the same titles, so nothing is lost. But those entries miss the one-line description in the page's language that the spec asks for, and the digit bug is deterministic and sticks for 30 days. Medium, at the low end.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/background/text.ts, normalize non-ASCII decimal digits (\p{Nd}: Persian ۰-۹, Arabic-Indic ٠-٩, Devanagari, full-width and others) to ASCII before numbersOf/sourceNumbers/LISTED match. Or match \p{Nd} and map it with its code-point offset. Then unsupported() in vetLine finds '۵' and '۱.۲' in the source. Separately, in doLines (src/background/index.ts ~line 568-578), when lineFits fails for the wrong language, ask the next model in the chain once, or save KEEP_TITLE with a short TTL instead of 30 days.

## F1233 (medium): BBC Anthropic article: Short version loses section '2.' and files the loss figures under '3. But luckily it has plenty of cash on hand'

Found by: T2-news. Where: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cwn8d7g29vd9o (Short version, cached, same on reload)

Evidence: The full text has H2s '1. Armageddon is an occupational hazard', '2. The company is haemorrhaging cash', '3. But luckily it has plenty of cash on hand', '4.…' and '5.…'. The Short version's headings are 1, 3, 4, 5. Under '3. But luckily it has plenty of cash on hand' it shows a list item 'Anthropic is losing money' and then the $2 trillion valuation and $80.7bn loss paragraphs (section 2's content). After that comes a list item 'It has substantial cash, but high costs'. The model-made sub-items ('AI could pose catastrophic risks', 'Anthropic is losing money', 'It has substantial cash…') are each a one-item ol with no start, so each is drawn as '1.'. A reader of '5 surprising things' sees 1, 3, 4, 5 and losses under a 'plenty of cash' heading. The numbers themselves match the source (ratio 0.47). Shots: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-2/research/shots/r12t2-bbc-anth-short-h3.png, /home/nico/projects/sift-test-2/research/shots/r12t2-bbc-cwn8d7g29vd9o-600.png

Then click:Show full text and list the h2s again → 1 to 5.

Suspected cause: The model dropped heading 2's placeholder and wrote its own numbered sub-points. The Markdown renderer also drops ordered-list start numbers (ol without start), so '2. …' and '3. …' show as '1.'.

Skeptic: Reproduced from the cached Short version. The Short version's h2s are '1. Armageddon…', '3. But luckily it has plenty of cash on hand', '4.…' and '5.…'. Section 2 'The company is haemorrhaging cash' is missing. Under heading 3 come the item 'Anthropic is losing money', then the $2 trillion valuation paragraph, the $80.7bn loss paragraph and the Hooper paragraph, then the item 'It has substantial cash, but high costs'. Each of those items is a separate <ol> with no start, drawn as '1.' (list-style decimal; shot checked). 'Show full text' and the original both list h2s 1 to 5. Reading the code supports both mechanisms. In short.ts planShort, a source heading the model dropped is put back before the next anchor (heading 3). The model's text for section 2 sits after heading 3, so the restored heading 2 has nothing under it, and the 'heading with nothing under it' pass removes it. In ui/markdown.tsx, ordered items render as <ol><li> and lose the model's number (line ~121 matches /^\s*\d+[.)]\s+/ but keeps no start). The result misfiles losses under a 'plenty of cash' heading and skips a number in a numbered listicle. Medium.

Skeptic's fix lead: src/content/short.ts planShort: when a dropped source heading would land with no text before the next heading, place it before the first text segment whose pos is in the range of that heading's section (pos between heading i and the next heading), even when that segment comes after the next heading. Or move that text segment up under the restored heading instead of deleting it in the empty-heading pass. src/content/ui/markdown.tsx: keep the first item's number as <ol start={n}> when it is not 1.
