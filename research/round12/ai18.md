## F1215 (high, regression): The Short version drops a sentence that differs from an earlier one by one word: the south district's boil-water notice, the evening dose, a recipe step, a second senator's vote

Found by: R3-ai-background. Where: src/background/text.ts:946-956 (dropRepeats nearOf), :945 and :972 (prose 'seen' is never reset at a heading); called from src/background/index.ts:896 (checked)

Evidence: In Chrome 9469 with my mock (fx repeats.html), the model's answer had both 'Residents of the north district must boil their tap water.' and 'Residents of the south district must boil their tap water.' The Short version shows only the north line. The south paragraph is not put back, and the page gives no sign that anything is missing. Offline (r12ai9/repeats.ts, repeats2.ts), dropRepeats also drops '- Take one tablet in the evening with water.' after the morning line, and '2. Add 200 g of sugar and mix well.' after the flour step. It drops 'Senator Jones voted for the bill on Tuesday.' after Smith, 'The museum is open on Sunday until late.' after Saturday, 'Children should take the vaccine…' after Adults, and 'The west line is closed this weekend…' after the east line. Across headings, fx sections.html lost the instruction under 'After using the toilet', because it repeated the one under 'Before cooking'. Offline, a section left empty this way loses its heading too.

Repro: bun /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/r12ai9/repeats.ts and repeats2.ts. In Chrome: run bun r12ai9/mock.ts (port 8889), then SIFT_PORT=9469 SIFT_TAB=R3-ai-background bun r12ai9/settings.ts mock, then bun r12ai9/watch.ts repeats.html 6. The Short version has no 'south district'.

Suspected cause: nearOf treats two sentences of 6 to 19 words as a repeat when one word is swapped (max = floor(len/10) || 1). Only numbers and negations are protected, so names, places, days, 'morning/evening', 'adults/children' and 'flour/sugar' are not. Prose sentences are compared across the whole text, including other sections. Lead: drop only exact repeats after normalizing. Or drop a near repeat only when the differing words are common function words. Keep the prose memory per heading section.

Skeptic: Confirmed, high, regression (dropRepeats is new in the round 11 diff). In Chrome 9469 with my copy of the mock, repeats.html gave 'Residents of the north district must boil their tap water.' but no south district line, on the first visit (1 call) and on the cached revisit (0 calls). The original page has the south paragraph, and nothing in Sift's view shows that anything is missing. sections.html lost 'Wash your hands…' under 'After using the toilet', so that heading was followed by an unrelated line. Offline, dropRepeats drops the south district, Senator Jones, Sunday, Children, the west line, '2. Add 200 g of sugar…' and '- Take one tablet in the evening…'. The 'Platform 4/5' pair is kept only because its numbers differ. Cause confirmed in nearOf: max = floor(len/10) || 1, so one swapped content word in a sentence of 6 to 19 words counts as a repeat. Only negations and digits are protected.

Skeptic's fix lead: src/background/text.ts dropRepeats: drop only exact repeats after normalizing. If near repeats stay, allow the differing words to be function words only, never names, places, days, times of day or nouns. Reset `prose` and `seen` at each heading line, so a section never loses its only sentence.

## F1216 (medium, regression): The speaker check now drops a correctly attributed quote when the paragraph names an organization before a 'she said' quote: 'Maria Lopez said “we will strike…”' disappears

Found by: R3-ai-background. Where: src/background/text.ts:754-760 (speakerNear pronoun branch, new in the F1117 fix) and :777-785 (namesPerson counts any two capitalized words, such as 'European Union' or 'Ford Motor Company')

Evidence: In Chrome 9469 with the mock (fx strike3.html), the source paragraph is 'Union leader Maria Lopez spoke to workers outside the plant on Friday. The European Union opened an inquiry into the sale of the plant last week. “We will strike if a single job goes,” she said.' The model wrote 'Maria Lopez said “we will strike if a single job goes”.' The Short version drops that sentence, and the article's headline point (the strike threat) is gone. Offline (r12ai9/spk.ts), unsupported() returns 'speaker' for it, and also when the middle sentence is 'Ford Motor Company bought the plant in 2019.' The same quote is kept when the middle sentence names no two-word name. In round 10 the pronoun branch accepted Lopez, who is named in the same section.

Repro: bun /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/r12ai9/spk.ts prints 'speaker' for cases 1 and 2. In Chrome with the mock: bun r12ai9/watch.ts strike3.html 6. No Lopez line.

Suspected cause: For a pronoun quote, any namesPerson() match after the speaker's mention rejects the speaker. namesPerson accepts any run of two or more capitalized words that does not follow 'in/from/…', so organizations, products and places count as people. Lead: count only names with a person cue (a role word, or a name the source ties to a speech verb elsewhere). Or ignore runs that start with 'The'.

Skeptic: Confirmed, medium, regression (namesPerson and the para check are new in round 11). In Chrome with the mock, strike3.html's Short version dropped 'Union leader Maria Lopez said “we will strike if a single job goes”.' on the first visit and on the cached revisit. In the source, 'she' clearly refers to Lopez. Offline, the reporter's spk.ts gives 'speaker' for 'The European Union' and 'Ford Motor Company'. My own cases also give 'speaker' for 'Real Madrid lost…' before a quote by Carlo Ancelotti, and for 'The White House has not commented' before a quote by Senator Cantwell. Without that sentence the same quote is kept, and the F1117 case (John Smith between the speaker and the quote) is still rejected correctly. Common news organizations, clubs and institutions therefore block correctly attributed 'he/she said' quotes.

Skeptic's fix lead: src/background/text.ts namesPerson: skip capitalized runs that start with 'The'. Count a two-word name only when it looks like a person: it has a role word before it, it is tied to a speech verb elsewhere in the source, or it is not a known organization pattern (Union, House, Company, Madrid…). Simplest: in the speakerNear pronoun branch, reject only when the later name is itself the subject of a speech verb or carries a ROLES word.

## F1217 (medium, regression): Numbers in a Short version are no longer checked in any language but six: a Russian, Japanese or Dutch Short version can state a number the article does not

Found by: R3-ai-background. Where: src/background/text.ts:814 and :830 (unsupported returns '' when lang is not en/es/pt/fr/de/it, or is ''); src/background/index.ts:896 passes want

Evidence: Offline (r12ai9/nums.ts). The Russian source has 12 people injured and 40 million rubles. The rewrite 'При пожаре в Казани пострадали 21 человек. Ущерб — 400 млн рублей.' is kept whole with lang 'ru'. Without a lang (the round 10 path) it was dropped. In Japanese, the source has 3 workers and 12 fire engines, and a rewrite with 8 and 20 is kept. In Dutch, the source has 7 injured and 2 million euros, and 70 and 20 million are kept. The same happens when proseLang returns '' (Serbian, Norwegian, Malay, or Galician on a pt page).

Repro: bun /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/r12ai9/nums.ts

Suspected cause: The F1115 fix stops the number check for every language without a NUMBER_WORDS list. The check is right in most of these, because the source writes the number in digits. Lead: in such a language, drop a sentence only when the source has no word next to the same noun that could spell the number. Or put the source sentence back, as warningOf does. Or skip only the numbers 0 to 99 that a spelled-out word could stand for, and keep checking the rest.

Skeptic: Confirmed, medium, regression from the F1115 fix (NUMBER_LANGS is new in round 11). Offline, nums.ts keeps the wrong Russian, Japanese and Dutch numbers when a lang is passed, and drops them without one (the old path). In Chrome with the mock, I made a Russian fixture (lang=ru: 12 injured, 40 million rubles). The mocked model answer said 21 and 400. The 'КРАТКАЯ ВЕРСИЯ' showed 'пострадали 21 человек' and 'Ущерб — 400 млн рублей' on two loads. The number check now guards only en/es/pt/fr/de/it, and pages where the language is ''. The trigger needs the model to misstate a number, but catching that is the whole point of the check.

Skeptic's fix lead: src/background/text.ts unsupported: do not return '' for every language outside NUMBER_LANGS. Skip only the numbers the source could be spelling out: digits with no digit match in the source, when the source has unknown non-digit words in the same position. Or reuse the warningOf approach and put the source sentence back instead of keeping the rewrite. Keep checking numbers of three digits or more, and numbers the source writes in digits.
