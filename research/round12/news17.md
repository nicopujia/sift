## F1232 (high): Corriere della Sera: no way to sign in from Sift

Found by: T2-news. Where: https://www.corriere.it/ and its articles (Sift header and Menu)

Evidence: Sift's header has only 'Cerca in Corriere', Cerca and Menu. No a or button in Sift matches /accedi|login/. The Menu dialog (2274 characters) has no Accedi or login entry. The original header has <a href="javascript:accediLinkPop();" class="button button--login">Accedi</a> (115 px wide, top 20). accediLinkPop sets location.href to https://www.corriere.it/account/login?landing=<page>. The original's consent wall also offers 'Sei già abbonato? Accedi'. A real click on that link opened https://www.corriere.it/account/login?landing=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.corriere.it%2F with email and password fields (shot /home/nico/projects/sift-test-2/research/shots/r12t2-corriere-accedi-orig2.png). In Sift, the only remaining route is the dialog that a story's SALVA button opens. Its ACCEDI button did nothing: the URL stayed https://www.corriere.it/ and the dialog stayed open (shot /home/nico/projects/sift-test-2/research/shots/r12t2-corriere-accedi-sift.png). Sift shows the login page itself correctly once you are there.

Repro: SIFT_PORT=9462 SIFT_TAB=T2-news bun scripts/act.ts goto:https://www.corriere.it/ wait:10000 'eval:[...document.querySelector("sift-root").shadowRoot.querySelectorAll("a,button")].filter(e=>/accedi|login/i.test(e.innerText)).length' click:Menu wait:1500 'eval:/accedi|login/i.test(document.querySelector("sift-root").shadowRoot.querySelector("[role=dialog]").innerText)' press:Escape 'eval:[...document.querySelectorAll("a.button--login")].map(a=>a.outerHTML.slice(0,120))'  → 0, false, and the original's Accedi link. Then: click:SALVA wait:3000 click:ACCEDI wait:5000 url → https://www.corriere.it/

Suspected cause: The only sign-in control is a javascript: link (javascript:accediLinkPop()), and the header or link filter drops javascript: hrefs. ACCEDI in the SALVA dialog is probably also a script-only control whose replay does not run.

Skeptic: Confirmed in part. Severity is medium, not high. The core reproduces on the home page and on the article. Sift's header is 'Cerca in Corriere | Cerca | Menu'. No a or button in Sift matches /accedi|login|abbonati/. The Menu dialog (2274 characters) has no sign-in entry. The original header shows a.button--login 'Accedi' at 1096,20, 115x19 (home) and 1161,13 (article). It is visible, inside header.header--topbar. The suspected cause is wrong. findTop() (src/content/extract/generic.ts ~6812) does select a[href] links with javascript: hrefs. The link is dropped because its label fails TOP_LABEL (~6789), which knows only English, Spanish and Portuguese words. I ran the regex: 'Accedi' false, 'Se connecter' false, 'Connexion' false, 'Anmelden' false, 'Sign in' true. One part of the claim is a harness artifact: the dialog route works. After SALVA, the page view also shows a second 'ACCEDI' button under the first story, behind the dialog's backdrop. act.ts click:ACCEDI picks the first match in document order, so it clicked that hidden copy. With the dialog's own button ('[role=dialog] button.btn'), Sift went to https://www.corriere.it/account/login?landing=https://www.corriere.it/. So that route works, but only through a story's SALVA dialog. The visible header sign-in is still missing on Italian pages, and the same gap likely hits French and German sites. Side note, not claimed: the duplicate 'ACCEDI' that appears in the page view after SALVA looks like a separate small extraction bug.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/content/extract/generic.ts, add sign-in and account words for the languages Sift has tables for to TOP_LABEL: it 'accedi|registrati|il mio account|abbonati?', fr 'se connecter|connexion|mon compte|s'inscrire', de 'anmelden|einloggen|registrieren|mein konto', and so on. A javascript: link already becomes a ref-only action through toAction, and act() replays it with proxy.click.

## F1234 (medium): Corriere (Italian page): Sift's own UI strings fall back to English

Found by: T2-news. Where: https://www.corriere.it/ and https://www.corriere.it/esteri/26_ottobre_27/israele-scontro-netanyahu-zamir-iran-b0536658-e493-4073-82aa-e8ec4a350xlk.shtml

Evidence: Page and view lang are it-IT. Some Sift labels are Italian: Cerca, Mostra testo completo, Versione breve, Vista Sift. Others are English: the skip button 'Skip to content', and the audio portal's name 'Interactive area from the original page: Example · widget.spreaker.com. Press Enter to use it, Tab to leave.' In src/content/i18n.ts the it table has 42 of the 99 keys of the en table. The 57 missing keys include skip, back10, fwd10, mute, unmute, video, interactive, enterToUse, shortening, shortReady, error, next and previous. So media bars, status announcements and portal hints on Italian pages are in English. The fr and de tables are about as incomplete (43 and 42 keys).

Repro: SIFT_PORT=9462 SIFT_TAB=T2-news bun scripts/act.ts goto:https://www.corriere.it/esteri/26_ottobre_27/israele-scontro-netanyahu-zamir-iran-b0536658-e493-4073-82aa-e8ec4a350xlk.shtml wait:15000 'eval:(()=>{const R=document.querySelector("sift-root").shadowRoot;return [document.documentElement.lang,[...R.querySelectorAll("button,.portal")].map(e=>e.getAttribute("aria-label")||e.innerText)]})()'

Suspected cause: The partial Italian (and French and German) tables in src/content/i18n.ts fall back to English for most keys.

Skeptic: Reproduced on the Corriere article (html lang it-IT, Sift's view lang it-IT). The Sift labels are 'Skip to content', 'Cerca in Corriere', 'Cerca', 'Menu', 'Mostra testo completo', then 'Interactive area from the original page: Example · widget.spreaker.com. Press Enter to use it, Tab to leave.', then 'Vista Sift'. So the skip link and the portal's name and hint are English on an Italian page. The code confirms it. In src/content/i18n.ts, en has 99 keys and it has 42 (fr 43, de 42; he, fa, ru, zh, zh-Hant, ja, ko, tr, nl and pl have 36). t() falls back to en[k] for a missing key. Screen reader users on these pages hear English UI strings: skip, interactive, enterToUse, shortening, shortReady, error, media controls and paging. This breaks 'keeps the page's language' and accessibility on a whole class of pages. Medium.

Skeptic's fix lead: Complete the it, fr and de tables in src/content/i18n.ts (and the 36-key tables) for at least the keys screen readers and status messages use: skip, interactive, enterToUse, the portal hint, shortening, shortReady, error, back10, fwd10, mute, unmute, video, next and previous. A typecheck that makes Table require those keys would stop new gaps.

## F1235 (medium): Corriere home: Enter in Sift's search sometimes posts the menu form and reloads the home with no results

Found by: T2-news. Where: https://www.corriere.it/ (Sift header search field, about 10 s after load)

Evidence: 2 of 4 tries about 9-10 s after load: typing 'Sinner' and pressing Enter sent 'NAV POST https://www.corriere.it/#' and left the home page. The field maps to the burger form (action '#', method post). The other 2 tries at about 9-11 s, and tries at 15 s and 30 s, went to https://www.corriere.it/ricerca/?q=Sinner, which has 5694 results. Clicking Sift's Cerca button worked at 9 s. The site's own handler (keyup Enter on .js-search-input, or a click on .js-search-button) does window.location.href='https://www.corriere.it/ricerca/?q='+value. On the article page, Enter worked 1 of 1.

Repro: D=/tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/r12t2; SIFT_PORT=9462 SIFT_TAB=T2-news bun $D/drive.ts goto:about:blank netlog goto:https://www.corriere.it/ wait:9000 'sclick:input[type=search]' type:Sinner press:Enter wait:7000 url   (repeat 3-4 times; a failure shows 'NAV POST https://www.corriere.it/#')

Suspected cause: Timing in the Enter replay (src/content/proxy.ts key() and submitImplicitly). Sometimes a native submit of the action='#' form wins over the site's keyup or lens-click handler.

Skeptic: Reproduced, but at a different time and for a different cause than claimed. 3 of 3 Enter tries at 3-5 s after DOMContentLoaded posted 'NAV POST https://www.corriere.it/#' and left the URL at https://www.corriere.it/#. A click on Sift's Cerca at 4 s did the same. 13 of 13 Enter tries at 7-11 s went to /ricerca/?q=Sinner. I never saw the failure at 9-10 s, though slower loads may push the window later. It is not a race in the Enter replay. Corriere binds its search late. CDP getEventListeners showed no keyup on .js-search-input and no click on .js-search-button until about 11-13 s after navigation start in one run. The load event fired at about 6.4-7.4 s. Before that, the burger form (action '#', method post, button type=submit) is submitted natively, by Chrome's implicit submission for Enter and by the click for Cerca. That only reloads the home and loses the query. In the original, the field sits inside the closed burger menu (left -352) behind the consent wall, so it cannot be used that early. Sift shows a working-looking search at once. Searching in the first seconds, which is realistic, silently does nothing useful. Medium.

Skeptic's fix lead: src/content/proxy.ts submit()/submitImplicitly (or H.act and the Enter path in index.ts): when the form's action is a placeholder ('#', or the current URL with only a fragment) and document.readyState is not 'complete', do not submit natively. Hold the replay until the window load event plus a short delay, then replay Enter or the click. Or show 'loading' on the field until then.
