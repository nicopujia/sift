## F1200 (high): On a page with a shown original (video, map, iframe), a popup the page appends to <body> and focuses during a Sift click still closes at once (F1102 on portal pages)

Found by: R2-interaction. Where: src/content/index.ts:3432 (clickSwallowed(el, () => proxy.click(el))); src/content/shield.ts:51-62 (ancestors of a portal stay out of inert); src/content/index.ts:2598 (new <body> children become inert only in the observer callback, after the click)

Evidence: Fixture /fx/r12int-focusportal.html is r11int-focusclose.html plus one iframe, so Sift shows a portal and <body> is not inert as a whole. In Sift, Invite people logs ['share open','share closed'] and no sheet stays open. On the original it logs ['share open'] with focus on #copyurl. A focus-event trace in Sift shows the sequence. blur on Sift's button (rel=copyurl), then focusin on #copyurl: the page's focus() succeeded because the new popup was not inert. Then 'inert sharebox=true' (refreshShield), then blur and focusout on #copyurl (rel=null), then the page removes the popup 150 ms later. Account and Rename work on this fixture: their elements sit in subtrees that were already inert.

Repro: SIFT_PORT=9467 ./scripts/chrome.sh start; cd /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/r12int; FX=http://127.0.0.1:8799/fx/r12int-focusportal.html SIFT_PORT=9467 SIFT_TAB=R2-interaction bun focusclose.ts sift 'Invite people' prints log ['share open','share closed']. The same with 'orig' prints ['share open'] and docActive button#copyurl.

Suspected cause: With any portal, desired() keeps <body> and the portal's ancestors out of inert. A node the page appends to one of them during the replayed click is reachable until the MutationObserver's refreshShield runs. So the page's focus() lands for real, out of Sift. Making the node inert afterwards blurs it, and widgets that close on blur or focusout close (React portals, menus and share popups that focus their first item). Lead: around proxy.click in H.act, tell the page-world helper that a Sift click is running (a sift-click start/end event). While it runs, the helper's focus wrapper skips the native focus() for targets outside [data-sift-portal] and fires sift-focus, as it does for inert targets. Refreshing the shield sooner does not help, because it still blurs.

Skeptic: Confirmed. I would rate it medium to high: the popup fails completely, but only on pages that have both a shown portal and a popup that closes on blur. On /fx/r12int-focusportal.html, focusclose.ts with 'Invite people' logged ['share open','share closed'] in Sift in 3 runs (1.5 s, 1.5 s and 4 s waits). No sheet opened and Sift's focus went back to its 'Invite people' button. With Sift off, 2 runs logged ['share open'] with document.activeElement = button#copyurl. On /fx/r11int-focusclose.html (the same page without the iframe), Sift logged ['share open'] twice, with the sheet shown and focus on 'Copy invite link'. So the shown portal is what breaks it. Before the click, my own trace showed body.inert=false, one [data-sift-portal], and data-sift-inert only on header, h1, p, the 4 sections and p#log. During the click, the events ran in this order: blur on Sift's button (rel=#copyurl), then focus and focusin on button#copyurl. So the page's focus() really landed, because #sharebox was a new, non-inert child of <body>. Next came 'added sharebox inert=true' (the observer's refreshShield), then blur and focusout on #copyurl (rel=null). The page then removed the box. Account and Rename still work on this page (['menu open'] and ['editing'] with focus in Sift's textarea), as the reporter said. The mechanism matches the code. In desired() (src/content/shield.ts), once there are exceptions, <body> and the portal's ancestors stay out of inert. New body children are made inert only in the MutationObserver callback (src/content/index.ts ~2598). That callback cannot run during the synchronous proxy.click inside clickSwallowed (index.ts ~3432).

Skeptic's fix lead: Mark the Sift click in the page-world helper, the same way sift-enter and sift-leave with detail 'key' are handled in src/main/index.ts. In H.act (src/content/index.ts ~3432), dispatch a start event before clickSwallowed(el, () => proxy.click(el)) and an end event in a finally. In the focus wrapper in src/main/index.ts (~194, the one that dispatches sift-focus), while that flag is set, skip the native focus() for a target that is not inside [data-sift-portal] and dispatch sift-focus. Sift's existing follow logic (followPageFocus) then moves focus to its copy, as it already does when <body> is inert. Calling refreshShield earlier does not help, because it still blurs the popup.
