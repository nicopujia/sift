H2 Wild moment New Jersey mayor charges at a resident in front of shocked locals during out-of-control public meeting
p: Rachel Bowman · Oct 23, 2026, 10:53 PM
    [link] 266 comments
    [link] New Jersey
  p: Pemberton Township Republican Mayor Matthew Bianchini, 59, had to be manhandled on Wednesday night after charging at a resident and telling him: 'I am f****** getting you.'
    [link] Pine Barrens Tribute
  p: Mayor Bianchini is seen leaving his seat after pointing his finger towards the resident, identified as Michael Sawka, and charging across the room to the public gallery.
  p: As the mayor approaches Sawka, a man in a blue checked suit steps between them and shouts 'stop' at Bianchini.
  p: Another man gets involved, and the two pin Bianchini against a wall, where he raises his hands as if accepting defeat.
  p: 'You should resign,' Sawka said, causing the mayor to try to lunge at him again; at this point, three men hold him back.
  p: The mayor was then led away by one man in a bear hug, as he shouted at the man: 'I am getting you, I am f****** getting you.'

  p: Pemberton Township Mayor Matthew Bianchini, 59, had to be held back from attacking a constituent during a tense town meeting
  p: Bianchini claimed he has a fraught relationship with the man and alleged the man had made rude comments to his family
  p: 'He just threatened me; that's on video. I want the police to arrest him; that is assault,' Sawka said.
  p: Bianchini was led away to a side room, and according to the tribute, everyone involved in the spat was asked to leave the meeting.
  p: Another angle captured by the township's own livestream showed the mayor and the man trading words across the room as others spoke.
  p: Just seconds before the mayor jumped out of his seat, the man was seen flipping him off with his middle finger.

  p: 'I just basically snapped,' Bianchini said. 'I hate to put it this way, but it's a long time coming.'
  p: According to Bianchini, Sawka and another resident were making rude comments to his wife and daughter, who were sitting in the back of the room during the meeting.
  p: 'Was it wrong? Absolutely. Was it justified? Absolutely. And I would do it again if I had to,' Bianchini said.

  p: 'I'm just sitting there. I never got up. I sat there the whole time. I was calm the whole time,' he said.
  p: Sawka said he wants the mayor to step down from office and be charged for his actions.
