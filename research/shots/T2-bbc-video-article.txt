  p: Mississippi releases footage of police shooting that killed infant
  p: Mississippi authorities have released body camera footage showing how a toddler was fatally shot by a police officer responding to a report of shoplifting in a Walmart car park.
  p: Kohen Wiley, aged one, was on his mother's lap in a moving car when officers surrounded the vehicle in the city of Senatobia on 14 June.
  p: The state's attorney general found the policeman was justified in using force, and the officer's lawyer argued in a statement to the BBC that the driver was attempting to kill law enforcement.
  p: The child's family disputes that the shooting was justified. Their lawyer, Ben Crump, has called the police response "excessive from the very start".
  p: Crump said an allegation of shoplifting "does not warrant the death" of a child.
  p: Kohen Wiley was riding in the vehicle with his mother, Vellesiya Wiley, and another woman at the time of the incident.
  p: Senatobia Mayor Greg Graves confirmed to the BBC's US partner CBS that Hunter Foster, the officer who opened fire, had resigned.
  p: The Senatobia Police Department did not immediately respond to the BBC's requests for confirmation.
  [img] Ben Crump Law Kohen Wiley
  figcaption: Kohen Wiley
  p: The footage released by Mississippi authorities on Thursday shows a first-person perspective of the officer who opened fire.
  p: After being told by a staff member that suspected shoplifters were getting away, five law enforcement officers run towards the car with guns drawn.
  p: The vehicle reverses, crashing into a parked car, before pulling forwards.
  p: The officer wearing the body camera raises his gun and aims it at the moving vehicle as he runs closer, shouting: "Stop!"
  p: As the car closely passes the officer, he fires his gun, shattering the passenger-side window.
  p: Four gunshots can be heard, as police command the driver to stop. The car continues to drive off slowly through the car park.
  p: CCTV video from the storefront appears to show that at the moment the policeman opens fire, another officer is standing near the front of the moving vehicle.
  p: "The driver of the vehicle was attempting to kill those 5 officers," Martin Zummach, an attorney representing the officer, said in a statement to the BBC, adding that his client "had no choice but to discharge his weapon
  p: "We are very sad for the loss of life," Zummach said. "But the outcome of the investigation was not only expected, but it was overdue."
  p: The child's mother, Wiley, has denied she and her friend stole nappies from the store. No charges have been filed against them.
  p: The boy's death has sparked protests and vigils in Mississippi.
  p: At a news conference, relatives demanded accountability.
  p: "After what I've seen today, I'm having a difficult time understanding the word, 'justified,' said LaSandra Williams, Kohen Wiley's grandmother.
