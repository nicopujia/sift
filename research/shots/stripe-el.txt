  p: If your integration isn’t working:

    li: Click the checkout button and confirm it sent an XHR request to your server-side endpoint (POST /create-checkout-session).
    li: Verify the request is returning a 200 status.
    li: Use console.log(session) inside your button click listener to confirm the correct data returned.
