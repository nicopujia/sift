## F1102 (high, regression): Since the F1040 fix, a widget that focuses itself (or a popup it adds) on click and closes on blur closes at once in Sift: menus, rename-in-place and on-demand popups no longer open

Found by: R2-interaction. Where: src/content/index.ts:3415-3418 (H.act: withoutShield around proxy.click, then back.focus)

Evidence: New fixture http://127.0.0.1:8799/fx/r11int-focusclose.html: three vanilla widgets that move focus during their click. (1) 'Account' calls this.focus(), opens its menu, and closes the menu when the button loses focus. (2) A role=button list name becomes contenteditable and takes focus; leaving it ends the edit. (3) 'Invite people' adds a popup to <body>, focuses its 'Copy invite link' button, and closes on focusout. Results with the merged build: Account logs ['menu open','menu closed'] in Sift and ['menu open'] on the original. Rename logs ['editing','edit ended: Groceries'] in Sift and ['editing'] on the original. Invite logs ['share open','share closed'] in Sift with no sheet, and ['share open'] on the original with focus on Copy invite link. Control build: a copy of the same tree where only line 3415 goes back to clickSwallowed(el, () => proxy.click(el)), run with the same fixture and script. There, Account gives ['menu open']. Rename gives ['editing'], with Sift's focus in the textarea that stands for the name. Invite gives ['share open'], with Sift's sheet open and focus on 'Copy invite link'. Those are the original's results, and the round 10 behavior. Modal <dialog>.showModal() (new or already in the page) behaves like the original in both builds. Copy buttons (F1040) work in both: the control build was checked only for these widgets.

Repro: SIFT_PORT=9467 ./scripts/chrome.sh start; cd /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/r11int; SIFT_PORT=9467 SIFT_TAB=R2-interaction bun focusclose.ts sift Account, then 'bun focusclose.ts sift Rename' and 'bun focusclose.ts sift "Invite people"'. Each prints the page log with the close entry. 'bun focusclose.ts orig <same>' prints the original's log without it.

Suspected cause: withoutShield(..., el) takes inert off el's whole subtree, and off any node the page adds under <body> or el's ancestors during the click. So a page focus() made during the click now succeeds. Before, it failed on the inert node, the page-world helper sent sift-focus, and Sift followed. Line 3418 then moves focus back to Sift's button synchronously, which fires blur and focusout on the page element the page just focused, and the page closes its menu, edit or popup. Lead: lift inert only for what copying needs, not for focus. For example, the page-world helper could wrap HTMLTextAreaElement/HTMLInputElement select() and Selection addRange/selectAllChildren, and ask Sift to take inert off that one node, while focus() during the click keeps failing as before. Or, when the page took focus during the click, leave it there and let the sift-focus follow move Sift's own focus later, without blurring the page element.

Skeptic: Confirmed, high severity, and a regression from the F1040 change. I ran focusclose.ts on /fx/r11int-focusclose.html twice on the merged build (port 9467, tab skeptic). Both runs gave the same result. Account logged ['menu open','menu closed']. Rename logged ['editing','edit ended: Groceries']. Invite people logged ['share open','share closed'], and no sheet appeared. Sift's focus ended back on the clicked button each time. On the original, Account logged ['menu open'] with focus on button#acct. Rename logged ['editing'] with focus on div#name. Invite logged ['share open'] with focus on button#copyurl. I made my own control build: a copy of the tree under /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/skeptic-r11/ctl where only index.ts:3415 goes back to clickSwallowed(el, () => proxy.click(el)), run on port 9479. There, Account gave ['menu open']. Rename gave ['editing'], with Sift's focus in a textarea. Invite gave ['share open'], with Sift's sheet showing 'Copy invite link' and focus on it. Those match the original. The code agrees with the stated cause. withoutShield(..., el) in shield.ts keeps el and its path out of inert, and a node appended to <body> during the click is not inert either. So the page's focus() during the click now succeeds. Then back.focus() at index.ts:3418 blurs the element the page just focused, and widgets that close on blur or focusout close at once. The fixture is hand-made, but these are common patterns: rename-in-place that commits on blur, and menus or popups that focus their first item and close on focusout.

Skeptic's fix lead: In H.act (src/content/index.ts:3415), go back to clickSwallowed(el, () => proxy.click(el)) so the page stays inert during replayed clicks. A focus() the page makes then fails, and the existing sift-focus follow takes over, as in round 10. Fix F1040 more narrowly in the page-world helper (src/main/index.ts). Wrap HTMLInputElement/HTMLTextAreaElement.prototype.select and setSelectionRange, and Selection.addRange and selectAllChildren (or document.execCommand('copy') itself). When the target is under Sift's inert shield, send an event that makes Sift take inert off just that one node until the copy finishes. Never lift inert from el's subtree or from <body>'s new children for focus. Retest r10int-copylink.html, r10skep-clipboardjs.html and r11int-focusclose.html.

## F1103 (medium): React Aria date field: a whole date typed in one go stays in the month segment (12/25/2027 becomes month 7, day and year empty); the reader must press Tab between segments

Found by: R2-interaction. Where: src/content/index.ts:978-983 (sliderKey: the digit's keydown and beforeinput reach the segment while the page is inert)

Evidence: /fx/r10skep-ariadate.html uses a real react-aria-components DateField. Typing 1,2,2,5,2,0,2,7 on Sift's month spinbutton gives the page '7 – July | dd | yyyy' with no value. On the original the same keys give 12/25/2027 (value 2027-12-25). With keys 1,2, month becomes 12, but focus does not move to the day, as it does on the original. The same keys with Tab between segments give 12/25 in Sift. While typing, no focus() call reaches the page: the page-world helper fired no sift-focus or sift-pagefocus event. In the page's react-aria@3.53.1 code, the focusable check `Cr(e)=e.matches(..)&&Co(e)&&!wb(e)` has wb() walk the parents for `.inert`. Under Sift's inert <body>, focusManager.focusNext() finds no next segment. Typing one digit per segment (the F1041 repro) works.

Repro: SIFT_PORT=9467 ./scripts/chrome.sh start; cd /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/r10int; SIFT_PORT=9467 SIFT_TAB=R2-interaction bun aria15.ts prints 'SIFT page: 7/7 – July | dd/Empty | yyyy/Empty | value undefined'. 'bun aria15.ts orig' prints 'ORIG page: 12/12 – December | 25/25 | 2027/2027 | value 2027-12-25'. 'KEYS=1,2 bun aria15.ts' shows month 12 with Sift's focus still on the month.

Suspected cause: React Aria moves to the next segment with focusManager.focusNext(), which skips inert elements. All segments sit under Sift's inert <body>, so no next segment is found and later digits overwrite the month. Lead: dispatch the digit's keydown and beforeinput with the shield lifted for the field's group (el.closest('[role=group]') or the segments' common parent), not only el's path. Then let Sift's existing page-focus follow move its focus to its copy of the next segment. Keep the first finding in mind: taking focus back must not blur a widget that closes on blur.

Skeptic: Confirmed, medium severity, not a regression. I ran aria15.ts on /fx/r10skep-ariadate.html (a real react-aria-components DateField) twice. Sift ended with page '7/7 – July | dd/Empty | yyyy/Empty' and no value. The original ended with 12/25/2027 and value 2027-12-25 both times. With KEYS=1,2, Sift sets month 12 and its focus stays on the month. With Tab between segments, Sift gives 2027-12-25. My control build (index.ts:3415 reverted) also gives month 7, so this is older than the F1040 change. The suspected cause is only half right. React Aria 3.53.1's createFocusManager.focusNext (FocusScope.mjs from esm.sh) starts from document.activeElement. If the group does not contain that element, it walks from the group root and wraps to the first segment. I tested this in two ways. First, I made <body>.inert read false to page scripts while keeping the real inert attribute. Then React Aria called focus() only on the month segment ('sift-focus 1', 'sift-focus 12', ...), never on the day, and the result stayed 7. That happens because document.activeElement is sift-root. Second, I combined that with a 'sift-enter' event from each keyed segment, so the helper's activeElement names the segment. Then the full date worked in Sift: 12|25|2027, value 2027-12-25. sift-focus fired on 'dd' and then 'yyyy', and Sift's focus followed to its year copy. The inert lie alone and the sift-enter alone each left month 7. So both blockers must go: the inert ancestor fails React Aria's focusable check, and document.activeElement names Sift's host. The reporter's lead, lifting the shield for the group, would still wrap focusNext to the first segment.

Skeptic's fix lead: In sliderKey (src/content/index.ts ~975-983), when a digit goes to a contenteditable page spinbutton, wrap proxy.key in el.dispatchEvent(new CustomEvent('sift-enter', {bubbles:true, composed:true})) and a matching 'sift-leave'. The page-world helper's activeElement override then names el, and focusNext starts from it. Also get the next segment past React Aria's inert-ancestor check without letting real focus land on the page. For example, the helper (src/main/index.ts) could make the inert getter return false for [data-sift-inert] elements while a Sift key dispatch runs. The page's focus() on the day segment then fails as before, sift-focus fires, and Sift follows to its own copy, with no blur on the page (see N0). In my test, this combination typed a full 12/25/2027 correctly.

## F1041 (still open, partly; original repro in /home/nico/projects/sift/research/round10/*.md)

Recheck by R2-interaction: The reported repro passes. segs.ts in Sift: typing '7' gives month=7 and ArrowUp gives 8, the same as the original. On the real React Aria DateField (/fx/r10skep-ariadate.html, aria15.ts with keys 1,2,2,5,2,0,2,7), Sift gives page '7 – July | dd | yyyy', while the original gives 12/25/2027. React Aria 3.53.1's focusable check walks up the parents looking for `.inert` (source: `function wb(e){...if(t.inert)return!0...}`). Under Sift's inert <body>, focusNext() therefore finds no next segment. Details in the second finding.

Skeptic: Partly fixed, as claimed. This is the same remaining defect as N1, so count it once. F1041's own repro passes. segs.ts in Sift gave month=7 after typing 7 and month=8 after ArrowUp, the same as the original, in two runs. On the real React Aria DateField, typed digits now reach the month segment, but React Aria never moves on to the next segment. The cause is the two-part one described under N1: the inert ancestor, and document.activeElement naming sift-root.

Skeptic's fix lead: Same as N1: in sliderKey (src/content/index.ts), make document.activeElement name the page segment during the digit's dispatch (sift-enter/sift-leave). Also let React Aria's focusable check pass for the next segment without lifting the real inert. Then the existing sift-focus follow moves Sift to its copy of the next segment.
