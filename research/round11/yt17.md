## F1120 (medium): Playlist watch at 600 px and at 200% zoom: no Previous or Next in Sift, and Shift+N does nothing (remaining part of F1000)

Found by: T1-youtube. Where: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ&list=PL2MI040U_GXobmpXtTwBF7oHBGT5BETSD at a 600 px window or 640x450@2x

Evidence: At 600 px and at 640x450@2x, Sift's bar is 'Pause|Back 10s|Ahead 10s|Mute|Captions|Full screen'. The original's .ytp-prev-button and .ytp-next-button are display:none at that player size. With focus on Back 10s, Shift+N leaves v=dQw4w9WgXcQ (t 73.6 -> 77.7). On the original at 600 px (Sift off, click on the video title, Shift+N), the URL becomes watch?v=yPYZpwSpKmA&list=…&index=2. At 1000 and 1280 px, Sift shows 'Replay/Previous' and 'Next video', and Shift+N works. Zoom users can reach the next item only through the playlist section. Shots: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1/research/shots/r11t1-plwatch-600.png, /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1/research/shots/r11t1-plwatch-200.png

Repro: cd /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1; SIFT_PORT=9461 SIFT_TAB=T1-youtube W=600 KEYS='Shift+N' GAP=4000 FOCUS='Back 10s' bun /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/t1r11/keys.ts 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ&list=PL2MI040U_GXobmpXtTwBF7oHBGT5BETSD'. The bar has no Previous/Next, and v stays dQw4w9WgXcQ. Original: SIFT_PORT=9461 SIFT_TAB=T1-youtube bun /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/t1r11/origshiftn.ts prints index=2.

Suspected cause: The adapter sets media.prev/next, and app.tsx draws them, only when the player's own buttons exist and are shown (elOf). onKey for Shift+N/P also needs media.next/prev. When YouTube hides them in a small player, both go away. The playlist panel's next item (or the original's keyboard shortcut) could be used instead.

Skeptic: Reproduced, three runs. On the playlist watch page at 600 px (twice) and at 640x450@2x (once), Sift's bar was 'Pause|Back 10s|Ahead 10s|Mute|Captions|Full screen|Position in video'. YouTube had hidden .ytp-prev-button and .ytp-next-button inline (style 'display: none;', player not in ytp-small-mode). With focus on Back 10s, Shift+N left v=dQw4w9WgXcQ in place (t 13.9 -> 18.9) both times. The original at 600 px (Sift off with trusted Alt+Shift+S, click on the title, Shift+N) went to v=yPYZpwSpKmA&index=2. At 1280 px, Sift's bar had 'Previous|Next video', and Shift+N went to index=2. The code matches the claim: youtube.ts playerBlock sets prev/next through vis(), which drops buttons whose computed display is none, and app.tsx onKey acts on Shift+N only when media.next is set. One nuance: at 600 px the original shows no Previous or Next buttons either. So the gap against the original is the Shift+N/Shift+P keys, plus F1000's policy, which asks for Previous and Next in Sift's bar on any playlist. Sift's playlist section ('RickAstley - Greatest Hits · 1 / 10') still reaches the next item. This is the same defect as F1000's remaining part.

Skeptic's fix lead: src/content/adapters/youtube.ts playerBlock: when the URL has a list, keep .ytp-prev-button and .ytp-next-button even when YouTube hides them inline at small sizes. Use q(sel, player) without the computed-display check, as the captions button already does. Or fall back to the playlist panel's next and previous ytd-playlist-panel-video-renderer. Check that proxy.click on the hidden button (index.ts H.media 'next'/'prev') still advances the playlist; if not, replay Shift+N or Shift+P to #movie_player.

## F1121 (medium): Playlist page: Sift's Menu has 3 entries (Home, Shorts, You); the original's menu button opens 11

Skeptic: Reproduced twice on fresh loads (15 s and 20 s waits). Sift's Menu dialog on /playlist?list=PL2MI040U_GXobmpXtTwBF7oHBGT5BETSD was 'Close | Home | Shorts | You'. The original's visible mini-guide on the same load had Home, Shorts, Subscriptions and You, so Sift drops Subscriptions even from that. The code agrees: youtube.extract returns null for /playlist, so the generic extractor builds the menu. youtube.fallback adds only Settings (top()) and the settings dialog, not the adapter's nav()/renderGuide(). Not in any rejected.md (round 5 rejected a different playlist-page report). Shot: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1/research/shots/skep-playlist-menu.png

Skeptic's fix lead: src/content/adapters/youtube.ts fallback(p): also set p.nav from the adapter's nav() when it returns entries. nav() calls renderGuide(), so the full guide gets drawn. Then pages the adapter does not read (playlist, channel) get the same Menu as home and watch.

## F1123 (medium): Watch comments stop at 400 threads in Sift while the original keeps loading (880)

Found by: T1-youtube. Where: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ (Comments)

Evidence: wheelc2.ts opens Comments and wheels fast (25 x 2500 px bursts). Sift/original top-level threads went 140/140, 260/260, 400/400, 400/580, 400/760. After 10 s it was 400/760. Then End x3 gave 400/780, 400/800, 400/820, and 3 slow wheel steps gave 400/840, 400/860, 400/880. Sift's view sits at its end (65507 of 66407, client height 900), with no note or button after the last comment. Each scroll still makes the hidden original load more (it shows a continuation spinner), but Sift never shows them. The cause is in src/content/adapters/youtube.ts commentList(): .slice(0, 400), with the comment 'scrolling to the end of Sift loads more', which no longer holds past 400. That code is older than this round, so this is not a regression. Shot: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1/research/shots/r11t1-comments-cap.png

Repro: cd /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1; SIFT_PORT=9461 SIFT_TAB=T1-youtube STEPS=5 bun /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/t1r11/wheelc2.ts prints 'sift 400 orig 880' at the end.

Suspected cause: src/content/adapters/youtube.ts commentList() caps at .slice(0, 400), while index.ts keeps loading more on the original. Either raise or window the cap, or stop asking for more and say the list is cut there.

Skeptic: Reproduced with my own script (opened Comments, then End plus wheel steps on Sift's view, 30 steps). Sift and original threads went 20/20, 80/80, 140/140, 260/260, 400/440, then Sift stayed at 400 while the original reached 1204, where YouTube's continuation ended. Sift's view sat at its end (65507/65507) for steps 7 to 30. Nothing came after the last comment ('Thanks for recommending <3') except Sift's switch, with no note or button. Each step still made the hidden original load another batch. The code agrees: youtube.ts commentList() does .slice(0, 400) under the comment 'scrolling to the end of Sift loads more', and loadMore() keeps scrolling the comments continuation into view. On this video, about two thirds of what the original offers cannot be reached, with no sign of it. This is silent loss of content, not polish, though only readers who scroll past 400 threads hit it. Shot: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1/research/shots/skep-comments-end.png

Skeptic's fix lead: src/content/adapters/youtube.ts commentList(): remove or raise the 400 cap (window it if rendering cost is the concern). Or, once the cap is reached, make loadMore() stop asking for more comments, and add a note at the end of Comments saying the list stops there, with a way to the original.
