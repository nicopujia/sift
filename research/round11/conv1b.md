## F1100 (medium, regression): Spanish Wikipedia 'Pan' Short version opens with the same sentence twice

Found by: T3-tools. Where: https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pan, the top of the Short version (VERSIÓN CORTA)

Evidence: After PROMPT_VERSION 12 regenerated the page (41.8 s), the first two paragraphs of Sift's Short version are identical: 'El pan se prepara principalmente con harina y agua, y la fermentación suele darle volumen y una miga esponjosa.' Both come before the hatnote 'Para otros usos de este término…'. The cached model output (read-only IndexedDB scan, key b6b5f967bffae2d5db305510e0a43845) starts with this sentence twice. So the model wrote the duplicate, and Sift shows it without removing it. 'Ver texto completo' shows the original lead with no duplicate. en Bread's Short version has 0 duplicate paragraphs. In round 10 this page's Short version had a single lead line, but in English. The language is now correct (Spanish). Shot: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-3/research/shots/R11-T3-eswiki-pan-short.png

Repro: cd /home/nico/projects/sift-test-3; export SIFT_PORT=9463 SIFT_TAB=T3-tools; bun /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/r11t3/shortpoll.ts https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pan 120; bun scripts/act.ts "eval:[...document.querySelector('sift-root').shadowRoot.querySelectorAll('main p')].map(p=>p.innerText.trim()).filter(t=>t.length>30).slice(0,3)"   (the first two strings are identical; cached, so it repeats on every load)

Suspected cause: The new prompt (v12) made the model add a lead sentence that repeats. The Short version assembly in src/content/short.ts does not drop a paragraph identical to the one before it.

Skeptic: Confirmed, medium (borderline). I loaded https://es.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pan twice (the second time with ?x=1) in the 9463 Chrome, tab 'skeptic'. Both times the Short version (VERSIÓN CORTA, ready at 4.8 s and 6.0 s from cache) opened with the same paragraph twice: 'El pan se prepara principalmente con harina y agua, y la fermentación suele darle volumen y una miga esponjosa.' The hatnote 'Para otros usos de este término…' came after both copies. Screenshot: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-3/research/shots/R11-skeptic-eswiki-pan-short.png. After clicking 'Ver texto completo', the full text starts with the hatnote and the original lead, with no duplicate. A read-only scan of the cache found the same duplicate. Key b6b5f967bffae2d5db305510e0a43845 starts with the sentence twice. The cached source text (the condense-article entry) has no duplicate. So the model wrote the sentence twice inside part 1. Sift did not add it. withChain and tryModel never join a partial answer with a retry: a stream that fails after its first text ends the chain. But Sift shows the duplicate as it is. planShort in src/content/short.ts and checked() in src/background/index.ts have no check for repeated paragraphs. The sentence is the first thing a reader sees, and it is cached for 30 days. It is not a matter of taste, but it is mild: no facts are lost or wrong. I did not check en Bread, or whether round 10 really had only one lead line.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/content/short.ts planShort(), after the parse loop's final flush(), drop any { md } segment whose normText(mdPlain(md)) equals that of an earlier md segment. Or do it before caching, in doCondense's checked() in src/background/index.ts: remove a paragraph that is identical (after normalizing) to an earlier one. Then the saved copy is clean too.

## F1101 (medium, regression): Debian wiki: the 'Text' (full-text search) button is no longer beside the search field; it sits alone at the end of the page

Found by: T4-generic. Where: https://wiki.debian.org/SourcesList (any MoinMoin page) and its search results pages

Evidence: Round 10 showed the field with 'Titles' and 'Text' side by side (/home/nico/projects/sift-test-4/research/shots/r10t4-debwiki.png). Now the field is in the bar with only 'Titles' and 'Login'. 'Text' is at y=10084 of a 10460 px page, after 'More information' and just above the footer links Wiki, Login, FrontPage (/home/nico/projects/sift-test-4/research/shots/r11t4-debwiki-textbtn.png). On the results page it is again at the end (y=655). The original keeps both buttons right after the field. The action still works when found: typing 'pinning' and clicking 'Text' gives ?action=fullsearch&context=180&value=pinning&fullsearch=Text. Enter gives the Titles search, as on the original. A reader who wants a full-text search, the useful one on a wiki, will not find the button near the field.

Repro: SIFT_PORT=9464 SIFT_TAB=T4-generic bun scripts/act.ts goto:https://wiki.debian.org/SourcesList wait:7000 'eval:(()=>{const r=document.querySelector("sift-root").shadowRoot;const b=[...r.querySelectorAll("button")].find(b=>b.innerText.trim()==="Text");return [Math.round(b.getBoundingClientRect().top+r.host.scrollTop),r.host.scrollHeight,[...r.querySelectorAll("header button")].map(x=>x.innerText.trim())]})()' prints [10084,10460,["Titles","Login"]].

Suspected cause: The F1046 change made MoinMoin's #header search the bar search (SEARCH_PLACE now includes #header, plus the new div#header branch in generic.ts that visits the header's children after the title). The bar search takes one submit button (Titles). The form's second submit (Text) is left behind and rendered later with the header's leftover links, so it ends up at the end of main. Lead: when the bar search's form has more than one submit, keep the others with the field in the bar, or leave the whole form in place.

Skeptic: Reproduced twice on https://wiki.debian.org/SourcesList. The eval printed [10084,10460,["Titles"]] both times. The bar shows the Search field, 'Titles' and 'Login' (Login is a link, not a header button). The 'Text' button sits in a div.row.actions at the end of main. It comes after 'CategoryPackageManagement' and just before the footer links Wiki, Login, FrontPage, RecentChanges, FindPage, HelpContents (screenshot: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-4/research/shots/skeptic-debwiki-text.png). On the original page with Sift off, #searchinput, #titlesearch and #fullsearch sit side by side at the top right, all at y=1 (/home/nico/projects/sift-test-4/research/shots/skeptic-debwiki-orig.png). Round 10's shot /home/nico/projects/sift-test-4/research/shots/r10t4-debwiki.png shows 'Titles' and 'Text' together under the field, so this is a regression. The action still works once found. I typed 'pinning' in the bar field and clicked 'Text', which went to ?action=fullsearch&context=180&value=pinning&fullsearch=Text. On that results page, 'Text' is again near the end (y=4199 of a 4576 px page), with only 'Titles' in the header. The cause is in the code. findSearch() in src/content/extract/generic.ts (around lines 6497-6520) picks one button from the form: the first submit, since neither button's name matches SEARCH_BUTTON. It marks only that button as used, so #fullsearch is left for the normal walk and lands with the header's leftover links. The full-text search is the main search on a wiki, and it still works, but a reader would not find it at the end of a 10,000 px page. Medium severity holds.

Skeptic's fix lead: In findSearch() in src/content/extract/generic.ts, after choosing btn, collect the form's other visible plain submit buttons (pick.form submits other than btn). Mark them in ctx.used. Expose them on the Field as an optional extra-submits list, for example f.moreSubmits = [{ref, label}], and render them in the bar next to the main submit in app.tsx's search bar component. This is an additive optional field in model.ts. The smaller alternative: when pick.form has more than one plain submit, keep the field and all its submits in place as a form in main instead of moving only the field and one button to the bar.
