## F1122 (medium): Spanish home: an Indonesian title gets an Indonesian one-line description instead of a Spanish one

Found by: T1-youtube. Where: https://www.youtube.com/?persist_hl=1&hl=es (home list one-liners)

Evidence: lang es-ES/es-ES. The entry by 'Boram Tube Stories' (11:07, 410 M views) shows 'Koleksi video Boram tentang hari-hari yang menyenangkan'. Its title is 'Koleksi video hari-hari menyenangkan Boram!'. The line is a rewrite in Indonesian, with no lang attribute. The other 22 lines in the batch are Spanish ('Cinco preguntas que no debes responder…' for a Persian title, 'Eggdog de vacaciones'). The same video on the English home gets 'Video collection of Boram's fun days'. It showed in 2 Spanish loads (the second from the cache). The cache was empty at the start (the worker was reinstalled), so the current build (PROMPT_VERSION 12) made the line. Shot: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1/research/shots/r11t1-es-home-indonesian-line.png

Repro: cd /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1; SIFT_PORT=9461 SIFT_TAB=T1-youtube W=60 bun /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/t1r11/lines.ts 'https://www.youtube.com/?persist_hl=1&hl=es'. Look for a Latin-script line in a language other than Spanish (here 'Koleksi video Boram tentang hari-hari yang menyenangkan'). The feed is personalized, so the entry may differ. Reset with ?persist_hl=1&hl=en.

Suspected cause: lineFits passes a same-script line when entryLang cannot reliably tell Indonesian from the page language, and then falls back to 'es'. A cached hit is checked again only with scriptFits and vetLine, not for language. A check that the line's language differs from the page's while matching the title's language could catch this.

Skeptic: Confirmed by the stored line and by running Sift's own check. I could not put it on screen myself: in two Spanish home loads (lang es-ES/es-ES, 23 entries), the personalized feed had no Boram entry, and all lines were Spanish. But Sift's IndexedDB cache in this profile holds the value 'Koleksi video Boram tentang hari-hari yang menyenangkan', written 2026-10-27T05:27Z. The English line 'Video collection of Boram's fun days' is a separate entry. In the service worker, chrome.i18n.detectLanguage on that line returns isReliable true, id 100%. I ran src/background/text.ts with that detection: scriptFits(line, latin, title) is true, and entryLang(line, 'es', det, output=true) returns 'es'. So lineFits passes it. Indonesian has no FUNCTION_WORDS list, so entryLang falls back to the page's language. A German line with a reliable 'de' detection is caught (returns 'de'). So any reliably detected Latin-script language without a word list passes. Cached hits get only scriptFits and vetLine, so the line shows again on revisits. The reporter's shot matches.

Skeptic's fix lead: src/background/text.ts entryLang: in output mode, when the detection is reliable, names a Latin-script language other than the page's, and that language has no FUNCTION_WORDS list, return the detected language instead of the page's (or add word lists such as id, tr, pl). Also in src/background/index.ts, check cached hits with lineFits (language), not only scriptFits and vetLine.

## F1124 (high, regression): Mirror home headline line turns the murder victim into the charged murder suspect

Found by: T2-news. Where: https://www.mirror.co.uk/ (home headline lines; works.md line 58 said the rewritten home lines were faithful)

Evidence: Sift line: 'A man who died after being found in a wheelie bin is named as a murder suspect, who has been charged'. Original card: 'Man who died after being found in wheelie bin named as murder suspect charged'. Article lead: 'A man who died after being found seriously injured inside a wheelie bin in Plymouth has been named, as a 36-year-old suspect is charged with his murder. Mohamed Abotaka … accused of the murder of Ali Barzai.' So the line says the dead man is the charged suspect. That is the opposite of the story. The other 21 rewritten Mirror lines I read are faithful.

Repro: SIFT_PORT=9462 SIFT_TAB=T2-news bun scripts/act.ts goto:https://www.mirror.co.uk/ wait:30000 'eval:[...document.querySelector("sift-root").shadowRoot.querySelectorAll("a")].filter(a=>/wheelie bin/.test(a.innerText)).map(a=>a.innerText+" | "+a.href)'  then open https://www.mirror.co.uk/news/uk-news/man-who-died-after-being-37807551 and read the first paragraph. The line is cached, so it comes back on every visit.

Suspected cause: The source headline packs two clauses with no punctuation ('…named | murder suspect charged'). The news-line rewrite reads it as 'named as murder suspect'. vetLine does not check the line against the card's standfirst or the article's lead, which would settle who was charged.

Skeptic: Reproduced on two loads. The Sift line reads 'A man who died after being found in a wheelie bin is named as a murder suspect, who has been charged'. The original card reads 'Man who died after being found in wheelie bin named as murder suspect charged', with no standfirst. The article lead says the dead man, Ali Barzai, was named and a 36-year-old, Mohamed Abotaka, is charged with his murder. So the rewrite takes the wrong reading of an ambiguous headline and states it as fact, which reverses who is the victim and who is the suspect. It is also longer than the headline it replaces. I count this as high severity. Calling it a regression is doubtful: this headline is new and was not among the 23 lines that works.md checked.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/background/index.ts, the news path in vetLine (around line 703) should keep the title, not the rewrite, when the line has as many words as the headline or more. NEWS_SYSTEM (around line 93) should also say that a headline already short, or ambiguous about who did what, is kept as written rather than resolved.

## F1137 (medium, regression): Short version can begin with the echoed instruction 'The text is in English. Write in English.'; nothing strips it

Found by: R1-extractor. Where: src/background/index.ts:839 (new language line in the condense prompt); src/background/text.ts:868-878 (stripPreamble) via checked() at index.ts:887

Evidence: Real extension (port 9468, PROMPT_VERSION 12, fresh cache) on fx/core-budget.html: the shown Short version starts with the paragraph 'The text is in English. Write in English.' and then a model note. A bun run of stripPreamble on 'The text is in English. Write in English.\n\n<real summary>' and on the Spanish 'El texto está en español. Escribe en español.\n\n…' returns both unchanged. Seen once, on a fixture whose text is placeholder. How often the model echoes it on real articles is not measured, to avoid AI cost.

Repro: cd /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad && bun strip.ts

Suspected cause: ai15 added 'The text is in X. Write in X.' to the user message. checked() removes an echoed 'Title:' line (dropTitleLine) but has no rule for an echoed language line, and NOTE_* patterns do not match it.

Skeptic: Confirmed, medium-low. How often it happens is unmeasured. The real extension's Short version on fx/core-budget.html begins 'The text is in English. Write in English.' and then a model note. The profile is shared with the reporter, so this was probably the reporter's cached result, not a fresh model call. I ran the full cleaning chain the extension uses: keepQuotes(dropUnsupported(stripPreamble(dropTitleLine(...)))). On an English output that starts with the echoed line, as its own paragraph or as its own line, and on the Spanish one, the line survives every time. The line is new this round (background/index.ts:839; absent in sift-r10-base). The only sighting is on a placeholder fixture.

Skeptic's fix lead: src/background/index.ts checked() (next to dropTitleLine): drop a leading line or paragraph that matches the injected language instruction exactly (`The text is in ${langName(want)}. Write in ${langName(want)}.`). Or move the language instruction into the system prompt so it cannot be echoed as content.
