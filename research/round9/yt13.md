## F920 (medium, regression): Tab never loads more entries in a list (home, search, comments), while the original loads more as focus moves

Found by: T1-youtube. Where: YouTube home, search results, and watch-page Comments (Sift's main view, keyboard only)

Evidence: Home: after focusing main, 60 Tabs left Sift at 21 entries. Focus wrapped to the header after about 25 Tabs ('Sign in' at 30, 'Menu' at 60). End then loaded 45. Search: 50 Tabs left it at 40 entries, and End gave 60. Comments open: 60 Tabs left it at 20 threads ('sift 20 orig 20') and focus wrapped to Search. With Sift off on the same pages, Tab through the original loads more: home 21 -> 45 grid items by 100 Tabs, and comments 20 -> 40 threads by 150 Tabs. End, PageDown and the wheel still load more in Sift. A keyboard user who only Tabs cannot get past the first page.

Repro: cd /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1; SIFT_PORT=9461 SIFT_TAB=T1-youtube N=60 bun /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/t1r9/tabhome.ts https://www.youtube.com/ (prints Sift list size every 10 Tabs, then End). Comments: same env, N=60 bun .../t1r9/tabcomments.ts. Original for comparison: bun .../t1r9/origtabhome.ts and .../t1r9/origtabcomments.ts (they switch Sift off with Alt+Shift+S, Tab 150 times, and switch Sift back on).

Suspected cause: The F820 fix in this merge: the host scroll listener in src/content/index.ts now returns early when tabAt > userScrollAt, so no focus-driven scroll loads more on any list until the reader scrolls. Before, a focus scroll near the end loaded more, as F820's evidence shows for Up next (inferred for the other lists). Lead: limit the Tab exception to a list that has content after it in an open section (Up next before Comments), or end each loadable list with an explicit 'More' control (Block.more) that Tab reaches.

Skeptic: Confirmed, medium, a regression from the F820 fix. Home in Sift: main focused, then 60 Tabs. The list stayed at 21 entries every 10 Tabs, and focus wrapped to the header ('Menu' at 30, 'Search' at 60). End then loaded 45. Watch page Comments opened with Enter, then 60 Tabs: 'sift 20 orig 20' throughout, and focus wrapped to Search twice. Original with Sift off: on home, Tab took the grid from 21 to 45 items by 100 Tabs. In comments, Tab took the threads from 20 to 40 by 150 Tabs. The cause is in the code: the host scroll listener in src/content/index.ts (around line 2520) has 'if (tabAt > userScrollAt) return;'. Tab sets tabAt (onSiftKey, line 894), and only wheel, touch, pointerdown or a scrolling key set userScrollAt. So after any Tab, a focus-driven scroll never loads more. Home and search have nothing after the list, so the F820 reason (reaching Comments after Up next) does not apply there. Inferred and not tested: a screen reader's browse-mode scrolling also loads nothing once the reader has pressed Tab.

Skeptic's fix lead: In the host scroll listener in src/content/index.ts, keep the Tab exception only for a list that has more content after it in Sift's view (Up next before Comments, i.e. youtube.loadMore would pick Up next). Otherwise let a focus scroll near the end load more as before. Or end each loadable list with a focusable 'More' control (Block.more) that Tab reaches and that calls loadMore.

## F921 (medium): After Up next navigation, the screen reader is told the previous video's title (and once 'YouTube' after Back)

Found by: T1-youtube. Where: YouTube watch page: Up next link + Enter, then browser Back; Sift's status live region (div.sr[role=status])

Evidence: Six runs of t1r9/nav2.ts from watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ. Going to kWBGcnYZWw4 and to -OfA04BD4GA (2 of 5 navigations to another video), the status region said 'Rick Astley - Never Gonna Give You Up (Official Video) (4K Remaster)', the title of the page just left. Meanwhile the new page showed YouTube's sign-in notice with H1 'YouTube', and for 1-2 s Sift still showed the old H1 under the new URL. In the first run, Back to dQw4w9WgXcQ announced 'YouTube' instead of the restored title (the H1 still said 'YouTube' at +3 s). In the other 5 runs, Back announced the right title. This works.md line 50 claim ('Back restores… the title announcement') held in 5 of 6 runs here, with load average about 14.

Repro: cd /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1; for n in 0 1 2 3 4 5; do SIFT_PORT=9461 SIFT_TAB=T1-youtube N=$n bun /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/t1r9/nav2.ts; done. It loads the watch page, focuses the Nth Up next link, presses Enter, prints h1 and status= every second for 9 s, then goes Back and prints for 8 s. Look for a status equal to the previous page's title.

Suspected cause: src/content/index.ts, navigation handling around lines 2185-2203. When the new page's title equals prevTitle (YouTube still shows the old title), titleWait starts a 2.5 s timer that then says store.page.title even if it is still the old title. Under load YouTube takes longer than 2.5 s to swap the title. This code was not changed by the merge. Lead: never announce a title equal to prevTitle. Keep waiting until it changes (or a notice replaces it), with a longer cap.

Skeptic: Confirmed for forward navigation, medium. With Up next open on watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ, I focused an Up next link to another video and pressed Enter. In 4 of 8 runs at load average about 2-3, Sift's status region said 'Rick Astley - Never Gonna Give You Up (Official Video) (4K Remaster)', the title of the page just left. This happened for Zi_XLOBDo_Y, pIgZ7gMze7A, yPYZpwSpKmA and -Go4OgFNNOg, at +1.5 to +2.5 s. Sift's H1 and document.title were already 'YouTube' (the sign-in notice), and nothing announced the new page afterwards. The other 4 runs announced 'YouTube'. The Back part did not reproduce: in my 6 runs, Back always announced the restored title. The status showed 'YouTube' at back+1s only as leftover text from the earlier announcement. The code allows it: in src/content/index.ts around lines 2190-2197, the titleWait timer says store.page.title after 2.5 s without checking that it differs from prevTitle. And youtube.ts watch() (around line 683) names an error-screen page from document.title, which still holds the previous video's title just after an in-place navigation. The bot check is the site's, but announcing the old title is Sift's own error.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/content/index.ts (navigation handling around 2190-2200), never say a title equal to prevTitle after a navigation: keep waiting until it changes or a notice replaces it, with a longer cap, and do not let a stale title clear titleWait. In youtube.ts watch(), on the error screen, use document.title only when it was set for this video (as loadedTitle is on first load), otherwise 'YouTube'.

## F922 (medium): Menu: Enter on 'Show more' or 'Show less' sends keyboard focus back to Close at the top of the dialog

Found by: T1-youtube. Where: YouTube Menu dialog (any page), keyboard

Evidence: menukb2.ts: 9 Tabs to 'Show more', then Enter. Entries went 12 -> 14 (Playables, Memberships), but focus moved to 'Close'. 11 Tabs back to 'Show less', then Enter: entries 14 -> 12, focus again on 'Close'. The same happens with a mouse click (menu.ts: 'Show more: n=14 … focus=Close'). To reach the new entries, a keyboard user must Tab through the dialog again.

Repro: cd /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1; SIFT_PORT=9461 SIFT_TAB=T1-youtube bun scripts/act.ts goto:https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ wait:15000; SIFT_PORT=9461 SIFT_TAB=T1-youtube bun /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/t1r9/menukb2.ts

Suspected cause: The Menu dialog is rebuilt when the guide expands, and focus goes to the dialog's first control. Lead: after the re-render, put focus back on the control the reader activated (by its ref or key), now labeled Show less or Show more.

Skeptic: Confirmed twice, medium (keyboard focus loss). On my tab, I opened Menu with Enter, pressed Tab 9 times to reach 'Show more', then Enter: entries went from 12 to 14, and focus moved to 'Close'. Then 11 Tabs to 'Show less', Enter: entries went from 14 to 12, and focus again moved to 'Close'. The original differs: with Sift off, in YouTube's guide, Enter on the 'Show more' paper item moved focus to the first new entry, 'Playables'. The next Tabs went to 'Memberships', then 'Show less'. Cause: nav() in youtube.ts keys each entry by its element ('n:'+refOf). 'Show more' and 'Show less' are different elements, so the focused li is replaced. refocusNow in src/content/index.ts (around line 355) then takes the '.layer' branch and focuses the sheet's first control (Close). It never reaches the spot.near fallback, which runs only after that branch.

Skeptic's fix lead: In refocusNow (src/content/index.ts ~355), when the lost spot was inside the open sheet, try spot.near (or the control at the same index or label) inside the layer before focusing the layer's first control. Or in youtube.ts nav(), give the guide's expander and collapser one stable key, so the same element stays focused while its label changes from Show more to Show less.
