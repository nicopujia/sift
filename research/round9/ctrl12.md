## F901 (high): A contextual 'Unsaved changes' save bar never appears in Sift, so changed settings cannot be saved or discarded

Found by: T6-fixtures. Where: http://127.0.0.1:8799/fx/r9-savebar.html (new fixture; a Shopify/WordPress-style admin page)

Evidence: After a change (Wed toggled, or 'Later' on the cut-off), the original shows a sticky bar 'Unsaved changes' with Discard and Save. Shot: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-6/research/shots/r9t6-savebar-dirty-original.png. The page's #savebar is visible (hidden=false), and its log has 'change day Wed'. Sift 2.5-4 s later has no 'Unsaved' text and no Save or Discard button, in the page or in the Menu sheet (Menu holds only Loafly Admin, Products, Settings). Shot: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-6/research/shots/r9t6-savebar-dirty-sift.png. The original's Save gives toast 'Settings saved' and log 'save {...}'. In Sift there is no way to save. Leaving the dirty page also triggers the page's beforeunload prompt. Harness probes: the bar is dropped whenever it sits outside <main> (static or sticky). Inside <main> it is kept, except when it is sticky and has id 'savebar'. The sift-r8-base bundle gives the same outline, so this is not a regression.

Repro: SIFT_PORT=9466 SIFT_TAB=T6-fixtures bun /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/r9t6/go.ts goto:http://127.0.0.1:8799/fx/r9-savebar.html wait:3000 click:Wed wait:2500 "eval:!document.getElementById('savebar').hidden" "sx:[...R.querySelectorAll('button')].some(b=>/^(Save|Discard)$/.test(b.innerText.trim()))". It prints true then false. Afterwards switch to the original (Alt+Shift+S) and click Discard, so the beforeunload prompt does not block the next navigation.

Suspected cause: Controls outside <main> are dropped as page chrome (inMain/isChrome in generic.ts). A sticky bar whose id holds 'bar' is also dropped inside main. A bar that appears after a change and holds Save or Discard buttons is the page's main action. It should be kept, for example in the top bar or as a sticky region of Sift's view.

Skeptic: Reproduced twice on fresh loads of r9-savebar.html in Sift. Clicking Wed logs 'change day Wed' and makes the page's #savebar visible (hidden=false). Sift's view still has no 'Unsaved' text and no Save or Discard button. The Menu sheet holds only Close, Loafly Admin, Products and Settings. On the original (Alt+Shift+S), the bar shows 'Unsaved changes' with Discard and Save, and Discard works (log 'discard'). Shot: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-6/research/shots/skeptic-savebar-dirty-sift.png and skeptic-savebar-original-dirty.png. Variant probes: moved into <main> and static, the bar is kept and shows Discard|Save. Inside <main> but sticky (even with id renamed to 'sb2'), it is dropped. So the sticky/fixed 'small bar' rule drops it, and the claim's 'id savebar' detail is not the factor. Outside main and static, it was also dropped in my probe, but that probe relied on a re-extract after a style change, so I am less sure of it. Sift already rescues the analogous payBar and bulkBar ('2 selected · Mark as shipped') but has no rule for a save bar. Real apps use this pattern (Shopify Polaris ContextualSaveBar, Discord's 'unsaved changes' bar), so settings cannot be saved in Sift. High.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/content/extract/generic.ts, add a saveBar(el) predicate next to payBar/bulkBar (~line 563). It should match a short bar (tlen < 200, 1 to 4 buttons) with a Save/Save changes/Update/Publish button plus a Discard/Cancel/Reset partner, or text like 'unsaved changes'. OR it into the sticky/fixed `keep` list (~line 3780). Also accept it in payBarsOutside (~line 628) so a bar outside <main> is pulled in. Render it at the top or bottom of Sift's view and replay clicks on the original.

## F902 (high): A custom spinbutton (role=spinbutton) becomes a plain button: its value is never shown and arrow keys do nothing

Found by: T6-fixtures. Where: http://127.0.0.1:8799/fx/r9-savebar.html, 'Order cut-off (hour, 24 h)': <span role=spinbutton aria-valuenow=18 aria-valuetext='18:00'> with Earlier/Later buttons

Evidence: Sift renders 'Earlier | [button] Order cut-off (hour, 24 h) | Later'. The text '18:00' appears nowhere in Sift's view. Clicking Later changes the page to '19:00' (log 'change cut 19'), but Sift still shows no value. Focusing Sift's 'Order cut-off' button and pressing ArrowUp leaves the page at 18:00 with an empty log. On the original, ArrowUp/ArrowDown on the spinbutton change it, and the value is visible. The harness outline for both the merged and the sift-r8-base bundle is 'actions: Earlier | Order cut-off (hour, 24 h) | Later', so this is not a regression. Shot: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-6/research/shots/r9t6-savebar-sift.png

Repro: SIFT_PORT=9466 SIFT_TAB=T6-fixtures bun /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/r9t6/go.ts goto:http://127.0.0.1:8799/fx/r9-savebar.html wait:3000 "click:Order cut-off (hour, 24 h)" press:ArrowUp wait:600 "eval:document.getElementById('cut').getAttribute('aria-valuetext')" "sx:R.querySelector('.sift').innerText.includes('18:00')". It prints "18:00" (unchanged) and false.

Suspected cause: generic.ts toField handles role=slider as a range field (aria-valuenow, valuetext) at ~line 1944, but not role=spinbutton. That falls through to toAction and becomes a button named by its label. Treat spinbutton like slider (a number or range field showing aria-valuetext, with ArrowUp/ArrowDown replayed on the page element).

Skeptic: Reproduced twice. Sift renders 'Earlier | [button] Order cut-off (hour, 24 h) | Later'. The value '18:00' appears nowhere in Sift's text (no /\d\d:00/ match), and there is no spinbutton, number or range field in Sift's root. Focusing Sift's 'Order cut-off' button and pressing ArrowDown, ArrowUp, ArrowUp leaves the page at 18:00 with an empty __log. Clicking Later changes the page to 19:00 (log 'change cut 19'), but Sift still shows no value, so the user changes the setting blind. On the original, focusing #cut and pressing ArrowUp gives 19:00 and the value is visible. Code confirms the cause. In generic.ts, toField handles role=slider (~line 1944) and controlInto's isFieldish (~line 2053) lists slider, but neither handles spinbutton, so it falls to toAction. Custom spinbuttons are common (React Aria date and time segments, MUI pickers, steppers). High.

Skeptic's fix lead: In generic.ts, add role === "spinbutton" to isFieldish in controlInto (~line 2053). In toField, handle it the same way as the slider branch (~line 1944): value from aria-valuenow, valueText from aria-valuetext, plus min and max. A 'number' kind would also do. The rendered field must show valueText and replay ArrowUp/ArrowDown (and typed digits) as keydown events on the original element through proxy.ts, as is done for sliders.

## F903 (high): An element whose id or class is 'adv' (short for 'advanced') is dropped as an ad: the 'Advanced' section and its Minimum order field are lost

Found by: T6-fixtures. Where: http://127.0.0.1:8799/fx/r9-savebar.html, <details id="adv"><summary>Advanced</summary> with the 'Minimum order ($)' number field

Evidence: Sift's view has no 'Advanced' summary and no 'Minimum order' field. R.querySelectorAll('details,summary') is empty. The original shows '▸ Advanced' (shot /home/nico/projects/sift-test-6/research/shots/r9t6-savebar-dirty-original.png). Harness probes: renaming id='adv' to 'q' brings back 'section: Advanced (collapsed) / form: fields=[number:Minimum order ($)]'. Opening the details, renaming the summary or adding a paragraph does not help. A <p class="adv-note"> inside main is also dropped. The sift-r8-base bundle gives the same result, so this is not a regression.

Repro: SIFT_PORT=9466 SIFT_TAB=T6-fixtures bun /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/r9t6/go.ts goto:http://127.0.0.1:8799/fx/r9-savebar.html wait:3000 "sx:/Advanced|Minimum order/.test(R.querySelector('.sift').innerText)" prints false. Harness: cd /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/r9t6 && bun runjs.ts http://127.0.0.1:8799/fx/r9-savebar.html "document.querySelector('#adv').id='q'" shows the section, and without the JS it is missing.

Suspected cause: AD_TOKEN in src/content/dom.ts includes the bare word 'adv' ((^|[\s_-])(ads?|adv|advert|…)). tokens() also splits camelCase ('advSearch' reads as 'adv-search'), so 'adv' ids and classes for 'advanced' (adv, adv-search, advOptions) read as ads. Require 'adv' to come with another ad sign, or drop it from the token list.

Skeptic: Reproduced on the fixture and on copies served from my own scratch server (port 8813). With id="adv", Sift's view has no 'Advanced' and no 'Minimum order' (regex false), and 0 details/summary elements. A copy with id="q" shows the section (true, 2 elements). class="adv-search" and id="advOptions" both drop it (false, 0), so camelCase splitting in tokens() makes the problem wider. Renaming the id in the live page did not bring it back. Sift seems to remember dropped elements, so only a fresh load shows the difference. The original shows '▸ Advanced'. Cause confirmed in src/content/dom.ts: AD_TOKEN includes a bare 'adv', and isAd() tests tokens(el) against it. An 'Advanced search' or 'Advanced options' form section is lost entirely. Note that 'adv' is a real ad marker on Italian sites ('box-adv', 'adv-300x250'), so dropping it outright would regress them. High to medium: a whole form section goes.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/content/dom.ts AD_TOKEN, replace the bare 'adv' with narrower forms, such as adv[-_]?(box|slot|unit|banner|wrap|container|\d) and box-adv. Or keep it but have isAd() reject a bare-'adv' match when the element holds form controls (input/select/textarea), a <summary> or a heading, or its text starts with 'Advanced' or 'Avanzad'.

## F904 (high): 'Copy link', 'Copy code' and 'Copy' buttons are removed everywhere, so a share link the page offers only through 'Copy link' cannot be had in Sift

Found by: T6-fixtures. Where: http://127.0.0.1:8799/fx/r9-activation.html (new fixture; a shared-folder page like Drive or Dropbox: 'Anyone with the link can view.' [Copy link], 'Invite code: BREAD-42' [Copy code])

Evidence: The original: clicking 'Copy link' puts 'https://drive.example/s/bakery-recipes' on the clipboard (log 'copied') and shows 'Link copied'. 'Copy code' puts 'BREAD-42' on the clipboard (log 'code copied'). In Sift neither button exists. The outline goes 'p: Shared with 4 people…', 'p: Invite code: BREAD-42', then Export list | Open help | Remove all. Clipboard stays 'EMPTY' and the log stays []. The share URL is not shown anywhere on the page, so in Sift the link cannot be obtained at all. Harness probe: 'Copy', 'Copy code' and 'Copy link' are dropped, while 'Copy invite code' and 'Copy URL' are kept. Shot: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-6/research/shots/r9t6-activation-sift.png. This is old behavior, not a regression.

Repro: cd /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/r9t6 && SIFT_PORT=9466 SIFT_TAB=T6-fixtures bun copycode.ts orig; SIFT_PORT=9466 SIFT_TAB=T6-fixtures bun copycode.ts sift. orig prints clipboard BREAD-42 and log ['code copied']. sift prints 'Copy code not found' and clipboard EMPTY. bun activation.ts orig / sift shows the same for 'Copy link'.

Suspected cause: SKIP_EXACT in generic.ts (~line 101) drops 'copy( code| to clipboard)?' and 'copied' everywhere, and SHARE_LABEL in dom.ts drops 'copy link'. Both are meant for code-block toolbars and article share bars. Drop them only inside a share bar, next to a pre/code block, or in an article. Keep them on app pages, where the copied value is often not on screen.

Skeptic: Reproduced on r9-activation.html. Sift's outline has 'p: Shared with 4 people…' and 'p: Invite code: BREAD-42', then Export list | Open help | Remove all. Neither 'Copy link' nor 'Copy code' exists, and 'drive.example' is nowhere in Sift's root. copycode.ts: Sift gives 'Copy code not found', clipboard EMPTY, log []. Original gives clipboard BREAD-42, log ['code copied'], and shows 'Code copied'. I also checked a more realistic case, a Drive-like role=dialog 'Share "Bakery recipes"' with 'Anyone with the link can view.' and Copy link | Done (scratch fixture). Sift shows the dialog with Close and Done, but drops 'Copy link'. So the share URL cannot be had even in a share dialog. Cause in code: usefulControl() returns false for SHARE_LABEL matches ('copy link', dom.ts) and SKIP_EXACT matches ('copy( code| to clipboard)?', generic.ts ~line 101). It keeps them only inside a form or for closesPanel, and dialogs get no exception. The lost 'Copy code' matters less, because BREAD-42 stays visible as text. The lost 'Copy link' loses the page's main action. High.

Skeptic's fix lead: In generic.ts usefulControl() (~lines 847 and 856), stop dropping 'copy link', 'copy' and 'copy code' everywhere. Drop them only when shareTarget(el) is true or they sit in an article or story share bar (ctx.inArticle). Drop 'copy'/'copy code' only when next to a pre/code block (the CODE_CHROME context). Keep them in dialogs (ctx.inDialog) and on non-article app pages. Alternatively, remove 'copy link' from SHARE_LABEL in dom.ts and rely on shareTarget() for share bars.
