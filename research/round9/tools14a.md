## F960 (medium): Google Maps route: picking a travel mode with the arrow keys moves focus to the top of the page

Found by: T3-tools. Where: https://www.google.com/maps/dir/Ferry+Building,+San+Francisco,+CA/Coit+Tower,+San+Francisco,+CA/data=!4m2!4m1!3e2?hl=en (Sift's travel mode radios)

Evidence: Seen 3 of 3 times. Focus was on Sift's checked radio ('Walking 24 min', then 'Transit 17 min') and ArrowUp or ArrowDown was pressed. The mode changed (!3e2 to !3e3 and back). Focus then left the radio: 'none' at about 600 ms, then <main> ('Ferry Building to Coit Tower …') at 830-1140 ms. A keyboard user trying modes must Tab back through the map portal and its buttons after each change. Original with Sift off: focus on the Transit button (role=radio), Enter, and focus stays on 'button role=radio Transit' after the route updates (!3e3). OpenStreetMap's Car/Bicycle/Foot radios keep focus in Sift ('input Bicycle' after ArrowUp, 2.4km 0:12). Shot: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-3/research/shots/R9-T3-gdir-mode-kbd-focus.png

Repro: cd /home/nico/projects/sift-test-3; export SIFT_PORT=9463 SIFT_TAB=T3-tools; S=/tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad; bun scripts/act.ts "goto:https://www.google.com/maps/dir/Ferry+Building,+San+Francisco,+CA/Coit+Tower,+San+Francisco,+CA/data=!4m2!4m1!3e2?hl=en" wait:8000 "eval:(()=>{const r=document.querySelector('sift-root').shadowRoot;const c=[...r.querySelectorAll('input[type=radio]')].find(i=>i.checked);c.focus();return c.labels[0].innerText})()"; (sleep 0.5; bun scripts/act.ts press:ArrowUp >/dev/null) & bun $S/r7t3/poll.ts 8 "(()=>{const r=document.querySelector('sift-root').shadowRoot;const a=r.activeElement;return (a?a.localName+' '+(a.labels?.[0]?.innerText||a.innerText||'').slice(0,20):'none')+' | '+(location.href.match(/!3e\\d/)||['none'])[0]})()" 100   (prints 'input Walking…' then 'none', then 'main Ferry Building to Co…')

Suspected cause: The mode change is a single-page navigation (URL changes). Sift treats it as a new page and moves focus to main/H1, even though the user's focus was on a control that is still present.

Skeptic: Reproduced 3 of 3 times on the Google Maps walking route at 1280 px, Sift on. Focus was on Sift's checked radio. ArrowUp from Walking moved focus to Transit (native radio behavior). About 350 to 900 ms later, as the URL changed (!3e2 to !3e3), focus moved to <main> ('Google Maps…' / 'Ferry Building to Co…'). ArrowDown from Transit to Walking did the same: focus was 'none', then main at 1419 ms, URL !3e2. A focus trace shows the jump to main lands at the same moment as the URL change, so it comes from Sift's navigation handling, not from the page. Original with Sift off: arrow keys do nothing on Google's role=radio buttons. Enter on Transit changed the mode to !3e3, and focus stayed on 'button radio 17 min' throughout. So in Sift, a keyboard user loses their place after every mode change, while the original keeps it. Medium is fair.

Skeptic's fix lead: src/content/index.ts render(), the `if (navigated && page.blocks.length)` branch (~line 2241). The radio is re-rendered on the navigation, so `before` is disconnected, which hits the `a === host && (!before || !before.isConnected)` → main().focus() path. When the user acted within the last few seconds (store.actAt) and `before` was a Sift control, first try its re-rendered twin (twinOf/refocus by data-ref, then by label in the same radio group), and fall back to main only when none exists. Or do not treat a URL change written by the user's own radio/select action as a new page.

## F961 (medium): Google Maps place: a 'Sponsored By …' ad with 'Why this ad?' and a tour link stays in Sift's place panel

Found by: T3-tools. Where: https://www.google.com/maps/place/Coit+Tower/@37.8023991,-122.4083971,17z/data=!3m1!4m6!3m5!1s0x8085808c40000001:0xde85b80121f2dd44!8m2!3d37.8023949!4d-122.4058222!16zL20vMDIycTBu?hl=en

Evidence: Seen on 3 of 3 loads, with different advertisers. Between the place summary and the Address row, Sift shows H2 'SponsoredBy Viator', a 'Why this ad?' button, the link 'San Francisco City Highlights Open-Air Private Driving Tour IRR 499 · 5.0 (18) Free cancellation' and '5.0 stars 18 Reviews'. Other loads showed 'SponsoredBy Tripadvisor' (Coit Tower Hike … App Tour) and 'SponsoredBy Trip.com' (Private San Francisco City Tour by Open-Air Jeep). The original marks it as an ad: h1 'Sponsored By Viator'. The spec says ads are removed. Shot: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-3/research/shots/R9-T3-gmaps-place-ad.png

Repro: cd /home/nico/projects/sift-test-3; export SIFT_PORT=9463 SIFT_TAB=T3-tools; bun scripts/act.ts "goto:https://www.google.com/maps/place/Coit+Tower/@37.8023991,-122.4083971,17z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m6!3m5!1s0x8085808c40000001:0xde85b80121f2dd44!8m2!3d37.8023949!4d-122.4058222!16zL20vMDIycTBu?hl=en" wait:9000 "eval:(()=>{const t=document.querySelector('sift-root').shadowRoot.querySelector('main').innerText;const m=t.match(/Sponsored[^\n]*\n[\s\S]{0,160}/);return m?m[0]:'none'})()"

Suspected cause: Google labels the block with an h1 'Sponsored' plus 'By <advertiser>', not an ad container class or iframe, so Sift's ad filter does not catch it.

Skeptic: Reproduced on 2 of 2 loads of the Coit Tower place page (both with Tripadvisor as advertiser). Sift's main shows h2 'SponsoredBy Tripadvisor', a 'Why this ad?' button, 'Coit Tower Hike Personalized Self-Guided App Tour IRR 5 · 3.0 (2) Free cancellation' and '3.0 stars 2 Reviews', between the place heading and the Address row. In the original, the block is an h1 with aria-label 'Sponsored · By Tripadvisor' and a 'Sponsored' span. It is an ad, and the spec says ads are removed. Medium is fair.

Skeptic's fix lead: src/content/extract/generic.ts, the sponsored-section filter (~lines 3753-3763, SPONSORED_HEAD at line 62). SPONSORED_HEAD is anchored, so 'Sponsored · By X' and 'SponsoredBy X' fail the test. Allow an optional '( · )?by <name>' tail, and read the heading's aria-label too. Also, the `!el.querySelector("h1,main,…")` exemption keeps this container because its sponsored heading is itself an h1. Do not exempt it when that h1 is the sponsored heading.
