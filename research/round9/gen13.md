## F955 (high, regression): GitHub Status history drops the preview incidents of the later months: 6 of 9 incident links on load, 19 of 25 after expanding October

Found by: T4-generic. Where: https://www.githubstatus.com/history

Evidence: On load, the original shows 3 incidents per month for October, September and August: 9 visible incident links. Sift has 6. All 3 August previews are missing ('Elevated rate of errors for OpenAI models provided by Copilot', 'Disruption with GitHub Billing', 'Incident with Copilot AI Model Providers'), and 'August 2026' is followed directly by '+ Show All 27 Incidents'. After '+ Show All 19 Incidents' (October), the original shows 25 links and Sift 19: the 3 September previews also vanish ('Copilot Code Review is unable to complete reviews', 'Disruption with billing information updates', 'Incident across several services'). Both runs gave the same result. Expanding August (33/33) and then September (44/44) brings everything back. Round 8 works.md line 34 had 25 incident links in Sift and 25 in the original after expanding October. Outline: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-4/research/shots/r9t4-ghhistory.txt, shot /home/nico/projects/sift-test-4/research/shots/r9t4-ghhistory-exp.png

Repro: SIFT_PORT=9464 SIFT_TAB=T4-generic bun scripts/act.ts goto:https://www.githubstatus.com/history wait:8000 'eval:(()=>{const r=document.querySelector("sift-root").shadowRoot;const s=new Set([...r.querySelectorAll("main a")].filter(a=>/\/incidents\//.test(a.href)).map(a=>a.href));const o=[...document.querySelectorAll("a")].filter(a=>/\/incidents\//.test(a.href)&&a.getBoundingClientRect().height>0);return [o.length,s.size]})()' 'click:+ Show All 19 Incidents' wait:2500 (same eval again). It prints [9,6], then [25,19].

Suspected cause: Unverified. The preview incidents of a month whose 'Show All' toggle is collapsed (aria-expanded=false) seem to be treated as hidden or folded content. Only the first month (or the month just expanded) keeps its previews. The 'Incident across several services' entry also gets a Short version ('Show full text'). A round 8 change to collapsed regions or post bodies in src/content/extract/generic.ts may be involved.

Skeptic: Reproduced twice with the given eval: [9,6] on load, then [25,19] after '+ Show All 19 Incidents'. The claim is wrong on one point: the incidents are not dropped. They are folded. Sift's shadow DOM has <details class=section data-key=s:r10><summary><h2>August 2026</h2></summary></details>, which is collapsed and renders its children only when opened. Clicking that summary shows the 3 August incidents ('Elevated rate of errors for OpenAI models provided by Copilot', and so on). After October is expanded, both 'September 2026' and 'August 2026' become collapsed sections. The original shows all three previews of every month in plain view. Cause, from the code: the incident bodies are div.message with text between <br><br> and no <p>. So the F832 fallback in findArticle() (src/content/extract/generic.ts, about line 3203, 'No story in <p>s: one set as runs of text between <br>s') picks one long incident body as the page's article. That is why 'Incident across several services' (and, after expanding, the Oct 5 'Incident with Actions') gets 'Show full text'. article() then sets ctx.afterArticle = true, and itemsInto() (about line 4704) folds every later list as 'more stories after the article', titled by the month H2. This fits a round 8 regression: works.md line 34 had 25/25. Severity is medium rather than high, because the content can be reached by opening the month, but nothing signals that the bare month heading holds incidents.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/content/extract/generic.ts findArticle(), in the F832 <br>-run fallback, skip a text run whose box sits inside one of several same-signature sibling entries (for example, an ancestor within 3 levels with a sibling of the same signature(), like div.incident-data), or apply the postGroup/inPostGroup/resultList filters to the fallback's candidates. Otherwise a list of entries is never one article. As a backstop, itemsInto() should not fold a list whose siblings include the list that holds ctx.articleEl.

## F956 (high): Cloudflare bot-check page: Sift hides the 'Verify you are human' checkbox, so a person in Sift cannot pass the check

Found by: T4-generic. Where: https://stackoverflow.com/questions/927358/how-do-i-undo-the-most-recent-local-commits-in-git?answertab=trending (when Cloudflare challenges the visit)

Evidence: One load of Stack Overflow got Cloudflare's 'Performing security verification' page. Sift showed only 'stackoverflow.com', the H2 'Performing security verification' and the paragraph about bots: no checkbox, no hint, nothing to act on (/home/nico/projects/sift-test-4/research/shots/r9t4-so-trending.png). With Sift switched off, the same page shows the Turnstile widget 'Verify you are human' with its checkbox (/home/nico/projects/sift-test-4/research/shots/r9t4-so-cf-orig.png). The page's light DOM has no iframe, only a 0x0 input, so the widget seems to sit in a closed shadow root that Sift's challenges.cloudflare/turnstile portal rule cannot see. I did not click or otherwise interact with the check. A later load passed on its own.

Repro: When stackoverflow.com (or any Cloudflare site) answers with 'Performing security verification': SIFT_PORT=9464 SIFT_TAB=T4-generic bun scripts/act.ts shot:cf-sift press:Alt+Shift+S wait:1500 shot:cf-orig press:Alt+Shift+S. cf-sift has no checkbox, and cf-orig shows 'Verify you are human'. Do not click the checkbox.

Suspected cause: Unverified. extract/generic.ts portals Turnstile only when it finds an iframe from challenges.cloudflare.com. On the new challenge page the widget is in a closed shadow root, so nothing is portaled. A challenge page (title 'Just a moment...', a cf-turnstile-response input) could show the original instead, or portal the widget's host element.

Skeptic: Stack Overflow was not challenged in my run. I reproduced the same Sift behavior on two other Cloudflare challenge pages, without clicking anything. On https://www.timeanddate.com/ (title 'Just a moment...', input[name=cf-turnstile-response] present), Sift's outline is only 'H1 Just a moment…' and 'p: Nothing to show yet.' (research/shots/skeptic-cf-sift.png). With Sift off, the original shows the 'Verify you are human' checkbox widget (research/shots/skeptic-cf-orig.png). It was the same on https://www.allrecipes.com/: Sift shows 'Just a moment... / Nothing to show yet.', and the original shows the checkbox widget (research/shots/skeptic-cf-orig2.png). Both pages have 0 iframes in the light DOM. The widget is a 650x69 (or 300x72) div whose only light children are div > input#cf-chl-widget-…_response (0x0), so the frame sits in a closed shadow root. The iframe-src rules (EMBED_HOST/TOOL_HOST 'challenges.cloudflare|turnstile') never match. A person in Sift gets a dead page with no control and no hint. They must find the switch to pass the check. The behavior is Sift's, not the site's, and no bot check is worked around. Not a regression: research/site-sweep.md already recorded 'Just a moment... / Nothing to show yet.' on Allrecipes.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/content/extract/generic.ts (the portal and embed detection that uses EMBED_HOST/TOOL_HOST), treat a Cloudflare challenge widget as a tool portal: the host element of input[name=cf-turnstile-response] or [id^=cf-chl-widget] (its sized ancestor), even when no iframe is visible. Or detect a challenge page (that input plus a title like 'Just a moment...' or 'Performing security verification') in src/content/index.ts and show the original page, or a notice with a 'Show original to verify' action, instead of 'Nothing to show yet.'

## F957 (medium, regression): GOV.UK passport renewal guide shows a 14-year-old date ('Oct 10, 2012') under the H1 that the page never shows

Found by: T4-generic. Where: https://www.gov.uk/renew-adult-passport and /renew-adult-passport/renew

Evidence: Sift's outline: 'H1 Renew or replace your adult passport' and then 'p: Oct 10, 2012' (<p class="small">). The original's text has no '2012' anywhere. The date comes from the page's JSON-LD ("datePublished": "2012-10-10T20:37:20+01:00"; dateModified 2015-01-12; meta govuk:updated-at 2026-09-09). The page has no meta article date and no <time>. A reader is told this passport guide dates from 2012 when it was updated in 2026. The round 8 F832 change added ldDate() as the byline date fallback (diff around line 1497, '+ if (!when) when = ldDate();'), so this is new with the merge. I did not run the old build. /council-tax and /pay-council-tax show no date. Outline /home/nico/projects/sift-test-4/research/shots/r9t4-govoverview.txt

Repro: SIFT_PORT=9464 SIFT_TAB=T4-generic bun scripts/act.ts goto:https://www.gov.uk/renew-adult-passport/renew wait:5000 'eval:[document.querySelector("sift-root").shadowRoot.querySelector("main h1 + p")?.innerText, /2012/.test(document.body.innerText)]' prints ['Oct 10, 2012', false].

Suspected cause: src/content/extract/generic.ts byline(): the new ldDate() fallback takes the first datePublished of any *Article in JSON-LD. GOV.UK guides use an Article schema with the first-publish date. The fallback could require that the visible page shows a date, or prefer dateModified, or apply only to news articles.

Skeptic: Reproduced on both URLs. On /renew-adult-passport/renew and /renew-adult-passport, the eval prints ['Oct 10, 2012', false]: Sift's p after the H1 says 'Oct 10, 2012', and '2012' appears nowhere in the original's text. The page has no <time>. Its JSON-LD has @type 'Article' with datePublished 2012-10-10T20:37:20+01:00 and dateModified 2015-01-12, while meta govuk:updated-at is 2026-09-09. In the code, byline() (src/content/extract/generic.ts, about line 6645) falls back to ldDate() ('TOI has no meta date and no <time>, F832'). ldDate() (about line 6575) accepts any @type ending in 'Article' and returns its datePublished. GOV.UK's plain 'Article' schema carries the 2012 first-publish date. Sift shows it with no label, as if it were the guide's date, on official guidance updated in 2026. The likely result is that every GOV.UK guide shows its 2012-2015 first-publish date. Medium: it misleads the reader about how current the guidance is.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/content/extract/generic.ts ldDate(), accept only news and blog types (/(NewsArticle|ReportageNewsArticle|AnalysisNewsArticle|BlogPosting|LiveBlogPosting)$/) rather than any '*Article'. Also, or instead, use the JSON-LD date only when its year (or the formatted date) appears in the page's visible text. Prefer dateModified, or a meta '*updated-at', when it is newer.
