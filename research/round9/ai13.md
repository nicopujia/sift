## F927 (high): Guardian headline line invents a sequence: 'The Zombies made Time of the Season after the singer’s death was reported in Rolling Stone'

Found by: T2-news. Where: https://www.theguardian.com/international, the culture card linking to /culture/2026/oct/26/how-the-zombies-made-time-of-the-season

Evidence: The original title is '‘My death was reported in Rolling Stone’: how the Zombies made Time of the Season'. The standfirst says 'A year after we broke up, we heard that two bands had started touring the US as the Zombies… because I’d been killed in a car crash'. So the death report came after the band broke up, not before the song was made. Sift's line makes the false report the setting for writing the song, a fact the source does not support. The same list has a milder case: '‘Bolso shows all signs of fascism’: the journalist who…' became 'A journalist who predicted Brazil’s future called Bolso a fascist…', which loses the quote marks and makes the claim stronger. The other 58 Guardian pairs I checked are faithful. Pairs: /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/r9t2/g-intl-pairs.txt

Repro: SIFT_PORT=9462 SIFT_TAB=T2-news bun scripts/act.ts goto:https://www.theguardian.com/international wait:75000 'eval:[...document.querySelector("sift-root").shadowRoot.querySelectorAll("a")].filter(a=>/Zombies/.test(a.innerText)).map(a=>a.innerText)'. Then goto the article and read [data-gu-name=standfirst]. The line is cached, so a reload shows the same text.

Suspected cause: A teaser with a quote ('My death was reported…') plus a 'how X made Y' title. The model joins them into a causal or time sequence. NEWS_SYSTEM forbids naming an unnamed speaker, but not inventing an order or relation between the quote and the title's event.

Skeptic: Reproduced. The line is cached. Sift shows 'The Zombies made “Time of the Season” after the singer’s death was reported in Rolling Stone'. The article says Time of the Season came out after the band split, and the false death report came 'well over a year since we’d broken up', when other bands toured as the Zombies. So the line invents a false order of events, drops the quote marks and the first person ('My death'), and adds 'the singer'. I did not find the 'Bolso shows all signs of fascism' line in Sift's links on this load, so that milder case is not verified.

Skeptic's fix lead: src/background/index.ts NEWS_SYSTEM: add a rule that a quoted teaser and the title's event stay separate. Never link them with after, before, because or so unless the headline does. Also enforce the existing 'keep a direct quote in quotation marks' rule in post-processing: keepQuotes or a check that a line's first-person quote was not turned into a third-person fact.

Suspected cause: The article text sent to condense changes while the page loads (lead and video blocks arrive late), so stableText gives a new key each time. A Short version already on screen is replaced instead of kept. Same cause as F829.

Skeptic's fix lead: src/background/index.ts doCondense key and src/content/index.ts: build the condense key only from the article's own body paragraphs, leaving out related-video and promo blocks (keep those out of the article blocks in generic.ts). Once a Short version is on screen, do not replace it within the same visit unless the body text changed substantially.

## F930 (medium): Guardian Lula Short version shows a stray 'Title: …' line repeating the headline

Found by: T2-news. Where: https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/oct/26/brazil-lula-vow-democracy-always-election-win (Short version, right under the lead photo)

Evidence: The first paragraph of the Short version reads 'Title: ‘Democracy always’: Lula vows no return to dictatorship in Brazil after election win'. It repeats the H1 with a 'Title:' label. The saved condense entry (00:04:22) begins '## Title: ‘Democracy always’…', so the model echoed its input field and Sift shows it. 1 of the 21 Short versions in the cache has this. The rest of the Short version is faithful (0.49 of the words; 64.2m/59.8m, 51.79%, at least 10m, 1964–85 and 'sacanagem' match). Shot: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-2/research/shots/r9t2-g-lula-title.png

Repro: SIFT_PORT=9462 SIFT_TAB=T2-news bun scripts/act.ts goto:<url> wait:60000 outline. The first p after the img is 'Title: …'. It is cached, so every visit shows it.

Suspected cause: stripPreamble and the heading-dedupe path do not catch a heading or line of the form 'Title: <headline>' (or 'Título:'). It should be dropped when it repeats the H1.

Skeptic: Reproduced. The line is cached. Right under the lead image, the Short version's first paragraph reads 'Title: ‘Democracy always’: Lula vows no return to dictatorship in Brazil after election win', which repeats the H1. In code, stripPreamble (src/background/text.ts:444) skips any paragraph that starts with '#'. So the saved '## Title: …' line is never dropped. This is a low-to-medium defect: a stray, misleading line, not a factual error. One other problem in the same Short version that the tester did not report: 'The article says it did, because abstention…' speaks about the article.

Skeptic's fix lead: src/background/text.ts stripPreamble (or the doCondense output): drop a first heading or paragraph that matches /^#*\s*(Title|Título|Titre|Titel|Headline)\s*:/i, or that repeats the page H1 after normalizing.
