Shots: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-2/research/shots/r9t2-g1-home.png (Sift), /home/nico/projects/sift-test-2/research/shots/r9t2-g1-home-orig.png (original)

Repro: SIFT_PORT=9462 SIFT_TAB=T2-news bun scripts/act.ts goto:https://g1.globo.com/ wait:30000 'eval:(()=>{const R=document.querySelector("sift-root").shadowRoot;const sl=[...R.querySelectorAll("a")].map(a=>a.href.split("#")[0]);const fp=[...document.querySelectorAll(".feed-post-body-title a, a.feed-post-link")].map(a=>a.href.split("#")[0]);return [R.querySelectorAll("a").length,fp.length,fp.filter(h=>!sl.includes(h)).length]})()' gives a missing count equal to the feed count.

Suspected cause: The extractor seems to take the first big widget (the svelte election map, or the 'Mais lidas' box) as the page's main content and drops the bastian feed (.feed-post items) below it, or it treats the feed as a lazy, infinite region it never reads.

Skeptic: Reproduced at both widths. At 1280: 16 feed links in the original, 16 missing from Sift (20 Sift links in total). Likely cause, checked in code: the feed container's class 'bstn-fd bstn-fd-csr bstn-fd-recommended' matches NOISE_TOKEN in src/content/dom.ts:800 through 'recommended' (tested: true). So generic.ts:3726 drops the whole feed as noise.

Skeptic's fix lead: src/content/dom.ts NOISE_TOKEN / src/content/extract/generic.ts:3726: do not drop a 'recommended' container as noise when it holds the page's main list of story links (for example, most of the page's headline links, or the main column). Or exclude the recommend token when the element is the largest link list on a home page.

## F926 (high): G1 at 1280 px: Sift has no sign-in control

Found by: T2-news. Where: https://g1.globo.com/ (and its articles) at 1280 px

Evidence: The original header has a visible BUTTON 'Entrar com Conta Globo' (aria-label 'Botão de login Conta Globo') at 1042,5, 226x43. Sift's header at 1280 px has only BUSCAR, Pesquisar and Menu, and the Menu dialog has no sign-in either. At 640 px Sift does show 'Botão de login Conta Globo' (shot /home/nico/projects/sift-test-2/research/shots/r9t2-z200-13.png), so the control is lost only in the desktop layout.

Repro: SIFT_PORT=9462 SIFT_TAB=T2-news bun scripts/act.ts goto:https://g1.globo.com/ wait:12000 'eval:[...document.querySelector("sift-root").shadowRoot.querySelectorAll("button,a")].map(e=>e.innerText).filter(t=>/Entrar|login|Conta/i.test(t))' gives []; 'eval:[...document.querySelectorAll("button")].filter(b=>/Conta Globo/.test(b.innerText)).length' gives 2 in the original.

Suspected cause: At desktop width the login button sits in a header region (or a fixed header copy) that Sift drops as page chrome. The mobile layout puts it where Sift keeps it.

Skeptic: Reproduced on two loads at 1280. Sift has no Entrar, login or Conta control in its header, and Sift's Menu dialog (151 controls) has none either. The only match there is 'Entre em contato'. With Sift off, the original's fixed header visibly shows 'Entrar com Conta Globo' at 1042,5 (226x43), and it is the top element at that point (shot research/shots/skep-g1-orig-1280.png). At 640, Sift does show 'Botão de login Conta Globo' (research/shots/skep-g1-640.png). The G1 article at 1280 also has no sign-in in Sift: the tab walk goes Skip, buscar, Pesquisar, Menu.

Skeptic's fix lead: src/content/extract/generic.ts header and account-control extraction: keep the sign-in button of a fixed or sticky desktop header (header-touchpoint-wrapper) even when that header is a duplicate or a scrolled-away copy. Choose the visible copy instead of dropping both.

## F931 (medium): G1 (Portuguese): Sift's own controls are a mix of English, Spanish and Portuguese

Found by: T2-news. Where: https://g1.globo.com/ and https://g1.globo.com/mundo/noticia/2026/10/26/trump-fala-sobre-eleicao-no-brasil-pareceu-mais-uma-eleicao-fraudada.ghtml (html lang pt-br)

Evidence: On the G1 article, Sift's buttons are: 'Skip to content', 'Reproduzir', 'Back 10s', 'Ahead 10s', 'Unmute', 'Tela cheia', 'Ver texto completo', label 'VERSÃO CURTA'. The portals are named 'Interactive area from the original page: … Press Enter to use it, Tab to leave.' The switch is named 'Vista Sift', which is Spanish. On G1's search page the submit button is 'Search'. The page text and Short version stay Portuguese. Spec: 'Keeps the page's language.'

Repro: SIFT_PORT=9462 SIFT_TAB=T2-news bun scripts/act.ts goto:<g1 article> wait:15000 'eval:[...document.querySelector("sift-root").shadowRoot.querySelectorAll("button")].map(b=>b.innerText||b.getAttribute("aria-label")).join(" | ")'. The switch name is in /home/nico/projects/sift-test-2/research/shots/r9t2-z200-14.png (zoomcheck prints 'name=Vista Sift').

Suspected cause: Sift's UI strings have a Portuguese table with only some keys. The missing keys fall back to English, and the switch label falls back to Spanish.

Skeptic: Reproduced on the pt-BR G1 article. Sift's buttons are 'Skip to content | Pesquisar | Menu | Reproduzir | Back 10s | Ahead 10s | Unmute | Tela cheia | Ver texto completo …'. One portal is named 'Interactive area from the original page: … Press Enter to use it, Tab to leave.', and the switch is aria-label 'Vista Sift'. The pt table in src/content/i18n.ts:203 is partial, so skip, back10, fwd10, unmute and the portal strings fall back to English. 'Vista Sift' is set in the pt table on purpose. 'Vista' is also a Portuguese word, so that part is a weak complaint. The English fallbacks are the real defect.

Skeptic's fix lead: src/content/i18n.ts: complete the pt table (skip to content, back10, fwd10, mute and unmute, the portal labels and hints, the search submit), and check the other partial tables (fr, de, it, …) against en's keys. Optionally use 'Visão Sift' for pt.

## F932 (medium): G1 article: a lazy in-article photo is lost; only its caption shows

Found by: T2-news. Where: https://g1.globo.com/mundo/noticia/2026/10/26/trump-fala-sobre-eleicao-no-brasil-pareceu-mais-uma-eleicao-fraudada.ghtml ('Trump e Lula durante encontro na Casa Branca…')

Evidence: Sift shows the caption 'Trump e Lula durante encontro na Casa Branca, em 7 de maio de 2026 — Foto: Presidência da República' with no photo. Sift's images are only the 2 video posters. In the original, the photo is a 0x0 data:image/gif placeholder until scrolled into view. After I scrolled the original to it (Sift off), it loaded at 648x443. Back in Sift, the photo then appeared (3 images). So a Sift reader never gets it, because Sift never scrolls the original.

Repro: SIFT_PORT=9462 SIFT_TAB=T2-news bun scripts/act.ts goto:<url> wait:15000 'eval:[...document.querySelector("sift-root").shadowRoot.querySelectorAll("img")].map(i=>i.alt.slice(0,40))' has no 'Trump e Lula durante encontro'. Then bun /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/r9t2/origclick.ts '[...document.querySelectorAll("p.content-media__description")].find(e=>/Casa Branca/.test(e.innerText))' 3000 '1', and the same eval now lists it.

Suspected cause: wakeEmbeds (F830) wakes only social embed fallbacks. Lazy images whose placeholder is a 1-px data gif (with a caption beside them) are not brought into the original's view, so they never load.

Skeptic: Reproduced twice, after 20 s and after 35 s. Sift's only images are the 2 video posters, and the caption 'Trump e Lula durante encontro na Casa Branca… — Foto: Presidência da República' shows alone, with no photo. In the original, the photo is an AMP <amp-img> with src and srcset already set, but no <img> child until it is scrolled into view (1 amp-img, 0 with an img). That is a slightly different mechanism from the claimed '0x0 data:image/gif'. generic.ts has no amp-img handling (it only handles lazy <img> with loading, srcset or data-src at line ~1508).

Skeptic's fix lead: src/content/extract/generic.ts image extraction: treat <amp-img> (and similar wrappers with src or srcset attributes) as an image block using its src or srcset, even with no <img> child. Or extend the lazy-image wake (wakeEmbeds in src/content/index.ts) to scroll such placeholders into the hidden original's view.

## F933 (medium): G1 article: an empty, unnamed summary is a tab stop (axe 'summary-name', serious)

Found by: T2-news. Where: https://g1.globo.com/mundo/noticia/2026/10/26/trump-fala-sobre-eleicao-no-brasil-pareceu-mais-uma-eleicao-fraudada.ghtml

Evidence: a11y.ts finds 2 violation types: [serious] summary-name '<summary><h2></h2></summary>' and [minor] empty-heading '<h2></h2>'. tabwalk puts this nameless summary right after Menu, before the video controls, so a screen reader hears an unnamed 'collapsed' control. All 13 other target pages give 0 violations.

Repro: SIFT_PORT=9462 SIFT_TAB=T2-news bun scripts/a11y.ts <url>; SIFT_PORT=9462 SIFT_TAB=T2-news bun scripts/tabwalk.ts <url> 6 (5th stop: 'SIFT <summary>' with no text).

Suspected cause: A section whose heading has no text (G1's empty h2 above the video) becomes a collapsible details. Its summary should take a fallback name, or the section should not be collapsible.

Skeptic: Reproduced. a11y.ts finds [serious] summary-name '<summary><h2></h2></summary>' and [minor] empty-heading. tabwalk.ts stops at 'SIFT <summary>' with no name right after Menu. The details is closed by default, and opening it shows G1's own article summary ('Donald Trump afirmou sem provas…'). So a reader also never knows what the unnamed toggle hides.

Skeptic's fix lead: src/content/ui/app.tsx Section (and the section builder in generic.ts): when b.title is empty, give the summary a fallback name (for example t('summary') or the first words of its content), or render the section open and without a summary.
