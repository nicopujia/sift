## F660 (high): MDN Try it: while you type in the editor, focus jumps to Sift's main and the rest of the keys are lost

Found by: T3-tools. Where: https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/JavaScript/Reference/Global_Objects/String/toUpperCase ('Try it' portal, CodeMirror editor)

Evidence: With Sift on, typing 'console.log("hello sift".toUpperCase())' at 120 ms/key after a real click in the editor lost focus to sift-root>main in 5 of 5 runs. Focus left at keys 22, 25, 27, 28 and 31, so the editor kept 'console.log("hello sift")', '…".)', '…".to)', '…".toU)' or '…".toUppe)'. The rest of the keys went nowhere. The same text at 40 ms/key left 'console.log("hello sift".toUpper)'. A mouse edit then Run printed 'undefined', from 'console.log("hello sift".toUpperC)' (shot /home/nico/projects/sift-test-3/research/shots/R6-T3-mdn-tryit-run.png). It also happened when typing began 40 s after load, so it is not a late Short version. A MutationObserver on Sift's shadow root logged a Sift childList re-render at 6495 ms, then the editor's focusout at 6504 ms. Control with Sift off, same script: the editor holds the full 'console.log("hello sift".toUpperCase())' and focus stays in it. Plain letters ('let abcdefghijklmnopqrstuvwxyz = 1') did not lose focus in 2 of 2 runs. Short statements did not either ('x = "a".b', 'f(1).b'). I do not know whether this is new: works.md says a mouse edit updated the output, but not how long the edit was.

Repro: cd /home/nico/projects/sift-test-3; S=/tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/r6t3; SIFT_PORT=9463 SIFT_TAB=T3-tools bun scripts/act.ts goto:https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/JavaScript/Reference/Global_Objects/String/toUpperCase wait:9000; SIFT_PORT=9463 SIFT_TAB=T3-tools bun $S/mdntype.ts 120 'c'; SIFT_PORT=9463 SIFT_TAB=T3-tools bun $S/mdntrace.ts 120 'onsole.log("hello sift".toUpperCase())'  (each line prints the editor's last line and the deep focus path; it switches to 'focus=sift-root>main' partway through). Control: press Alt+Shift+S first, and the same two commands keep the full text.

Suspected cause: A Sift re-render while focus is inside a portaled original element (the editor) moves focus to main. Likely candidates are the new focus-keeping paths in index.ts: keepSiftFocus/main()?.focus() when portalOf(active) && !enteredPortal(), or focus restore after paint.

Skeptic: Reproduced in 4 of 4 runs with Sift on, typing at 120 ms per key on the toUpperCase page after a real click in the editor. Focus moved to sift-root>main around 'toU'/'toUp'/'toUppe', and every later key was lost. In one run focus went to body first, then main. A main-world MutationObserver showed the cause. Sift's main removed 2 nodes (h2 'Try it' and figure.portal-wrap, the editor's portal), the editor fired focusout with relatedTarget null 13-46 ms later, and Sift added both nodes back 330-950 ms later. Control with Sift off kept the full 'console.log("hello sift".toUpperCase())' and focus stayed in the editor. My first three 'no repro' runs were void: the control's Alt+Shift+S had left Sift off for the site in that tab.

Skeptic's fix lead: Sift should never drop a portal that holds focus. In index.ts, in the paint path just before setPortals/paint, if the old page's portal contains the deep active element and the new extraction lacks it, keep the old portal block in place or defer the repaint until focus leaves. Also, when focus falls to body because a portal was hidden, restore it to the original element after the portal comes back, not main()?.focus(). Separately, find why the extractor drops the 'Try it' interactive-example for a moment while the runner re-renders its output (extract/generic.ts portal detection).

## F661 (high): Wikipedia header search: picking a suggestion (click or ArrowDown+Enter) does not open the article

Found by: T3-tools. Where: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bagel and /wiki/Rye_bread, 'Search Wikipedia' field in Sift's top bar

Evidence: After typing 'pumpernick', Sift's sheet shows 'Pumpernickel Type of rye bread'. ArrowDown focuses it, and Enter gives this: the page field becomes 'Pumpernickel', the sheet closes, focus goes back to Sift's field, and the URL stays /wiki/Bagel 11 s later. This held in 3 of 3 runs: 2 keyboard and 1 mouse click on the suggestion. On /wiki/Rye_bread, clicking 'Focaccia Oven-baked Italian leavened flatbread' also stayed on /wiki/Rye_bread. A second Enter in the field then opens /wiki/Pumpernickel. Original with Sift off: the same ArrowDown+Enter opens https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pumpernickel at once. The page's option is <li role=option class=cdx-menu-item> holding <a href='…/w/index.php?title=Special:Search&search=Pumpernickel&wprov=acrw1_0' class=cdx-menu-item__content>, while Sift's suggestion is a <button>.

Repro: cd /home/nico/projects/sift-test-3; SIFT_PORT=9463 SIFT_TAB=T3-tools bun scripts/act.ts goto:https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bagel wait:10000 "type:header input=>pumpernick" wait:2500 press:ArrowDown press:Enter wait:8000 url  (prints /wiki/Bagel; expected /wiki/Pumpernickel). Mouse: replace press:ArrowDown press:Enter with "click:.sheet button".

Suspected cause: The suggestion replays a click on the li[role=option], which only selects it, instead of on its inner a.cdx-menu-item__content link that navigates.

Skeptic: Reproduced. On /wiki/Bagel, typing 'pumpernick' and pressing ArrowDown focused Sift's suggestion button 'Pumpernickel Type of rye bread' (data-ref to the page option). Enter set the field to 'Pumpernickel', and the URL was still /wiki/Bagel 8 s later. On /wiki/Rye_bread, a mouse click on 'Focaccia Oven-baked Italian leavened flatbread' also stayed on /wiki/Rye_bread. Control with Sift off: a real click in the page's search field, typing, then ArrowDown+Enter opened https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pumpernickel. In index.ts fieldList, each action's ref is the li[role=option] (refOf(o)), not its inner a.cdx-menu-item__content link. A click replayed on the li only selects it.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/content/index.ts fieldList(), use the option's own link as the action ref when it has one: ref: refOf(o.querySelector('a[href]') ?? o). Or, in proxy.ts click replay, send the click to the single a[href] that fills an option. Then Enter and click both navigate, as on the original.

## F512 (still open, partly; original repro in /home/nico/projects/sift/research/round5/*.md)

Recheck by T3-tools: Fixed on walking and on explicit driving. On /maps/dir/Ferry+Building,+San+Francisco/Golden+Gate+Bridge/data=!4m2!4m1!3e2, clicking 'via Mason St' gave H1 'Ferry Building to Golden Gate Bridge' and H2 'via Mason St'. There was no 'Saved' and no 'Continue your trip', no sheet, and no duplicated section. 'via The Embarcadero N' (!5i2) behaved the same. Driving with !3e0, clicking 'via Lombard St and US-101' gave the right H1 and H2. Still wrong on the bare URL /maps/dir/Ferry+Building,+San+Francisco/Golden+Gate+Bridge ('Best travel modes' selected), 2 of 2 runs: after picking the alternative, H1 still names the first route. First run: H1 'via Bay St and US-101' with H2 'via US-101'. Second run: H1 'via US-101' with H2 'via Lombard St and US-101'. The original's h1s were 'via US-101|via Lombard St and US-101|6:48 PM—7:30 PM'. No noise or duplicate. Shots: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-3/research/shots/R6-T3-gdir-alt.png, /home/nico/projects/sift-test-3/research/shots/R6-T3-gdir-best-alt.png

Skeptic: Still partly not fixed, as claimed. On the bare /maps/dir/Ferry+Building,+San+Francisco/Golden+Gate+Bridge ('Best travel modes'), 2 of 2 runs: before the click Sift's H1 is 'via US-101'. After clicking 'Driving 19 min 5.2 miles via Bay St and US-101' (URL gains !5i1), H1 is still 'via US-101' at the top of the view. Then comes a link 'via US-101 · Driving · 19 min · 5.4 miles · Best route', and the chosen route sits under H2 'via Bay St and US-101'. The original's visible h1s are 'via US-101 | via Bay St and US-101 | 7:23 PM—8:05 PM'. The noise part is fixed: no 'Saved', no 'Continue your trip', no sheet, no duplicate section. Cause: titleH1's 'several alike' test needs every plain h1 to share className and parent class. The transit h1 ('7:23 PM—8:05 PM') differs, so it falls back to plain[0]. The remaining issue is a misleading page title, medium severity.

Skeptic's fix lead: In extract/generic.ts titleH1(), return null (use document.title, 'Ferry Building to Golden Gate Bridge') when two or more plain h1s are alike, or when they head sibling entries of one list, instead of requiring plain.every(alike). Alternatively, prefer the h1 of the selected or expanded route.
