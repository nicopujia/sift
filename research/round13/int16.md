## F1301 (high): On a page with a shown original, a popup that takes focus right after a Sift click, or by select() or dialog.show(), still closes at once

Found by: R2-interaction. Where: src/main/index.ts:215-221 (only HTMLElement.prototype.focus, and only while `clicking`); src/content/index.ts:3457-3463 (sift-click 'end' is sent right after proxy.click, before the page's microtasks, frames and timers run)

Evidence: Fixture /fx/r13int7-focusasync.html (with an iframe, so Sift shows a portal). Each button appends a share popup to <body>, focuses its first control and closes on focusout. In Sift: 'Share in a microtask' logged ['share open micro focus copyurl','share closed'], and the same for the frame and timeout variants. 'Share with the link selected' (input.select(), no focus() call) logged ['share open sel focus url','share closed']. 'Share in a dialog' (a non-modal <dialog> with show()) logged ['dialog open focus dcopy','dialog closed']. In all five, Sift's focus went back to the button pressed and no sheet showed. On the original each logged only '… open', with document.activeElement on the popup's control. Control case /fx/r13int7-focusasync-noportal.html (the same page without the iframe): all five stay open in Sift and show as a sheet, because <body> is inert there. Also seen: a clipboard.js copy on /fx/r12int-clipjs-portal.html leaves document.activeElement on its textarea during the copy, so select() really moves focus out of Sift on portal pages.

Repro: SIFT_PORT=9467 ./scripts/chrome.sh start; cd /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/r13int7; FX=http://127.0.0.1:8799/fx/r13int7-focusasync.html SIFT_PORT=9467 SIFT_TAB=R2-interaction bun focusclose.ts sift 'Share in a microtask' (also 'Share in a frame', 'Share after a timeout', 'Share with the link selected', 'Share in a dialog'); the same with 'orig' in place of 'sift' for the original; FX=http://127.0.0.1:8799/fx/r13int7-focusasync-noportal.html for the control.

Suspected cause: The F1200 guard is narrow. It fails only a synchronous HTMLElement.prototype.focus() inside proxy.click. React 18, Vue and Headless UI focus in a microtask, effect or frame after the event, and by then 'end' was sent. input.select() and dialog.show() focus natively without calling focus(). The new popup is not inert until the MutationObserver's refreshShield runs, which then blurs it. Lead: while Sift is shown, have the page-world helper treat any element that is not shownAt() and sits in a <body> child Sift has not shielded yet (neither data-sift-inert nor a portal ancestor) like an inert one, at all times rather than only during a click. Wrap select() (use setSelectionRange without focus there) and HTMLDialogElement.show() the same way. Or keep 'clicking' set until a timeout and a frame after the click.

Skeptic: Confirmed; I would rate it medium to high. I used my copies of the reporter's scripts, port 9467, tab 'skeptic'. On /fx/r13int7-focusasync.html, focusclose.ts in Sift logged [... open, ... closed] for all five buttons: micro, frame, later, sel and dlg. Each time Sift's focus went back to the button pressed and no sheet showed (layer null). I reran micro, sel and dlg with a 4 s wait and got the same result. The open line itself names the popup's control ('focus copyurl', 'focus url', 'focus dcopy'). So focus really left Sift, and the popup closed when the shield made it inert. With Sift off, all five logged only '... open', with document.activeElement on #copyurl, #url or #dcopy. The control page without the iframe (r13int7-focusasync-noportal.html) kept all five open in Sift as a sheet with Sift's focus inside it. So the shown portal is the cause. The code matches. In src/main/index.ts:215-221 the guard wraps only HTMLElement.prototype.focus and only while clicking > 0. In src/content/index.ts the sift-click 'end' is sent in the finally right after proxy.click, so microtasks, frames and timers run after the flag is cleared. select() and dialog.show() focus natively, so they bypass the wrapper even during the click. Two of the five (sel and dlg) are synchronous inside the click, so they are squarely F1200's own case.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/main/index.ts, stop gating the focus guard on `clicking`. While Sift is shown, treat any connected target that is not shownAt() and sits outside the elements Sift exempts as inert: skip the native focus and dispatch sift-focus. Wrap HTMLInputElement/HTMLTextAreaElement.prototype.select (set the selection without focusing) and HTMLDialogElement.prototype.show the same way. A narrower option is to keep `clicking` set until a setTimeout(0) plus a requestAnimationFrame after 'end' in H.act (src/content/index.ts ~3457). That covers microtask and frame focus, but not a later timeout.

## F1303 (high, regression): A held submit on a '#' or 'javascript:' form runs the page's submit handler once a second (one Enter adds the item 8 times), delays a plain '#' form until 8 s after load, and never sends it while the page is still loading

Found by: R2-interaction. Where: src/content/proxy.ts:593-616 (submit: the hold and its retry, which re-runs the whole submit) and src/content/proxy.ts:631-636 (holdsSubmit: any '#' or 'javascript:' form, until load plus 8 s, readyState only)

Evidence: Fixture /fx/r13int7-heldsubmit.html. Form 'Add an item' has action="javascript:void(0)" and a submit listener that appends the item without calling preventDefault, as such forms usually do because their action does nothing. In Sift, typing Milk and pressing Enter once, 1.8 s after load (load ended at 0.35 s), logged 'added Milk at 1768', then 2771, 3775, 4780, 5785, 6788, 7794 and 8798. That is 8 list entries. On the original the same Enter logged one entry. Each retry calls submit() again, which presses Enter in the field again, clicks the submit button again and fires change and search again. The hold catches each new submit event because the page never cancels it, until 8 s after load. Same fixture, a plain GET form with action='#' and no script (Sift shows it as the bar search): Enter at 1.9 s navigated to ?q=milk# only at about 8.3 s. On the original, navigation started at once. /fx/r13int7-heldsubmit-slow.html is the same page plus an image whose request never completes, so readyState stays 'interactive'. There the search was still not sent 30 s after Enter. The retry polls every 500 ms for as long as the page is not 'complete'.

Repro: cd /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/r13int7; SIFT_PORT=9467 SIFT_TAB=R2-interaction bun drive.ts goto:http://127.0.0.1:8799/fx/r13int7-heldsubmit.html wait:1000 'sclick:input[type=text]' type:Milk press:Enter wait:13000 'eval:window.__log' → 8 'added Milk' entries; original: goto, press:Alt+Shift+S, 'oclick:#item' type:Milk press:Enter wait:5000 'eval:window.__log' → 1 entry (press Alt+Shift+S again after). Delay: goto the same, wait:800 netlog 'sclick:input[type=search]' type:milk press:Enter, then url every 2 s → NAV GET ...?q=milk# only about 6.4 s after Enter; with r13int7-heldsubmit-slow.html no navigation after 30 s.

Suspected cause: news17's hold (F1235) applies to every form whose action starts with '#' or 'javascript:' while the page loads or within 8 s of load, with no upper bound while readyState is not 'complete'. Each retry replays the whole submit (key events, the default button's click, the submit event), so a page that handles the submit without cancelling it acts each time. Lead: hold only when the native submission would reload this document (action '#' or the current URL, not 'javascript:') and only while no page handler has acted. Retry the submission itself once, not the Enter and click with all their handlers. Cap the wait at a few seconds after DOMContentLoaded, not 'complete' plus 8 s, and show 'loading' on Sift's field while it waits.

With Sift off, the same Enter logged one entry ('added Milk at 2271'). The plain GET form with action '#' (Sift's bar search, 'Show items with'): Enter at about 1.6 s in Sift navigated to ?q=milk# only between 7.6 s and 9.6 s. With Sift off, navigation started at once. On r13int7-heldsubmit-slow.html, readyState stayed 'interactive' in Sift and there was no navigation at 17.6 s or 32.6 s. With Sift off on the same slow page (readyState interactive), Enter navigated at once. The code matches. In src/content/proxy.ts submit(), the hold prevents every submit event the page has not cancelled. Its retry calls the whole submit() again, which replays the Enter key, the default button's click and the submit event. holdsSubmit stays true for any '#' or 'javascript:' action until loadEventEnd + 8000, and the retry re-polls every 500 ms for as long as readyState is not 'complete'.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/content/proxy.ts holdsSubmit/submit: never hold a 'javascript:' action, because the browser's submission does nothing there anyway. Hold a '#' form only if the page ran no handler (check with watchReaction-style detection, or that the field value was not consumed). On retry, call f.requestSubmit(submitter) once instead of submit() and do not re-hold it. Cap the wait at a few seconds after DOMContentLoaded rather than waiting for readyState 'complete'.

## F1302 (medium): On a page with a shown original, a popup opened by a Sift checkbox or by Enter in a form field still closes at once (the F1200 guard covers only H.act)

Found by: R2-interaction. Where: src/content/index.ts:3571 (H.check → proxy.setChecked → click) and src/content/index.ts:3598 (H.submit → proxy.submit), neither wrapped in sift-click start/end

Evidence: Fixture /fx/r13int7-focuspaths.html (with an iframe). 'Send me alerts' is a checkbox whose change handler appends a 'How often' popup to <body> and focuses 'Daily'. In Sift, clicking it logged ['freq open','freq closed'], and focus stayed on Sift's checkbox with no sheet. On the original it logged ['freq open'] with focus on <button>Daily. 'New name' is a form whose submit handler appends a 'Confirm rename' popup and focuses 'Yes, rename'. Enter in Sift's field logged ['confirm open','confirm closed']. Sift's Rename button (the H.act path) logged ['confirm open'] and showed the sheet with focus on 'Yes, rename'.
