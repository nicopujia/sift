## F1311 (high): The Short version drops a step or an answer that repeats an earlier one word for word: 'Click Next to continue.' as step 4 of 5 disappears

Found by: R3-ai-background. Where: src/background/text.ts:994-1006 (dropRepeats: `const earlier = mark ? list : seen; if (earlier.has(key)) return false;`), called from src/background/index.ts:902 (checked)

Evidence: In Chrome 9469 with a Bun mock model, fx/steps.html is an install guide with an ol of 5 steps, and steps 2 and 4 are both 'Click Next to continue.'. The model's answer kept all 5 steps. Sift's Short version shows one ol with start=1 and only 4 items: 'Open the file… / Click Next to continue. / Accept the licence… / Choose the folders to watch, click Install, and wait.'. The second 'Click Next' is gone, and nothing says that a step is missing. The full text has all 5. Offline, dropRepeats also drops '4. Cover and let the dough rest for one hour.' from a bread recipe. It drops the answer 'Yes, you can bring a pet on the train.' under 'Can I bring a cat?', which leaves that question with no answer. planShort puts back only fresh or changed paragraphs and blocks, so prose that the check dropped never comes back.

Repro: bun -e 'const {dropRepeats}=await import("/home/nico/projects/sift-test-9/src/background/text.ts"); console.log(dropRepeats("1. Open the installer.\n2. Click Next to continue.\n3. Accept the licence.\n4. Click Next to continue.\n5. Click Install."))' prints the list without step 4. Script: /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/r13ai/rep3.ts. In Chrome: run r13ai/mock.ts, then r13ai/settings.ts mock, then r13ai/watch.ts steps.html 6.

Suspected cause: A list item that matches an earlier item of the same list word for word is treated as a repeat, and so is a sentence that matches an earlier one in the same section. In steps, recipes and FAQ answers the same words are often a real second step or a second answer. Possible lead: never drop list items, or drop a repeat only when it sits next to the earlier one.

Skeptic: Confirmed. Severity is medium to high. Offline, dropRepeats in /home/nico/projects/sift-test-9/src/background/text.ts removes a list item that repeats an earlier item of the same list word for word. '4. Click Next to continue.' goes from the 5-step list, and the output jumps from '3.' to '5.'. The bread step '4. Cover and let the dough rest for one hour.' goes too. In the FAQ case, the second 'Yes, you can bring a pet on the train.' goes from the same section, so 'Can I bring a cat?' is left with no answer. In Chrome 9469 with my own mock on port 8894, fx/steps.html showed the Short version with one ol with no start attribute and 4 items: 'Open the file… | Click Next to continue. | Accept the licence… | Choose the folders to watch…'. The second 'Click Next to continue.' was gone, and nothing marked it. The page text says 'The installer asks the same question twice, so do not stop after the first one.', so the Short version now contradicts itself and drops an action step. dropRepeats runs in checked() (index.ts:902) on every Short version.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/background/text.ts dropRepeats, never drop a list item: skip the repeat check when `mark` is set. Also check prose sentences only against the same paragraph, or only against the line just before it, not the whole section. The model echo from F1100 is adjacent, but a repeated FAQ answer or step is not.

## F1312 (medium, regression): F1218 regression: a byline or count line with a name ('Posted by Sam Rivers, 3 hours ago', 'Reuters, 5 min ago', 'Live: 12,400 watching') is now part of the Short version's cache key, so a revisit pays for a new model call and shows the full text until it comes

Found by: R3-ai-background. Where: src/shared/ticks.ts:64-73 (onlyTicks: the VOLATILE shortcut is gone, and only bylines split by '·', '|' or ' - ' count), used by src/background/text.ts:1141/1151 (stableText, the cache key) and :1578 (textLines, reuseShort)

Evidence: In Chrome with the mock, fx/blog.html is a post whose article has 'Posted by Sam Rivers, 3 hours ago' as a paragraph. That line reaches the text sent to the model. Visit 1 made 1 condense call. A reload made 0. After the line became '4 hours ago', the revisit made 1 new call (condense=1), and the Short version was not shown until that call ended. I rebuilt the pre-round-12 onlyTicks offline (r13ai/tickcmp.ts). It returns true for 'Posted by Sam Rivers, 3 hours ago', 'By Jane Doe, 3 hours ago', 'Reuters, 5 min ago', 'Por Juan Pérez, hace 3 horas', 'Jane Doe 3 hours ago', 'Sports desk, updated 3 hours ago' and 'Live: 12,400 watching'. The current one returns false for all of them. So stableText changes every time the relative time or the count ticks, and reuseShort refuses too, because its textLines differ.

Repro: bun /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/r13ai/stable2.ts prints 'differs' for 'By Jane Doe, 3 hours ago'→'4 hours ago' and the others. In Chrome: cp fx/blogA.html fx/blog.html; watch.ts blog.html 6, twice, which gives condense=1 then 0. Then cp fx/blogB.html fx/blog.html; watch.ts blog.html 6, which gives condense=1.

Suspected cause: onlyTicks now treats a line as ticking only if, once its VOLATILE and TICK parts are removed, every word left is a label. A byline joined by a comma or a space keeps a name ('sam rivers', 'reuters', 'live') and counts as content. Possible lead: also split byline parts at ', ' before a VOLATILE part, or drop a short line whose VOLATILE part sits at its end after a name. Keep F1218's sentence cases ('drew 9 million viewers') as content.

Skeptic: Confirmed. This is a medium regression from the F1218 fix. git diff 7bd95ba..ae0fe8f -- src/shared/ticks.ts shows that the VOLATILE shortcut was removed. I copied the round-11 onlyTicks and compared it with the current one. Old returns true and current returns false for 'Posted by Sam Rivers, 3 hours ago', 'By Jane Doe, 3 hours ago', 'Reuters, 5 min ago', 'Por Juan Pérez, hace 3 horas', 'Jane Doe 3 hours ago', 'Sports desk, updated 3 hours ago' and 'Live: 12,400 watching'. With the real stableText, the cache key changes when only the time in those lines changes. 'BBC · 3hrs ago', 'Jane Doe · 3 hours ago' and 'By Jane Doe | Updated 3 hours ago' still keep the same key. In Chrome 9469 with the mock, blogA gave condense=1 on the first visit, and the reload gave condense=0. After the line changed to '4 hours ago' (blogB), the revisit showed the full text at 1 s with condense=0 and no Short version. A new call followed (condense=1), and the Short version came at 3 s. So an article with a comma-joined byline and a relative time pays for a new call on almost every revisit. F1218's sentence case ('The final drew 9 million viewers.') is still treated as content, as intended.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/shared/ticks.ts onlyTicks, also split byline parts at a comma or colon followed by a space: add `[,:]\s+` to PART. The existing check `parts.some(p => VOLATILE.test(p) && labelOnly(p))` then accepts 'Reuters, 5 min ago' and 'Posted by Sam Rivers, 3 hours ago'. A sentence like 'The final drew 9 million viewers, a record.' stays content, because its VOLATILE part is not label-only.

## F1313 (medium): A one-day 'no Short version' marker (and a one-day keep-title) is saved again for 7 days when the page turns out to be signed in after it asked

Found by: R3-ai-background. Where: src/background/index.ts:797 (`if (hit && !ttlOf(req, pc)) pc.saved.set(key, hit)` runs for a NO_SHORT hit too), :528 (the same for KEEP_TITLE hits), :974 (the 'private' task re-saves every pc.saved entry with PRIVATE_TTL)

Evidence: In Chrome with the mock, fx/latepriv.html is an article whose header gets a 'Sign out' link 4 s after load, and the mock answer is the full text, so the result is 'not much shorter'. Visit 1, before the link appears, stored '\u0000no-short' with exp 1d, as intended ('kept for a day at most, whichever model wrote it'). On visit 2 the request hit that marker and the page then turned private. IndexedDB now holds '\u0000no-short' with exp 7d, written fresh. That article gets no Short version for a week instead of a day. KEEP_TITLE hits take the same path: F1231 made them last a day, and here they become 7 days.

Repro: Run r13ai/mock.ts and r13ai/settings.ts mock. Run r13ai/watch.ts latepriv.html 3, then r13ai/idb.ts | grep no-short (exp 1d). Run r13ai/watch.ts latepriv.html 8, then r13ai/idb.ts | grep no-short (exp 7d).

Suspected cause: pc.saved remembers every hit so that it can shorten its lifetime later. Markers that already expire in a day or 6 hours are re-saved with the 7-day private TTL, which lengthens their lifetime instead. Possible lead: never put NO_SHORT or KEEP_TITLE hits in pc.saved, or re-save with min(remaining, PRIVATE_TTL).

Skeptic: Confirmed, medium. In code, both cache-hit paths call pc.saved.set for any hit: index.ts:797 (condense, NO_SHORT included) and :528 (lines, KEEP_TITLE included). When the page turns private, the 'private' task at :974 re-saves every pc.saved entry with cacheSet(key, v, PRIVATE_TTL), which gives a fresh 7-day expiry. In Chrome 9469 with the mock, visit 1 to fx/latepriv.html (checked at 3 s, before 'Sign out' appears at 4 s) stored '\u0000no-short' with exp 1d. Visit 2 (8 s) hit the marker, and the page turned private. IndexedDB then held '\u0000no-short' with exp 7d and a new ts. A third visit renewed it again (ts moved, still 7d). So each revisit within the week extends the block, which is worse than the claim says. I checked the KEEP_TITLE path by reading the code only: it follows the same pattern at :528.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/background/index.ts, do not put marker hits in pc.saved. At :797, use `if (hit && hit !== NO_SHORT && !ttlOf(req, pc))`. At :528, do the same with `hit !== KEEP_TITLE`. Another option: in the 'private' handler (:974), skip v === NO_SHORT || v === KEEP_TITLE.

## F1314 (medium): Usage report retried without limit: while sift.pujia.ar/e fails, Sift sends the same day's report again about every 2 s of browsing, not 'one anonymous request per day of use'

Found by: R3-ai-background. Where: src/background/stats.ts:37 and :52 (every bump schedules save(), which calls flushStats()) and :71-84 (a non-ok status leaves sent[day] unset, so the next save sends again; nothing backs off)

Evidence: Offline harness with a fake chrome.storage and fetch (r13ai/statsretry.ts) runs 20 bumps 2.1 s apart. With the endpoint answering 503, stats.ts sent 20 GET reports for the same past day in about 42 s and never set sent. With 204 it sent 1. Every active user would send a request every few seconds for as long as the endpoint is down or returns 404. That contradicts README.md:51 and site/index.html:48 ('one anonymous request/report per day of use') and puts load on the owner's server just when it is failing.

Repro: timeout 120 bun /tmp/claude-1001/-home-nico/aac11194-acaf-4dad-a334-0620115b1898/scratchpad/r13ai/statsretry.ts 503 prints '20 report requests'. With argument 204 it prints 1.

Suspected cause: flushStats runs after every saved bump, with no record of when it last tried. Possible lead: remember the last attempt per day in storage, and retry at most every few hours, or on worker startup only, as onStartup already does.

Skeptic: Confirmed, medium at the low end. In src/background/stats.ts, every bump schedules save() after 2 s, and save() always calls flushStats(). When the request gets a non-ok status (:76), sent[day] stays unset. When fetch throws (:80), the loop breaks, and nothing records the attempt. Nothing backs off. I ran the reporter's fake-chrome harness with 8 bumps 2.1 s apart, all four runs side by side. The endpoint answering 503, 404 or nothing at all got 8 report requests each for the one past day, with sent={}. With 204 it got 1 request, and sent was set. So while sift.pujia.ar/e is down or returns 404, every active user sends about one request per page view, with no limit. That breaks the README's 'one anonymous request per day of use'. The same-id retry the README mentions does not cover a retry on every bump.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/background/stats.ts, store the last attempt time (for example `tried[day]` or one `lastTry` timestamp in chrome.storage.local). flushStats should skip a day it tried within the last few hours. Another option: call flushStats from save() only when today() changed since the last call, and leave retries to onStartup and onInstalled.
