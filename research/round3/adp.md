## F15 [medium] Replies are flattened into the top-level list with no way to load more, and after re-sorting the old replies show under the wrong comment
- **where**: YouTube watch page, Comments: 'N replies' and Sort
- **evidence**: (1) Clicking '731 replies' removes the button and inserts 10 reply rows as sibling li.item elements. They have the same class, no indent (margin-left 0), and no 'reply to' marker, so they read as new top-level comments. There is no 'Show more replies' or 'Hide replies', but the original has 'Show more replies'. Only 10 of 731 replies can be reached. Same with '632 replies' on another thread. (2) After expanding replies and choosing Sort > Newest, YouTube reuses the first thread element and hides the old replies (ytd-comment-replies-renderer is display:none). Sift still lists them right after the new first comment: '@Macoypogo111…', '@elijahmerciers5854…', '@Capinet…'. The original's visible order is @Macoypogo111, @blackcatpirates8134, @Ale_256. Reproduced on 2 loads. Screenshots: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-1/research/shots/R3T1-yt-replies.png, R3T1-yt-sort-stale.png
- **repro**: Watch page > Comments > '731 replies' (wait about 9 s) > Sort comments > 'Newest…'. Then list the Comments li texts.
- **suspectedCause**: The reply extractor ignores visibility of #replies and its 'Show more replies' continuation, and emits replies as plain items without nesting.
- **verdict**: Reproduced on 2 loads. After clicking '56 replies', 10 replies appear as sibling li.item rows with margin-left 0 and no reply marker. The original then shows 'Show more replies', which has no counterpart in Sift. After Sort > Newest, Sift's order was @NofilKhokar, @WindowsitoVistaXPME7, @jonathan18.10., … (the old replies). The original's visible order was @NofilKhokar, @الحريفه-ض4م, @NotMikeKowalski, … The cause: the extractor takes every ytd-comment-thread-renderer, nested replies and hidden ones included, as a flat item. A further failure I saw: clicking '731 replies' on a thread below the original's viewport hid the button, but the replies never loaded. The original's spinner sat at y=897, below the 813 px viewport, so nothing showed at all.
- **fix**: Skip threads inside hidden #replies. Nest replies under their parent item. Expose 'Show more replies' and 'Hide replies' as actions. Scroll the reply continuation into view (as loadMore does) after opening replies.

## F20 [medium] On a Spanish YouTube page, Sift shows English labels and drops the Save button
- **where**: YouTube watch with hl=es (document lang es-ES), e.g. watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ&persist_hl=1&hl=es and watch?v=9bZkp7q19f0
- **evidence**: Sift's own chrome is Spanish ('Ir al contenido', 'Buscar', 'Pausar', 'Subtítulos'), but the page shows 'Like 19 M', 'Dislike', 'H2 Description' and 'H2 Up next'. Search results show 'Channel · @SoyCeliacoNoExtraterrestre'. The original's save button is labeled 'Añadir a lista de reproducción'; Sift has no Save action (English pages have 'Save to playlist'). Seen on 2 videos.
- **repro**: Load https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ&persist_hl=1&hl=es and read the outline. (Reset afterwards with &hl=en&persist_hl=1.)
- **suspectedCause**: youtube.ts uses hardcoded labels (toAction(like,'Like'), 'Dislike', section titles 'Description','Up next','Shorts','Topics', meta 'Channel'), and the save regex is /^(save|guardar)/. amazon.ts and gmail.ts have the same hardcoded titles ('Filters','Product details','Reviews','Customers say','Saved for later','Compose').
- **verdict**: Reproduced on 2 videos with persist_hl=1&hl=es (document lang es-ES). Sift's own controls were Spanish, but the page showed 'Like 19 M', 'Dislike', H2 'Description' and H2 'Up next'. The original's save button aria-label is 'Añadir a lista de reproducción', which /^(save|guardar)/ misses, so Save is absent. The English pages had 'Save to playlist'. Code at youtube.ts:441-448, 481 and 499 hardcodes these English labels. I reset the language to hl=en afterwards.
- **fix**: Use the original buttons' own text or aria-label (or i18n t() keys) for Like, Dislike and the section titles. Find Save by its position or icon, not by an English or Spanish word.

## F21 [medium] Product page gets an AI 'Short version' of 'About this item', which adds fake H2 sections and is barely shorter
- **where**: Stand-in http://127.0.0.1:8799/fx/amazon-product (synthetic)
- **evidence**: 'About this item' shows 'SHORT VERSION / Show full text'. It adds headings h2:'Hybrid active noise cancelling', h2:'Detailed audio', h2:'Battery life and charging' and h2:'Comfortable fit'. They sit at the same level as h2:'Product details' and h2:'Reviews', so heading navigation reads them as page sections. The short text is 886 characters against about 1,100 for the 4 original bullets. The goal says no Short version on pages with controls, and this page has Color, Quantity, Add to Cart and Buy Now. Reproduced on 2 loads.
- **repro**: Load the stand-in, wait 6 s, and list h1-h4 in Sift's shadow root.
- **suspectedCause**: The condense rule treats a long feature-bullet list inside a product page as prose, and Markdown headings in the model output are rendered as h2 without nesting under the section level.
- **verdict**: Rests on the synthetic stand-in, though real Amazon uses the same #feature-bullets markup. Reproduced on 2 loads. They sit at the same level as h2 'About this item', 'Product details' and 'Reviews'. The short segment is 886 characters against 1012 for the original bullets. amazon.ts:350-355 sends the bullets as an article on purpose. That conflicts with the stated goal of no Short version on pages with controls. markdown.tsx clamps headings to h2 or deeper, with no nesting under the section's heading level.
- **fix**: Show the feature bullets in full (they are already a list) instead of condensing them. At least, render model headings one level below the section heading (h3 under the 'About this item' h2).
