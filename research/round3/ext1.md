## F2 [high] BBC articles get no Short version, a split byline and a Google promo when the window is 760 px wide or less (zoomed in or on a phone)
- **where**: https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c39wz740e0nyo and https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cxyvrged14d0o at a CSS viewport of 760 px or less
- **evidence**: Fresh load, Sift's article blocks counted every 0.5 s. At 400, 600 and 760 px: articles=0, short=false, and the line under the H1 is '9 minutes ago' / '14 hours ago'. At 900 and 1024 px: articles=1, byline 'Dan Sales · 24 Oct 2026, 12:59', 'Shortening…' and then the Short version within 3 s. Outline at 600 px: 'p: 12 minutes ago', '[link] Add as preferred on Google', 'p: Dan Sales', then the full text as loose paragraphs with no Short version. Screenshot research/shots/r3t2-bbc-narrow.png (400 px): 'Add as preferred on Google' and 'James Waterhouse Paris Correspondent, Limoges…' sit as body lines. At 400 px La Nación, Infobae and the Daily Mail article still get articles=1 and a Short version, so this is BBC-specific. A 1280 px window at 175% zoom or more is in this range, so low-vision users who zoom never get the Short version on BBC.
- **repro**: Set the viewport to 600x800 (or zoom a 1280 px window to 200%). Open https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c39wz740e0nyo and wait 10 s. There is no 'Short version' and no author in the byline, and the Google promo shows. Repeat at 1024 px: the Short version appears.
- **suspectedCause**: BBC's responsive layout at narrow widths moves the byline and timestamp blocks, so the generic extractor's article detection (src/content/extract/generic.ts) no longer wraps the body in an article block. With no article block, processAi in src/content/ai.ts never condenses it.
- **verdict**: Reproduced on two articles. With narrow.ts (setViewport, then a count of Sift <article> blocks every 1.5 s), c39wz740e0nyo at 600 px gave articles=0 and short=false for 14 s. The outline showed 'p: 38 minutes ago', '[link] Add as preferred on Google', 'p: Dan Sales', then loose paragraphs. At 1024 px it gave articles=1 and a Short version by 3 s, with byline 'Dan Sales · 24 Oct 2026, 12:59'. cxyvrged14d0o gave articles=0 at both 600 and 760 px, and articles=1 plus a Short version at 900 px. The DOM changes at narrow width: each body <p> sits directly in its own GridItem with no LayoutBlock wrapper. So findArticle (generic.ts ~1536) gets different parent scores and returns no article. A 1280 px window zoomed to 175% or more falls in this range.
- **fix**: Make findArticle robust to BBC's narrow grid. When many sibling GridItems each hold one paragraph, score the grid that contains them as the article. Or fall back to the enclosing <article> when it holds most of the page's prose. Add a regression check at 600 px.
