## F1403 (medium): Guardian articles lose the lead photo's caption

Found by: T2-news. Where: https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/oct/30/pope-leo-votes-us-midterms-first-political-ballot-pontiff and the Dolomites article, in both Short version and full text

Evidence: In the original, the lead figure shows a caption under the photo. For the Dolomites article it is a visible figcaption at 250,1204, 620x44: 'Soldiers from Italy’s Julia Alpine brigade work to make a first world war bomb safe before removing it. Photograph: Julia Alpine brigade'. For the Pope article it is 'Leo believes it is ‘the right and the duty of citizens’ to vote, the Vatican said. Photograph: Maria Grazia Picciarella/Sopa Images/Shutterstock'. Sift's first figure has the image with its alt text and no figcaption, in Short version and in full text. Later inline photos keep their captions ('Soldiers from Italian army’s Julia Alpine brigade at work…'). The lead figure holds two figcaptions: a hidden one (its parent div is display:none) and the visible one. Round 12's outline (r12t2-guardian-art.txt) also had no lead caption, so this is not new. Shot: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-2/research/shots/r13t2-guardian-pope.png

Repro: cd /home/nico/projects/sift-test-2; SIFT_PORT=9462 SIFT_TAB=T2-news bun scripts/act.ts goto:https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/oct/27/melting-glaciers-dolomites-first-world-war-munitions-italy wait:12000 'eval:[...document.querySelectorAll("figure")].slice(0,1).map(f=>[...f.querySelectorAll("figcaption")].map(c=>c.getBoundingClientRect().height+" "+c.innerText.slice(0,60)))' 'eval:document.querySelector("sift-root").shadowRoot.querySelector("figure figcaption")?.innerText'  → the original has a visible caption (the zero height is the hidden copy); Sift's first figcaption is the second photo's caption.

Suspected cause: Sift likely reads only the first figcaption of the lead figure, which is the hidden copy, and drops it as invisible.

Skeptic: Reproduced on both Guardian articles. Dolomites: the original's first figure holds two figcaptions, one 0 px high (hidden) and one 43.8 px high and visible ('Soldiers from Italy’s Julia Alpine brigade work to make a first world war bomb safe…'). Sift's first figure shows the image and alt text with no caption, both in the Short version and after 'Show full text'. The caption text is nowhere in Sift's shadow root. Later photos keep their captions. Pope Leo: same pattern. The original has a hidden and a visible caption ('Leo believes it is ‘the right and the duty of citizens’ to vote…'). Sift shows only the image with alt 'Pope Leo' and no caption, so the caption's content is lost. In code, generic.ts line 6372 takes `el.querySelector("figcaption")`, the first one, which is the hidden copy. captionText/textIn then return '' for it. siblingCaption does not find the visible one, so the caption is dropped.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/content/extract/generic.ts, in the figure branch near line 6372 (`let cap = … el.querySelector("figcaption")`), pick the first figcaption that is visible and has text. For example: `Array.from(el.querySelectorAll("figcaption")).find((c) => visible(c) && captionText(c)) ?? el.querySelector("figcaption")`. Apply the same change to the player branch near line 6365.

## F1404 (medium): USA Today home: 14 headlines stay cut off with '…' though the full headline is on the link

Found by: T2-news. Where: https://www.usatoday.com/ (Discover lists: Graphics, News in Pictures, Trending Video, Sports and others)

Evidence: 16 Sift links end in '…'. For 14 of them the original link's title attribute holds the full headline. Sift shows 'Walter Koenig, who played Chekov on original Star Trek…', while the title is '…, has died at 90'. Sift shows 'How did high-speed passenger trains collide? Nearly 70…', while the title is '…Nearly 70 dead in Spain crash'. Sift shows 'Teller explains why Houdini fascinates us 100 years after…', while the title is '…after his death'. The key fact, often the news itself, is lost. The original also clips the text visually but shows the full title on hover.

Repro: cd /home/nico/projects/sift-test-2; SIFT_PORT=9462 SIFT_TAB=T2-news bun scripts/act.ts goto:https://www.usatoday.com/ wait:12000 'eval:(()=>{const R=document.querySelector("sift-root").shadowRoot;const s=[...R.querySelectorAll("main a")].filter(a=>/…$/.test(a.innerText.trim()));return s.map(a=>a.innerText.trim()+" => "+([...document.querySelectorAll("a")].find(x=>x.href===a.href&&x.title)?.title||""))})()'

Suspected cause: The link text is taken from the clipped visible text. The title attribute is not used when the text ends in an ellipsis.

Skeptic: Reproduced on https://www.usatoday.com/. 16 Sift links end in '…', and 14 of them have the full headline in the original link's title attribute. Examples: 'Walter Koenig, who played Chekov on original Star Trek…' (title ends ', has died at 90') and 'How did high-speed passenger trains collide? Nearly 70…' (title '…Nearly 70 dead in Spain crash'). The site writes the cut-off text into the DOM, but its links keep the full title, which shows on hover. Sift's link has no title or aria-label (outerHTML is just href and data-ref). So Sift loses the full headline the original still offers, and often that cut-off part is the news itself.

Skeptic's fix lead: In src/content/extract/generic.ts, in toItem (around lines 3274 to 3276, where `title = textIn(main) || accName(main)`) and in the plain link-list path: when the visible text ends in '…' or '...' and the anchor's `title` attribute starts with that text minus the ellipsis and is longer, use the title attribute.

## F1409 (high): Craigslist: on a 3-photo listing, 'Image 3' shows photo 1 in Sift while the original shows photo 3

Found by: T4-generic. Where: Craigslist listing page, image buttons under the photo (src/content/extract/generic.ts figureImage, the 'framed' slide test near line 6333)

Evidence: Listing https://www.craigslist.org/view/d/novato-fuji-adventurer-mountain-bike/9BHB9U3Ac3WQ3XfVJsKrpw has 3 photos (#thumbs 3, Sift shows Image 1-3). After clicking 'Image 3' (3 tries, waits of 3-5 s), Sift's main img src is 00t0t… (photo 1) and its alt ends in '… Needs Repair 1'. 'Image 3' still gets aria-pressed=true. The original's .swipe box is at left 159, width 600. Its slides sit at 759 (photo 1), -441 (photo 2) and 159 (photo 3), so the original shows photo 3. Image 1 and Image 2 show the right photo (Image 2: src 00N0N…, slide 2 at 159). Shot: /home/nico/projects/sift-test-4/research/shots/r14t4-cl-img3.png ('Image 3' pressed, photo 1 shown). On a 5-photo listing (/view/d/los-gatos-2005-specialized-rockhopper/ao92DvgK9PFs4S6XFgbZuL), Image 3, 4, 5 and 1 all matched the original. Round 12 checked a 10-photo listing, so I cannot call this a regression.

Repro: SIFT_PORT=<port> SIFT_TAB=<tab> bun scripts/act.ts goto:<any Craigslist listing with exactly 3 photos> wait:8000 'click:Image 3' wait:3000 "eval:[document.querySelector('sift-root').shadowRoot.querySelector('main img').src.split('/').pop(), [...document.querySelectorAll('.slide')].map(s=>Math.round(s.getBoundingClientRect().left)+':'+s.querySelector('img').src.split('/').pop())]" prints Sift's img as photo 1, and the slide at the .swipe left edge (159) is photo 3.

Suspected cause: figureImage keeps the slides that intersect the figure element's box and picks the first one. In this 3-slide layout, photo 1 waits as the 'next' slide at left 759, the right edge of the 600 px .swipe box. It still counts as framed (the figure's box is wider than .swipe, or the edge test includes it) and comes first in DOM order. Testing against the clipping slider box (.swipe, overflow hidden), or preferring the slide whose left equals the box's left, would pick photo 3.

Skeptic: Confirmed, high. I reproduced it on 2 loads of the same 3-photo listing. After 'Image 3', Sift shows 00t0t (photo 1, alt '... Needs Repair 1'), and 'Image 3' has aria-pressed=true. The original's slides sit at 759 (00t0t), -441 (00N0N) and 159 (00c0c). With Sift switched off, elementFromPoint in the .swipe box returns 00c0c, and the original reads 'image 3 of 3' (shots: research/shots/skeptic-cl-sift.png and skeptic-cl-orig.png). Image 2 is correct. The cause is exact. figure.iw spans 159..761, but .swipe (overflow hidden) spans 159..759. Slide 1, at 759..1359, passes the framed test because 759 < fr.right - 1 = 760. It then ties slide 3 on width (600) and wins on DOM order, since the width loop only takes a wider image (w > bestW). main's figureImage (generic.ts:6329) is identical, so main has the bug too.

Skeptic's fix lead: src/content/extract/generic.ts figureImage (about line 6333-6349). Rank framed images by how much of their area is visible inside the figure's box (or inside the nearest clipping ancestor with overflow hidden, here .swipe), not by width with DOM order breaking ties. Or count an image as framed only if at least half its width is inside the box. A 2 px sliver of the next slide must not win.
